# Information Technology (IT)

Welcome to **Schwork Information Technology** — complete, thorough study documentation for Jamaican high school students (Grades 10–11), aligned closely with the revised high school IT curriculum.

Every lesson puts **deep, textbook-quality study notes**, clear comparison tables, **worked exam problems** (spread across spreadsheets, databases, IPO charts, pseudocode, trace tables, and programming), and **hover/tap formula hints** front and centre — with optional quizzes and classification drills neatly tucked inside a collapsed **Extra Practice** drawer at the bottom of each page.

---

## How the IT Exam & SBA Are Structured

| Component | Format & Duration | Weight | What It Tests |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Paper 1** | 60 Multiple-Choice Questions *(1 hr 15 min)* | **30%** | Rapid recall & application across all 8 sections: hardware, storage units, networks, cybersecurity, spreadsheets, databases, logic & algorithms. |
| **Paper 2** | 4 Compulsory Structured Questions *(90 marks, 2 hrs)* | **45%** | Applied scenarios across Theory (Sec 1–3), Productivity Tools (Sec 4–6), and Problem-Solving & Programming (Sec 7–8 — language-neutral pseudocode/flowcharts & trace tables!). |
| **Paper 3/1 (SBA)** *(or Paper 3/2 Private, 2 hrs)* | Integrated Practical Project *(50 marks)* | **25%** | **Word-Processing (10)** + **Web Page Design (5)** + **Spreadsheets (10)** + **Database Management (10)** + **Problem-Solving & Programming (15)**. |

## How to Get a Grade I in IT

1. **Master the Paper 2 "Big Three" Skills:** Spreadsheet functions (`IF`, `VLOOKUP`, `COUNTIF`, `$` absolute references), Database QBE queries & keys, and Pseudocode + Trace Tables make up well over half of Paper 2!
2. **Learn the Concepts, Not Just One App:** Whether your school uses Microsoft Office, LibreOffice, or Google Workspace — or codes in Pascal, Python, C, or VBA — exams test transferable logic and standard syntax!
3. **Treat Your SBA as One Connected Project:** Clean data in your Spreadsheet feeds directly into your Database, Web Page, Mail Merge letters, and Problem-Solving algorithm!
