## 1. Effect of Automation on Job Security (Skilled vs Unskilled!)

**Automation** means using computers, robots, self-service kiosks, or software algorithms to perform tasks with minimal human intervention (e.g. bank ATMs, supermarket self-checkout lanes, automated car assembly robots, AI chatbots).

### Balanced Exam Assessment: Job Loss vs Productivity Gains
Examiners want a **two-sided, balanced argument** — NEVER just say *"computers steal all jobs!"*

| Impact Dimension | **Unskilled / Repetitive Jobs** *(cashiers, bank tellers, factory packers, typists, toll collectors)* | **Skilled / Technical & Creative Jobs** *(IT engineers, cybersecurity analysts, doctors, data scientists, robot technicians)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Job Displacement / Risks** | **Highest risk of job loss (redundancy)** because routine, predictable tasks are easiest and cheapest to automate 24/7! | Lower risk of total replacement — **BUT** workers face **deskilling or obsolescence** if they fail to **retrain and upskill** constantly! |
| **Productivity & New Opportunities** | Automated kiosks cut queues, reduce human errors, operate 24 hours without fatigue, and lower business costs! | **Creates brand-new high-paying careers** building, programming, maintaining, securing, and managing automated systems — freeing experts to focus on higher-value human decisions! |

## 2. Roles of Computer-Related Professions (Syllabus Objective 3.5!)

Paper 1 loves giving you a job description and asking you to pick the **exact ICT job title** — learn how similar-sounding titles differ!

| ICT Career Title | Core Job Role & Plain-English Distinction |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Computer Systems Analyst** | Studies how an organisation currently works, interviews users, identifies problems, and **designs the blueprint/specifications** for a new or upgraded computer system! *(The "architect" — sits between business managers and programmers!)* |
| **2. Computer Programmer** | **Writes, tests, debugs, and maintains source code** in programming languages (Python, Java, C#, Pascal) following the specifications designed by analysts! |
| **3. Software Developer** | Broader role spanning the **entire software lifecycle** — researching user needs, designing architecture, coding, testing, releasing, and updating complete applications! |
| **4. Web Developer** | Designs, builds, tests, and maintains **websites and web applications** (front-end visual layout + back-end servers/databases, e-commerce carts, security)! |
| **5. Network Engineer** | **Designs, plans, installs, and optimises network infrastructure** (routers, switches, fibre cabling, Wi-Fi coverage, bandwidth, enterprise security architecture)! |
| **6. Network Administrator** | Handles **day-to-day running and maintenance** of an existing network — creating user accounts, assigning permissions, monitoring uptime, and fixing connectivity issues! |
| **7. Systems Administrator** | Installs, configures, patches, backs up, and keeps the organisation's **servers, operating systems, and enterprise hardware** running reliably 24/7! |
| **8. Database Administrator (DBA)** | Designs, secures, backs up, optimises, and controls access to the organisation's **databases** — ensuring data integrity, speed, and zero unauthorised leaks! |
| **9. Computer Support Specialist** *(Help Desk / IT Technician)* | Front-line problem solver assisting everyday employees/customers with **hardware faults, software crashes, password resets, printer issues, and setup**! |
| **10. Social Media Specialist** | Plans, creates, schedules, analyses, and manages an organisation's **brand presence, marketing campaigns, and customer engagement** across Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube, and Facebook! |

## 3. Impact of ICT on Five Major Fields (Syllabus Objective 3.6!)

Memorise **2 clear positives + 1 realistic challenge/drawback** for each of these **FIVE official fields** — instant full marks on Paper 2 essay/scenario questions!

| Sector | Key Positive Impacts (Exam Terms!) | Key Challenges / Negative Risks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Education** | • **Instant access to vast online information** & free study platforms.<br/>• **Distance teaching & reach:** rural/overseas students take UWI/online classes via Zoom & LMS.<br/>• **Collaborative teaching & learning** (shared Google Docs, virtual labs) and **online tutoring**! | • **Plagiarism** (copy-pasting internet/AI work without citing!).<br/>• **Digital divide** (unequal laptops/Wi-Fi access), screen distractions & cyberbullying! |
| **2. Medicine (Healthcare)** | • **Telemedicine & Teleradiology:** rural clinics in Jamaica send X-rays/MRIs digitally for instant diagnosis by specialists in Kingston or overseas!<br/>• **eHealth:** online appointment booking, digital patient records (EHR) accessible quickly in emergencies.<br/>• **Better information access** for doctors and patients! | • **Increase in dangerous self-diagnosis** (patients misdiagnosing serious illnesses from random web searches!).<br/>• **Privacy/security risks** if sensitive medical databases are breached or hit by ransomware! |
| **3. Business** | • **E-commerce:** selling 24/7 globally with lower physical rent costs.<br/>• **Electronic Point of Sale (EPOS):** barcode scanning automatically prints receipts AND updates inventory counts in real time!<br/>• **Telecommuting (Remote Work) & Email:** staff work from home saving commute time/office space while communicating globally in seconds! | • High initial setup/cybersecurity costs.<br/>• Loss of personal face-to-face customer warmth; small traditional shops struggle against online giants; blur between work and home hours! |
| **4. Law Enforcement** | • **E-surveillance:** CCTV cameras (e.g. JamaicaEye), licence-plate scanners, and digital forensics help solve crimes and deter criminals.<br/>• **Biometrics & Digital Fingerprinting (AFIS):** matching crime-scene fingerprints, DNA databases, and passport facial scans in seconds! | • **Privacy concerns** ("Big Brother" over-surveillance of innocent citizens).<br/>• Rise of sophisticated cross-border **cybercrime** requiring costly specialist police training! |
| **5. Recreation (Entertainment)** | • **Digital Music & Streaming** (Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music): Caribbean artists reach global fans instantly without physical CDs!<br/>• **Gaming & Esports:** immersive 3D multiplayer games, VR experiences, and global online communities! | • **Music & game piracy** hurting creators' income.<br/>• **Sedentary lifestyles**, eye strain, gaming addiction, and reduced outdoor physical activity! |

## 4. Worked Example: ICT Careers & Telemedicine Scenario (Paper 2!)

<WorkedExample
  title="Choosing ICT Specialists & Assessing ICT in Medicine"
  marks={8}
  problem={
    <>
      A rural parish hospital in St Elizabeth installs a new digital patient management system connected to Kingston Public Hospital.
      <br />
      (a) State which <strong>ICT professional</strong> is responsible for each job:
      <br />
      &nbsp;&nbsp;(i) Interviewing doctors and nurses to study current paper workflows and design the blueprint for the new digital system.
      <br />
      &nbsp;&nbsp;(ii) Managing backups, table security, and user permissions for all patient medical records.
      <br />
      &nbsp;&nbsp;(iii) Helping nurses immediately when a ward desktop freezes or a label printer jams.
      <br />
      (b) Explain <strong>ONE benefit</strong> of <strong>teleradiology / telemedicine</strong> for patients in rural St Elizabeth, and <strong>ONE risk</strong> created by patients searching symptoms online.
    </>
  }
  examTip="Notice the crisp distinctions between IT jobs: studying workflows & designing blueprints = Systems Analyst; securing/backing up records tables = Database Administrator (DBA); fixing ward PCs/printers for staff = Computer Support Specialist!"
>

### (a) Matching ICT Professionals
- **(i)** **Computer Systems Analyst**
- **(ii)** **Database Administrator (DBA)**
- **(iii)** **Computer Support Specialist** *(IT Support Technician)*

### (b) Impact on Medicine
- **Benefit of Telemedicine / Teleradiology:** Rural patients receive fast, expert X-ray/scan interpretations from specialist radiologists in Kingston (or abroad) **without travelling hours across parishes**, speeding up life-saving treatment and cutting travel costs!
- **Risk of Online Symptom Searches:** **Dangerous self-diagnosis** — patients may misinterpret unreliable websites, panic unnecessarily, or self-medicate incorrectly instead of seeing a qualified doctor in time!

</WorkedExample>
