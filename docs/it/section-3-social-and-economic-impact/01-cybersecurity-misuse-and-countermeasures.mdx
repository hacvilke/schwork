## 1. Computer Security, Cybersecurity & The Four Risk Elements

- **Computer Security:** Protecting computer hardware, software, and stored data from physical damage, theft, unauthorised access, or accidental loss (whether online or offline!).
- **Cybersecurity:** The specialised branch of computer security focused on protecting **internet-connected networks, devices, cloud accounts, and digital data** from **cyberattacks and digital misuse** — assessing and minimising risk!

### The Four Elements of Security Risk (Know Every Definition!)
1. **Vulnerability:** A **weakness or flaw** in a system, software, hardware, or human habit that could be exploited (e.g. an unpatched Windows laptop, a weak password like `123456`, or an unlocked server room door!).
2. **Threat:** Any **potential danger or source of harm** capable of exploiting a vulnerability (e.g. hackers, ransomware, disgruntled insiders, hurricanes/floods, power surges).
3. **Attack:** An **actual deliberate action** taken to exploit a vulnerability and breach security (e.g. sending a fake bank phishing link or launching a DDoS flood!).
4. **Countermeasure (Control):** A **safeguard or protective action** (physical, software, or personal) installed to **prevent, detect, or reduce** the impact of an attack (e.g. encryption, firewalls, backups, 2FA)!

## 2. All 14 Forms of Computer Misuse & Who Gets Hurt! (Syllabus Obj 3.2!)

Examiners test BOTH **identifying the exact misuse** AND **identifying the primary entity impacted** (**Individual**, **Organisation**, or **Government** — note many threats hit *more than one*!):

| Computer Misuse / Threat | Plain-English Explanation | Primary Entity Impacted & Real Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Phishing Attacks** | Fake emails, SMS ("smishing"), or spoofed websites pretending to be a trusted bank (e.g. fake NCB/Scotiabank alert) to trick victims into typing passwords or card PINs! | **Individual & Organisation** — stolen bank funds, compromised corporate accounts! |
| **2. Identity Theft** | Stealing a person's private credentials (TRN, passport, DOB, card numbers) to impersonate them — taking out loans or shopping in their name! | **Individual** — ruined credit rating, financial loss, legal nightmares! |
| **3. Cyberbullying** | Using social media, messaging apps, or anonymous accounts to harass, threaten, humiliate, or spread rumours about someone online. | **Individual** *(especially youth/students)* — severe emotional distress, anxiety, school disruption. |
| **4. Viruses & Malware** | Malicious software designed to damage, spy on, or hijack computers — including **Viruses** (attach to host files), **Worms** (self-replicate across networks), **Trojans** (disguised as useful apps), **Spyware/Keyloggers**, and **Ransomware** (locks files and demands ransom!). | **Individual, Organisation & Government** — wiped files, frozen hospital/bank operations, ransom extortion! |
| **5. Denial of Service (DoS / DDoS) Attacks** | Flooding a server or website with millions of fake traffic requests simultaneously until it crashes and legitimate users are locked out! | **Organisation & Government** — e-commerce store offline (lost sales!), government tax/exam portal unreachable! |
| **6. Data Theft (Data Breach)** | Unauthorised copying or exfiltration of confidential records (customer credit cards, medical files, exam papers). | **Organisation, Government & Individuals** — massive fines, loss of public trust, identity fraud! |
| **7. Financial Abuses (Online Fraud)** | Unauthorised electronic transfers, card skimming, fake online stores, or manipulating payroll/accounting records digitally. | **Individual & Organisation** — direct theft of money and business bankruptcy! |
| **8. Copyright Infringement** | Copying, distributing, or publishing someone else's protected creative work (books, photos, articles, videos) without permission or licence. | **Individual creators & Organisations** — lost royalties and intellectual property rights! |
| **9. Software & Music Piracy** | Illegally downloading, cracking, burning, or sharing copyrighted software (e.g. pirated MS Office) or music/movies without paying! | **Organisations (software/record companies) & Artists** — lost revenue, plus pirated files often hide Trojans/malware! |
| **10. Violation of Privacy** | Collecting, leaking, selling, or viewing someone's private personal data, photos, or location without consent. | **Individual** — exposure of sensitive personal/medical/family details! |
| **11. Online Publication of Obscene Materials** | Uploading, sharing, or distributing explicit, indecent, or illegal content online (including non-consensual sharing). | **Individual & Society** — harm to minors, reputational damage, criminal prosecution under Jamaica's Cybercrimes Act! |
| **12. Propaganda** | Deliberately spreading biased, misleading, or fabricated information ("fake news", bot campaigns) online to manipulate public opinion or elections! | **Government & Public / Individuals** — social unrest, voter manipulation, distrust in public health or institutions! |
| **13. Electronic Eavesdropping** | Secretly intercepting private digital communications (unencrypted Wi-Fi traffic, calls, emails) as they travel across a network! | **Individual, Organisation & Government** — stolen passwords, private conversations, or state secrets! |
| **14. Industrial Espionage** | Competitors secretly hacking or stealing a company's **trade secrets, secret recipes, upcoming product designs, or pricing bids** to gain unfair commercial advantage! | **Organisation (and National Economy)** — destroys years of R&D investment and market edge! |

## 3. Countermeasures: Physical vs Software vs Personal Practices!

Paper 1 & 2 constantly ask you to classify countermeasures into **THREE exact categories** (check carefully: **Biometrics** can be classified under BOTH Physical access control [fingerprint door lock] AND Software authentication [Face ID phone unlock]!):

### A. Physical Countermeasures *(Tangible locks, hardware & environmental protections!)*
- **Backup & Off-Site Recovery Procedures:** Keeping regular copies of critical data on external drives/tapes stored in a **separate fireproof off-site location** (or encrypted cloud) so fires, floods, hurricanes, or ransomware can't wipe out the organisation!
- **Hardware Firewall:** A dedicated physical network appliance sitting between the school/company LAN and the Internet that blocks unauthorised external traffic!
- **Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS):** Monitors network/server activity continuously and raises instant alerts when suspicious break-in patterns occur!
- **Physical Biometrics & Access Controls:** Fingerprint/retina door scanners, keycard locks, CCTV cameras, security guards, surge protectors/UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply), fire extinguishers, and cable locks!

### B. Software Countermeasures *(Digital programs & security settings inside systems!)*
- **Strong Passwords & Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA / 2FA):** Long, unique combinations of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols + a second verification step (SMS code, authenticator app, or **software biometrics** like Face ID/Touch ID)!
- **Data Encryption:** Scrambling readable plaintext data into unreadable **ciphertext** using a mathematical key so even if a hacker steals the file or intercepts Wi-Fi traffic, they **cannot read it** without the decryption key!
- **Software Firewall:** Built into operating systems (e.g. Windows Defender Firewall) to filter incoming/outgoing app connections on a single device.
- **Antivirus & Anti-Malware Software:** Continuously scans downloads, USB drives, and email attachments to **detect, quarantine, and remove** viruses, spyware, and ransomware *(must keep definitions updated!)*.

### C. Personal Security Practices *(Smart Everyday Habits!)*
1. **Verify Email & Sender Authenticity:** Never blindly click urgent "Your bank account will be locked in 2 hours!" links — check the actual sender domain and contact the bank independently!
2. **Inspect Website URLs Before Logging In:** Confirm **`https://`** (padlock) AND exact spelling (`www.jncb.com`, NOT `www.jncb-login-verify.xyz`!).
3. **Avoid Sensitive Tasks on Open Public Wi-Fi:** Never do online banking or type passwords on unsecured café/airport Wi-Fi (or use mobile data / a trusted VPN) to prevent **electronic eavesdropping**!
4. **Secure Mobile Devices:** Enable PIN/biometric screen lock, auto-lock timer, remote wipe ("Find My Device"), and install apps ONLY from official stores (Google Play / Apple App Store)!
5. **Protect Yourself on Social Media:** Set profiles to **Private**, avoid oversharing your home address/school timetable/real-time vacation location, block & report cyberbullies, and never share intimate photos or passwords!

## 4. Worked Example: Matching Threats to Countermeasures (Paper 2!)

<WorkedExample
  title="Cybersecurity Audit for a Jamaican E-Commerce Company"
  marks={8}
  problem={
    <>
      <strong>YardStyle Boutique</strong> runs an online clothing store in Kingston. Recently:
      <br />
      (i) A hacker flooded its website with millions of fake visits, crashing the checkout page during a holiday sale.
      <br />
      (ii) Several customers received fake emails saying “Click here to claim a J$10,000 YardStyle voucher” and asking for their credit card CVV codes.
      <br />
      (iii) A store clerk accidentally downloaded a cracked photo-editing app that encrypted all store inventory files and demanded Bitcoin to unlock them.
      <br />
      (a) Name the exact computer misuse/threat in <strong>(i)</strong>, <strong>(ii)</strong>, and <strong>(iii)</strong>.
      <br />
      (b) Recommend <strong>ONE Physical</strong> and <strong>ONE Software</strong> countermeasure to help YardStyle recover from and prevent threat <strong>(iii)</strong>.
    </>
  }
  examTip="Always tie countermeasures SPECIFICALLY to the exact threat in the prompt! For ransomware (iii), 'Regular offline/off-site backups' lets the firm restore encrypted files WITHOUT paying ransom, while 'Updated Antivirus + blocking pirated software' prevents infection!"
>

### (a) Identifying Each Threat
- **(i) Flooding the site to crash checkout:** **Denial of Service (DoS / DDoS) Attack**
- **(ii) Fake voucher email stealing credit card CVVs:** **Phishing Attack**
- **(iii) Cracked app encrypting files for Bitcoin:** **Ransomware (Malware) via Software Piracy / Trojan**

### (b) Targeted Countermeasures for Threat (iii)
- **Physical Countermeasure:** **Regular Off-Site / Offline Backup & Recovery Procedures** — store clean encrypted backups disconnected from the main network so YardStyle can wipe the infected PC and restore all inventory files cleanly without paying criminals!
- **Software Countermeasure:** **Updated Antivirus / Anti-Malware Software** *(plus strict user account permissions blocking staff from installing unauthorised pirated programs)* to detect and quarantine malicious files before execution!

</WorkedExample>
