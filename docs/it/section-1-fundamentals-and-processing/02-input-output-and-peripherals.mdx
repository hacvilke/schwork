## 1. Input Devices: Manual vs Automated (Direct Data-Entry)

**Input devices** feed data and commands into the computer system. Exams love testing whether you can pick the **fastest, least error-prone input device** for a specific job!

### A. Automated / Direct Data-Entry Input Devices *(Exam Favourites!)*

| Input Device | How It Works | Classic Exam & Jamaican Real-World Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **OMR** *(Optical Mark Reader)* | Shines light across a form to detect the **exact position of shaded pencil/ink bubbles** — does NOT read letters! | Marking **Paper 1 Multiple-Choice exam sheets**, national lottery tickets (Cash Pot / Lotto slips), school surveys & attendance sheets. |
| **OCR** *(Optical Character Reader)* | Scans printed or neatly handwritten text and **converts characters into editable digital text** inside a word processor! | Digitising old printed books/legal contracts, scanning passports at Norman Manley Airport, automatic licence-plate reading, utility turnaround bills. |
| **MICR** *(Magnetic Ink Character Recognition)* | Reads special numbers printed in **iron-oxide magnetic ink** along the bottom of bank cheques — works even if the cheque is stamped, crumpled, or scribbled over! | **High-speed bank cheque clearing & fraud prevention** (extremely hard to forge!). |
| **Barcode Reader / Scanner** | Uses a laser or camera to scan vertical black-and-white bars (product ID) and pulls the price live from the store database! | **Supermarket POS checkouts** (Hi-Lo, MegaMart, PriceSmart), library books, courier parcels, hospital wristbands. |
| **Biometric Systems** | Scans unique physical human traits (**fingerprint, iris/retina, face, voiceprint**). | Unlocking smartphones, airport immigration e-gates, high-security bank vaults, staff clock-in time attendance (stops "buddy punching"!). |
| **Sensors** | Automatically measure physical environmental quantities (**temperature, pressure, light, motion, humidity, pH**) continuously without human typing! | Smart greenhouse irrigation, hospital ICU patient heart/oxygen monitors, car airbags/parking sensors, automated street lights & burglar alarms. |
| **Document Scanner** | Captures an exact digital **image** (JPEG/PDF) of a physical photograph, ID card, or signed paper form. | Archiving birth certificates, TRN cards, signed invoices, and artwork. |

> **#1 Paper 1 Trap — OMR vs OCR vs MICR:**
> - **Shaded bubbles** on MCQ exams = **OMR** *(Mark!)*
> - **Printed letters converted to editable Word text** = **OCR** *(Character!)*
> - **Bank cheques with magnetic ink** = **MICR** *(Magnetic!)*

### B. Everyday & Specialised Manual Input Devices

| Input Device | Key Function & Best Scenario |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Keyboard** | Primary text/number entry for typing documents, emails, and writing program code *(specialised ergonomic or Braille keyboards assist users with physical/visual needs)*. |
| **Pointing Devices (Mouse, Trackpad, Trackball, Stylus)** | Moving an on-screen cursor to select icons, drag items, and draw cleanly inside a GUI. |
| **Touch Screen / Touch Terminals** | Combines **Input AND Output** in one glass panel — ideal for **smartphones, tablets, bank ATMs, fast-food self-order kiosks, and retail POS terminals**! |
| **Joystick / Game Controller** | Multi-directional movement control for **flight simulators, video games, wheelchairs, and industrial cranes**. |
| **Light-Pen** | Light-sensitive wand touched directly to a screen for drawing/selecting menu points *(older CAD & medical display systems; largely replaced by modern touch styluses)*. |
| **Digital Camera & Webcam** | Capturing still photos/video directly into digital memory (journalism, Zoom/Google Meet online classes, video conferencing). |
| **Sound Capture (Microphone) & Voice Recognition / Voice Response** | Capturing spoken audio for calls, podcasts, hands-free dictation, and automated telephone banking menus ("Press or say 1 for Account Balance"). |
| **Remote Control** | Infrared or Bluetooth handheld keypad controlling TVs, classroom projectors, air conditioners, and drones from a distance. |

## 2. Output Devices: Visual & Audible Output

**Output** comes in two forms:
- **Soft Copy:** Temporary, electronic output viewed on a screen or heard as audio (e.g. Monitor display, Speakers).
- **Hard Copy:** Permanent, tangible physical output you can hold in your hand (e.g. Printed paper, 3D-printed object, Microfilm).

### A. Monitors & Microfilm
- **Monitors (LCD / LED / OLED Display Screens):** Primary visual **soft-copy** output device — displays text, graphics, and video in pixels (sharpness depends on **resolution**, e.g. `1920 × 1080 Full HD` or `4K`).
- **Microfilm / Microfiche:** Photographic film strips/sheets storing **microscopically shrunk images of thousands of documents** (read with a special magnifying reader) — used by **national archives, newspapers, libraries, and registries** for ultra-compact, long-life archival storage!

### B. All Six Printer Types (Know Impact vs Non-Impact!)

Paper 1 and Paper 2 LOVE asking which printer fits a specific workplace — especially **Impact vs Non-Impact**:
- **Impact Printers** physically strike an inked ribbon against paper (noisy, slower, **CAN print multi-part carbon copies!**).
- **Non-Impact Printers** form images without touching paper with a hammer (quiet, fast, sharp — **CANNOT print carbon copies!**).

| Printer Type | Impact or Non-Impact? | How It Works | Best Exam Scenario & Key Traits |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Laser Printer** | Non-Impact | Uses a laser beam, charged drum, and dry **toner powder** fused with heat! | **High-volume offices & schools** — fastest text speed, razor-sharp quality, lowest cost *per page* for heavy printing. |
| **2. Inkjet Printer** | Non-Impact | Sprays microscopic droplets of **liquid ink** through tiny nozzles onto paper. | **Home users & vibrant colour photo printing** — cheap to buy upfront, quiet, rich colour, but ink cartridges are pricey per page and can smear if wet! |
| **3. Dot-Matrix Printer** | **Impact** | Tiny metal pins hammer an inked ribbon against paper to form characters out of dots. | **Warehouses, auto parts shops, payroll/delivery receipts needing multi-part carbon copies!** Noisy & low print quality, but ultra-durable & cheap ribbon! |
| **4. Thermal Printer** | Non-Impact | Applies **heat directly onto special heat-sensitive paper** (no ink or toner needed!). | **Supermarket POS receipts, ATM slips, bus/parking tickets, courier shipping labels** — fast, silent, compact, though print fades over time! |
| **5. Plotter** | Non-Impact *(wide-format pen/inkjet)* | Draws ultra-precise continuous vector lines on giant rolls of paper (A1/A0 size). | **Architects, civil engineers & surveyors** printing building blueprints, technical CAD drawings, highway maps, and giant banners! |
| **6. 3D Printer** | Non-Impact *(Additive)* | Builds solid **3-dimensional physical objects** layer by layer from melted plastic resin or metal! | **Medical prosthetics & dental models, engineering prototypes, custom spare parts, architectural scale models!** |

### C. Audible Output Devices
- **Speakers:** Broadcast sound aloud to a group (classroom presentations, multimedia movies, alarms, announcements).
- **Headphones & Earphones:** Private personal audio output without disturbing others (computer labs, language listening exams, video calls, music on the bus!).

## 3. Worked Example: Selecting & Justifying Peripherals for a Jamaican Hospital

<WorkedExample
  title="Choosing the Right Input & Output Devices (Paper 2 Applied Question)"
  marks={8}
  problem={
    <>
      A busy Jamaican hospital is upgrading its IT systems. Recommend the <strong>MOST suitable input or output device</strong> for each task below and give <strong>ONE</strong> clear reason for your choice:
      <br />
      (a) Automatically monitoring a critically ill patient’s heart rate and blood oxygen level 24 hours a day.
      <br />
      (b) Printing 3-copy carbon-paper pharmacy delivery notes for the storeroom, ward nurse, and accounts office.
      <br />
      (c) Printing large A1 architectural floor plans for the hospital’s new maternity wing.
      <br />
      (d) Scanning patient wristbands quickly to pull up accurate medication records at the bedside.
    </>
  }
  examTip="Whenever Paper 2 asks for a peripheral AND a justification, match the unique technical strength of that device directly to the clue in the scenario (e.g. '3-copy carbon paper' → ONLY an impact Dot-Matrix printer can strike through carbon layers!)."
>

| Task | Recommended Device | Clear Exam Justification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **(a) 24-hour patient vitals** | **Sensors** *(Input)* | Continuously measure physical readings (pulse, oxygen) automatically in real time with zero human typing errors and instant alarm triggers. |
| **(b) 3-copy carbon delivery notes** | **Dot-Matrix Printer** *(Output)* | It is an **impact printer** whose pins strike through multi-part carbon/NCR paper to print all three copies simultaneously. |
| **(c) Large A1 building blueprints** | **Plotter** *(Output)* | Handles extra-wide paper rolls (A1/A0) and draws ultra-accurate continuous lines required for architectural CAD blueprints. |
| **(d) Scanning patient wristbands** | **Barcode Reader** *(Input)* | Captures each patient's unique ID instantaneously with near-zero data-entry errors, preventing dangerous medication mix-ups! |

</WorkedExample>
