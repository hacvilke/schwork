# Welcome to Schwork

**Schwork** is a free study platform structured like clear technical documentation. Every subject is organized cleanly section by section — easy to read and search — so students can revise comfortably on a phone, laptop, or school computer.

## How to Use Schwork

1. **Follow the Structured Sections**
   Each subject is broken down topic by topic so you always know what to study next.
2. **Track Your Learning Objectives**
   Every lesson starts with a **Learning Objectives** checklist and progress bar. Tap each item as you master it — your progress stays saved locally on your device.
3. **Explore Interactive Graphs & Diagrams**
   - Drag sliders on interactive graphs to see how equations change in real time.
   - Tap any point on a graph to read its $(x, y)$ coordinates.
   - Tap **Expand** on any diagram to view it full-screen on a phone.
4. **Practice With Instant Feedback**
   Work through the **Worked Examples** (hide steps first to try on paper) and check your understanding with the **Quick Checks** and quizzes built into each lesson.

## Designed for Comfort on Any Screen

- **Soft warm off-white backgrounds and dark charcoal text** reduce eye strain during long study sessions.
- **Wide equations and tables** swipe smoothly sideways — nothing gets cut off on small screens.
- **Print-ready notes:** Use your browser’s **Print** option — navigation bars hide automatically so only the clean lesson prints.
- **Switch Dark / Light Mode** anytime using the icon in the top bar for comfortable night study.

Ready to see a full topic in action? Open the **[Sample Lesson](/docs/sample-lesson)** or browse the **[Subjects](/subjects)** directory.
