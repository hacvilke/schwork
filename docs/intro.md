# Welcome to Schwork

**Schwork** is a free study site built for Jamaican high school students (Grades 10–11). Every subject is organized clearly section by section — like an easy-to-search reference guide — so you can revise faster on a phone, laptop, or library computer.

## How to Use Schwork

1. **Follow the Syllabus Sections**
   Each subject is broken down topic by topic so you never have to guess what to study next.
2. **Tick Off Objectives as You Learn**
   Every lesson starts with a **Learning Objectives** checklist. Tap each item when you understand it — your progress stays saved on your device.
3. **Play With the Graphs & Zoom Diagrams**
   - Drag sliders on interactive graphs to see how equations change in real time.
   - Tap any point on a graph to read its $(x, y)$ coordinates.
   - Tap **Expand** on any geometry, vector, or science diagram to view it full-screen on a phone.
4. **Test Yourself Before Moving On**
   Work through the **Worked Examples** (you can hide the steps first to try on paper) and check your understanding with the **Quick Checks** at the end of each topic.

## Studying on a Phone or Library PC?

- **Wide equations and accounting tables** swipe smoothly sideways — nothing gets cut off on small screens.
- **Need paper notes?** Use your browser’s **Print** option at school or the parish library — navigation bars hide automatically so only the clean lesson prints.
- **Switch Dark / Light Mode** anytime using the icon in the top bar for comfortable night study.

Ready to see a full topic in action? Open the **[Sample Lesson](/docs/sample-lesson)** or browse the **[Subjects](/subjects)** directory.
