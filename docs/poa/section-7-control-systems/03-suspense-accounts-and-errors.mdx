## 1. What Is a Suspense Account & When Do We Need One?

Suppose on 31 December your Trial Balance fails to agree — **Total Credits exceed Total Debits by J\$12,400** — yet management needs draft financial statements immediately while the accounting team hunts down the posting mistakes.

A **Suspense Account** is a **temporary holding account** opened on the **SHORTER side** of a disagreeing Trial Balance so both columns temporarily balance while errors are investigated:
- If **Credits > Debits** by J\$12,400 → open Suspense Account with a **DEBIT balance of J\$12,400** (`Balance b/d` on Debit).
- If **Debits > Credits** by J\$12,400 → open Suspense Account with a **CREDIT balance of J\$12,400** (`Balance b/d` on Credit).

As each one-sided error is discovered and corrected through the General Journal, entries post into the Suspense Account until its balance hits **ZERO (`J$0`)** — proving every Trial Balance disagreement has been found!
*(What if an error is STILL unfound on Balance Sheet day? A remaining **Debit** Suspense balance appears temporarily under **Current Assets**, and a remaining **Credit** Suspense balance appears temporarily under **Current Liabilities**!)*

## 2. Two Groups of Errors: Suspense Needed vs NO Suspense Needed

| **Group A: Do NOT Affect Trial Balance** *(NO Suspense Account!)* | **Group B: DO Affect Trial Balance** *(USE Suspense Account!)* |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1. **Error of Omission** *(left out of BOTH Dr and Cr)* | 1. **Single-sided omission** *(entered on Dr, forgot Cr — or vice versa)* |
| 2. **Error of Commission** *(wrong person's account, same class)* | 2. **Posted to the SAME side twice** *(both Dr & Dr, or both Cr & Cr → fix with **DOUBLE the amount**!)* |
| 3. **Error of Principle** *(wrong class — e.g. Repairs put in Motor Vehicles)* | 3. **Undercasting or Overcasting** a Day Book or account *(column added too low or too high)* |
| 4. **Error of Original Entry** *(wrong figure on BOTH Dr and Cr)* | 4. **Different amounts posted on Dr and Cr** *(e.g. Dr 1,500, Cr 5,100)* |
| 5. **Complete Reversal of Entries** *(Dr and Cr swapped on both sides → fix with **DOUBLE the amount**!)* | 5. **Extracting a ledger `Balance b/d` into the wrong Trial Balance column** *(or omitting a balance from the TB)* |
| 6. **Compensating Errors** *(two unrelated equal errors on opposite sides)* | |

## 3. The Foolproof 3-Step Method to Fix ANY Error!

Never guess a Journal correction! Run every single error through these **3 steps on rough paper**:
1. **SHOULD HAVE BEEN:** Write the exact Debit & Credit that *should* have been recorded.
2. **WAS ACTUALLY DONE:** Write what the clerk *actually* posted (including any blank missing half!).
3. **FIXING ENTRY (Journal):** Compare Step 2 to Step 1 — cancel anything wrong, add anything missing, and if only ONE real account needs fixing (a Group B error!), plug the other half straight into **`Suspense Account`**!

### Watch the "Double-Amount" Rule in Action!
Suppose Cash paid for **Office Equipment J\$2,200** was correctly credited in Cash, but **wrongly credited to Office Equipment**:
- *Should have been:* **Dr Office Equipment 2,200** | Cr Cash 2,200
- *Actually done:* Cr Office Equipment 2,200 | Cr Cash 2,200 *(Cash is fine! Only Office Equipment is wrong!)*
- *How to fix Office Equipment:* First **Dr 2,200** to erase the wrong credit (bringing it back to 0), THEN **Dr another 2,200** to put the true debit in → **Dr Office Equipment J\$4,400** / **Cr Suspense Account J\$4,400**!

---

## 4. Which Errors Change Net Profit & Which Change the Balance Sheet?

After fixing errors, examiners love asking for a **Statement of Revised (Corrected) Net Profit** (and sometimes the Balance Sheet impact!). Apply this simple rule:

- **Does the correcting entry touch a NOMINAL Account** *(Sales, Purchases, Returns, Carriage, Discount, Rent, Wages, Repairs, Bad Debts, Depreciation)*?
  - **Credit to a Nominal Account** *(increasing Revenue or reducing an Expense/Purchases)* → **ADDS TO (`+`) Net Profit!**
  - **Debit to a Nominal Account** *(increasing an Expense/Purchases or reducing Revenue)* → **SUBTRACTS FROM (`−`) Net Profit!**
- **Does the correcting entry touch ONLY Real/Personal Accounts & Suspense** *(e.g. Office Equipment, Cash, Debtors, Creditors, Drawings)*?
  - **ZERO effect on Net Profit!** *(It updates ONLY the Balance Sheet — e.g. fixing Office Equipment increases Fixed Assets on the Balance Sheet, and fixing a debtor updates Current Assets!)*
