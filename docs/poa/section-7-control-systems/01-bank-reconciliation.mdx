## 1. Why Are the Cash Book & Bank Statement Opposites? (The Mirror-Image Rule!)

Every month, your bank (e.g. NCB, Scotiabank, JMMB) sends you a **Bank Statement** — a copy of **your account as it looks in the bank's books**.

Here is the **#1 secret** every POA student MUST remember before ticking a single number:

| In YOUR **Cash Book** *(Bank Column)* | Mirrors... | On the **Bank Statement** *(Bank's Books)* | Why? |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **DEBIT (Dr) = Money IN** *(Deposits / Receipts)* | ↔ | **CREDIT (Cr) = Money IN** *(Lodgements / Deposits)* | To you, positive bank cash is an **Asset** (↑ Debit). To the bank, holding your cash is a **Liability** — the bank *owes you* that money (↑ Credit)! |
| **CREDIT (Cr) = Money OUT** *(Cheques Paid / Withdrawals)* | ↔ | **DEBIT (Dr) = Money OUT** *(Payments / Cheques Cleared)* | Paying out reduces your Asset (↓ Credit) and reduces what the bank owes you (↓ Debit)! |
| **Dr Balance** = Positive money in bank | ↔ | **Cr Balance** = Positive money in bank | Exact mirror image! |
| **Cr Balance** = **Bank Overdraft** *(you owe the bank!)* | ↔ | **Dr Balance** = **Bank Overdraft** *(O/D)* | Exact mirror image! |

:::tip Golden Cross-Ticking Rule ("Play Snap Across Opposites!")
When given BOTH a Cash Book and a Bank Statement:
1. Check every item on the **DEBIT side of the Cash Book** against the **CREDIT column of the Bank Statement** — put a tick (`✓`) next to every matching pair!
2. Check every item on the **CREDIT side of the Cash Book** against the **DEBIT column of the Bank Statement** — put a tick (`✓`) next to every matching pair!
3. Now look at whatever is **LEFT UNTICKED**:
   - **Unticked on the Bank Statement** → The bank knows about it, **you didn't!** → Put it in the **UPDATED CASH BOOK** *(on the OPPOSITE side!)*
   - **Unticked in the Cash Book** → You recorded it, **the bank hasn't processed it yet!** (*Timing Differences*) → Put it in the **BANK RECONCILIATION STATEMENT**!
:::

## 2. Updated Cash Book Blueprint: Which `Balance b/d` Starts It & What Gets Ticked Off (`✓` vs `✗`)

Students lose easy marks on Paper 2 by starting the Updated Cash Book with the wrong balance or dumping Unpresented Cheques into the Cash Book. Follow this exact **3-Rule Checklist**:

### Rule A — Which `Balance b/d` Starts the Updated Cash Book?
- **ALWAYS start with the UNADJUSTED CLOSING `Balance b/d`** from the end of the month in your **OLD Cash Book** *(e.g. `J\$42,000 Dr` at 31 October — NEVER the Bank Statement balance and NEVER the 1 October opening balance!)*
- Put it on the **DEBIT side** if it is a positive bank balance (`Dr`), OR on the **CREDIT side** if your Cash Book shows a **Bank Overdraft** (`Cr`)!

### Rule B — What Gets Ticked Off (`✓`) vs What Is Left Unticked (`✗`)?

| Status After Cross-Ticking (Dr CB ↔ Cr BS, Cr CB ↔ Dr BS) | What Is Included? | Exact Action to Take |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`✓` Ticked on BOTH Cash Book & Bank Statement** | Opening balance (if matched), cleared cheques paid, cleared customer deposits & cash banked. | **Already agrees — leave alone!** Do NOT copy ticked items into the Updated Cash Book or Reconciliation! |
| **`✗` Unticked on BANK STATEMENT *(Missing from Cash Book)*** | **Bank Statement Credit:** Credit Transfers, Direct Deposits, Dividends, Bank Interest Received.<br/>**Bank Statement Debit:** Standing Orders, Direct Debits, Bank Charges, Dishonoured Cheques (NSF). | **PUT INTO THE UPDATED CASH BOOK!** *(Flip sides: BS Credit → Updated CB **Debit**; BS Debit → Updated CB **Credit**!)* Also fix any **Cash Book amount error** using **the difference only**. |
| **`✗` Unticked in CASH BOOK *(Missing from Bank Statement)*** | **Cash Book Credit:** Unpresented Cheques.<br/>**Cash Book Debit:** Late Lodgements (Uncredited Deposits). | **DO NOT put in the Updated Cash Book!** Copy ONLY these timing differences into the **Bank Reconciliation Statement**! |

## 3. Quick Reference: Every Unticked Item Sorted

| Where Is the Unticked Item? | What It Is (Plain-English Meaning) | Exactly Where You Put It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bank Statement — CREDIT** *(not in Cash Book)* | **Credit Transfer / Direct Deposit** (customer paid straight into your bank), **Interest Received**, **Dividends Received** | **Step 1: DEBIT side of Updated Cash Book** *(increases bank balance)* |
| **Bank Statement — DEBIT** *(not in Cash Book)* | **Standing Order** (fixed regular payment like rent/insurance), **Direct Debit** (variable utility bill like JPS/Flow), **Bank Charges**, **Dishonoured Cheque** (customer's cheque bounced — NSF!) | **Step 1: CREDIT side of Updated Cash Book** *(decreases bank balance)* |
| **Cash Book Recording Error** | You wrote the wrong amount on a cheque or deposit in your Cash Book | **Step 1: Updated Cash Book** — adjust by the **DIFFERENCE ONLY** *(never the whole cheque!)* |
| **Cash Book — CREDIT** *(not on Bank Statement)* | **Unpresented Cheques** — cheques you wrote to suppliers that they haven't cashed at the bank yet | **Step 2: Bank Reconciliation Statement** *(ADD when starting from Updated Cash Book)* |
| **Cash Book — DEBIT** *(not on Bank Statement)* | **Late Lodgements / Uncredited Deposits** — cash/cheques you deposited near month-end that the bank hasn't credited yet | **Step 2: Bank Reconciliation Statement** *(SUBTRACT when starting from Updated Cash Book)* |

## 5. Worked Example 2: Overdraft Reconciliation & Cash Book Cheque Error!

Examiners repeatedly test **Bank Overdrafts** and **Cheque Amount Recording Errors** — here is the clean, foolproof negative-number method so you NEVER flip a sign!

<WorkedExample
  title="M. Gordon — Overdraft Updated Cash Book & Bank Reconciliation Statement"
  marks={10}
  problem={
    <>
      On 30 November 2026, <strong>M. Gordon’s</strong> Cash Book showed a <strong>Bank Overdraft (Credit balance) of J$15,000</strong>.
      Checking the Bank Statement revealed:
      <br />
      1. Credit Transfer received from a debtor: <strong>J$9,500</strong>
      <br />
      2. Standing Order: <strong>J$2,500</strong> &nbsp;|&nbsp; Bank Charges: <strong>J$500</strong>
      <br />
      3. A cheque paid to supplier J. Brown for <strong>J$4,200</strong> was wrongly entered on the Credit side of the Cash Book as <strong>J$2,400</strong>.
      <br />
      4. Timing differences: Unpresented Cheques <strong>J$6,800</strong>; Late Lodgements <strong>J$3,500</strong>.
      <br />
      Prepare the <strong>Updated Cash Book</strong> and the <strong>Bank Reconciliation Statement</strong> to find the Bank Statement balance.
    </>
  }
  examTip="CRITICAL EXAM ALERT on Error #3: NEVER record the full J$4,200 again! You already credited J$2,400, so credit ONLY the missing DIFFERENCE (4,200 − 2,400 = J$1,800) in the Updated Cash Book!"
>

### Step 1 — Updated Cash Book *(Notice Overdraft starts on the CREDIT side!)*

<TAccount
  title="M. Gordon — Updated Cash Book (Bank Column, 30 Nov 2026)"
  debit={[
    { date: 'Nov 30', details: 'Credit Transfer (Debtor)', amount: '9,500' },
    { date: 'Nov 30', details: 'Balance c/d (Adjusted Overdraft)', amount: '10,300', isBalance: true },
  ]}
  credit={[
    { date: 'Nov 30', details: 'Balance b/d (Overdraft)', amount: '15,000', isBalance: true },
    { date: 'Nov 30', details: 'Standing Order', amount: '2,500' },
    { date: 'Nov 30', details: 'Bank Charges', amount: '500' },
    { date: 'Nov 30', details: 'J. Brown (error: 4,200 − 2,400)', amount: '1,800' },
  ]}
  total="19,800"
  creditBroughtDown={{ date: 'Dec 1', details: 'Balance b/d (Overdraft)', amount: '10,300', isBalance: true }}
  note="Adjusted Cash Book shows a Credit (Overdraft) balance of J$10,300 — treat it as NEGATIVE (-10,300) in the reconciliation!"
/>

### Step 2 — Bank Reconciliation Statement *(Using Signed Brackets — Same Rules Always Work!)*

| Particulars | J\$ |
| :--- | :---: |
| **Overdraft as per Updated Cash Book (Cr)** *(negative!)* | **(10,300)** |
| **Add:** Unpresented Cheques | +6,800 |
| | (3,500) |
| **Less:** Late Lodgements (Uncredited Deposits) | (3,500) |
| **Overdraft as per Bank Statement (Dr)** | **(7,000)** |

*(Quick Reverse Check from Bank Statement Overdraft: $-7{,}000 + 3{,}500 - 6{,}800 = -10{,}300$ ✓ — exact match!)*

</WorkedExample>
