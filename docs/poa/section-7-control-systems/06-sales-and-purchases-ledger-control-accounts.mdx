:::info Essential Understanding
Constructing a **Sales Ledger Control Account (SLCA)** or **Purchases Ledger Control Account (PLCA)** is easy once you unlock ONE secret: **treat the SLCA like ONE GIANT DEBTOR (Asset)** and **treat the PLCA like ONE GIANT CREDITOR (Liability)**! Anything that makes customers owe us MORE goes on the **Debit** of SLCA; anything that cuts what customers owe us goes on the **Credit** of SLCA — and PLCA is the exact mirror image!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Constructing **SLCA (Total Debtors)** and **PLCA (Total Creditors)** including **minor opposite balances**, **Set-Offs (Contras)**, **Dishonored Cheques**, and **Interest Charged** |
| **Exam Focus** | **EXCLUDING the 4 classic trap items** (`Cash Sales`, `Cash Purchases`, `Provision for Doubtful Debts`, `Trade Discounts`) and placing minor opposite opening/closing balances on the exact right sides! |
| **Learning Outcome** | Construct fully balanced Sales Ledger and Purchases Ledger Control Accounts accurately from mixed month-end schedules |

## 1. The 4 Classic Exam Traps (NEVER Put These in Control Accounts!)

Examiners ALWAYS slip 2 or 3 of these decoy items into a Control Account question to test whether you truly understand credit ledgers — **cross them out immediately!**

| Decoy Item | Why It MUST Be EXCLUDED From SLCA &amp; PLCA |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Cash Sales** | Paid immediately in cash (`Dr Cash / Cr Sales`) — **never enters a customer’s credit account!** |
| **2. Cash Purchases** | Paid immediately in cash (`Dr Purchases / Cr Cash`) — **never enters a supplier’s credit account!** |
| **3. Provision for Doubtful Debts** | Kept in its own **General Ledger** contra-asset account — **never reduces individual customer balances!** *(Note: Confirmed **Bad Debts** DO go in SLCA Cr!)* |
| **4. Trade Discounts** | Deducted on the invoice **before** recording Credit Sales/Purchases — never entered in any ledger! *(Note: **Cash Discounts Allowed/Received** DO go in Control Accounts!)* |

*(Bonus note: **Bad Debts Recovered** is also normally excluded unless first reinstated through the debtor’s account on BOTH Dr and Cr — netting to `$0`!)*

### Why Can a Control Account Have TWO Balances at the Same Time?
- **Minor Credit Balance in SLCA** *(Customer we owe money to)*: Happens when a customer **overpays** their invoice, pays a deposit in advance, or **returns goods after already paying in full**.
- **Minor Debit Balance in PLCA** *(Supplier who owes US money)*: Happens when we **overpay** a supplier or **return goods after paying our bill in full**.
- **Rule:** NEVER net them together into one single figure! Carry **BOTH** balances down separately on their exact sides!

    },
    {
      statement: 'A Dishonored Cheque received from a credit customer is posted to the CREDIT side of the Sales Ledger Control Account.',
      answer: false,
      explanation: 'A bounced cheque REINSTATES what the customer owes us, so it increases Debtors → DEBIT side of SLCA!',
    },
    {
      statement: 'A Set-Off (Contra) between a customer who is also a supplier is CREDITED to the SLCA and DEBITED to the PLCA.',
      answer: true,
      explanation: 'Yes! Canceling mutual debt reduces both Trade Receivables (Cr SLCA) and Trade Payables (Dr PLCA).',
    },
    {
      statement: 'Provision for Doubtful Debts is credited inside the Sales Ledger Control Account.',

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **SLCA = Giant Debtor (Dr ↑ / Cr ↓)**; **PLCA = Giant Creditor (Cr ↑ / Dr ↓)**!
- **Contra / Set-Off:** **Cr SLCA** &amp; **Dr PLCA**.
- **EXCLUDE:** **Cash Sales**, **Cash Purchases**, **Provision for Doubtful Debts**, and **Trade Discounts**!
:::
