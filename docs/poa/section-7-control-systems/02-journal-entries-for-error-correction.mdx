:::info Essential Understanding
In real accounting, you are **NEVER allowed to use correction fluid, scratch out figures, or rip pages out of a ledger** — that destroys the audit trail! Instead, every mistake is corrected transparently through a formal **General Journal entry** (complete with a clear **narration**). Use the foolproof **3-Step Method** (*Wrong Done → Right Needed → Correcting Entry*), and even the trickiest Reversal or Suspense entry takes 15 seconds!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Writing **General Journal correcting entries** (with narrations) for **two-sided COPCOR errors** AND **single-sided Suspense errors** |
| **Exam Focus** | Never forgetting the **`2×` amount rule** for Reversals &amp; Wrong-Side postings, checking whether an error needs **Suspense**, and writing crisp **narrations** |
| **Learning Outcome** | Draft accurate General Journal entries (with narrations) to correct any two-sided or single-sided accounting error |

## 1. The 3-Step Method + Quick Correction Patterns

Whenever a question asks for correcting Journal Entries, run this quick check on scrap paper:
1. **Step 1 — What WAS done?** *(Write the wrong Dr &amp; Cr posted)*
2. **Step 2 — What SHOULD have been done?** *(Write the right Dr &amp; Cr)*
3. **Step 3 — Bridge Step 1 → Step 2:**
   - Leave any **correct** half completely untouched!
   - **Cancel** any wrong Debit by **Crediting** it (and cancel any wrong Credit by **Debiting** it).
   - **Record** any missing correct Debit or Credit.
   - If only **ONE** real account needs fixing → plug **`Suspense`** on the opposite side!

### Quick Pattern Reference Table

| Error Situation | How to Fix It Instantly | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Error of Commission / Principle** | **Dr** Right A/c / **Cr** Wrong A/c *(or reverse if Credit side was wrong)* | New Van `$8,000` put in Repairs → `Dr Motor Vehicles $8,000 / Cr Repairs $8,000` |
| **Complete Omission** | Just post the **normal entry**! | Omitted credit sale `$700` to J. Lee → `Dr J. Lee $700 / Cr Sales $700` |
| **Error of Original Entry (Too Low)** | Post the **missing difference** on the normal sides | `$520` purchase posted as `$250` (short `$270`) → `Dr Purchases $270 / Cr Supplier $270` |
| **Error of Original Entry (Too High)** | **Reverse** the excess difference | `$350` sale posted as `$530` (over `$180`) → `Dr Sales $180 / Cr Customer $180` |
| **Complete Reversal of Entries** | Post correct sides for **DOUBLE (`2×`) the amount!** | Paid `$450` cash to Creditor reversed → `Dr Creditor $900 / Cr Cash $900` |
| **Day Book Undercast / Overcast** | Fix the ONE nominal account + **Suspense** on opposite side! | Sales Book undercast `$300` → `Dr Suspense $300 / Cr Sales $300` |

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **3-Step Rule:** `Wrong Done` → `Right Needed` → `Correcting Entry` (never touch an already-correct half!).
- **Undercast** = add to **normal side**; **Overcast** = post to **opposite side**!
- **Complete Reversal** OR **Wrong-Side Single Posting** = **DOUBLE (`2×`) the amount**!
:::
