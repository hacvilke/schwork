## 1. What Are Control Accounts? (Kid-Friendly Explanation!)

Imagine a large Jamaican hardware wholesaler with **800 credit customers (Debtors)** in its Sales Ledger and **250 credit suppliers (Creditors)** in its Purchases Ledger. If the Trial Balance fails to balance, checking 1,050 individual accounts one by one would take days!

A **Control Account** is a **summary ("Total") account kept in the General Ledger** built directly from the **totals of the Books of Original Entry**:
- **Sales Ledger Control Account (Total Debtors Account):** Summarises **ALL credit customers** in one T-account. Its closing balance MUST equal the sum of every individual debtor in the Sales Ledger!
- **Purchases Ledger Control Account (Total Creditors Account):** Summarises **ALL credit suppliers** in one T-account. Its closing balance MUST equal the sum of every individual creditor in the Purchases Ledger!

### Four Exam Uses of Control Accounts
1. **Quick Totals for Final Accounts:** Gives Total Debtors and Total Creditors immediately without adding hundreds of pages.
2. **Locates Errors Fast:** Tells you immediately *which* ledger (Sales, Purchases, or General) contains an error.
3. **Detects & Prevents Fraud:** Prepared by a senior accountant independently of the clerks running the Sales and Purchases Ledgers (separation of duties!).
4. **Improves Credit Control:** Helps management monitor overdue debtors and cash flow closely.

## 2. Exact Blueprint & Sources of Information

### A. Sales Ledger Control Account *(Total Debtors — Normal DEBIT Balance!)*
Think of it as one giant **Debtor Account**: anything that makes customers owe us **MORE** goes on **Debit**; anything that reduces what customers owe us goes on **Credit**!

| **DEBIT Side** *(Increases what Debtors owe)* | Source Document / Book | **CREDIT Side** *(Decreases what Debtors owe)* | Source Document / Book |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Opening Balance b/d *(Dr)* | Previous Control A/c | Opening Balance b/d *(minority Cr)* | Previous Control A/c |
| **Credit Sales** | **Sales Journal** | **Cash & Cheques received from Debtors** | **Cash Book (Dr)** |
| **Dishonoured Cheques** *(debt restored!)* | **Cash Book (Cr)** | **Discount Allowed** | **Cash Book (Dr)** |
| **Interest charged on overdue accounts** | **General Journal** | **Returns Inwards** *(Sales Returns)* | **Returns Inwards Journal** |
| Cash refunded to customers *(overpayment)* | Cash Book (Cr) | **Bad Debts written off** | **General Journal** |
| Closing Balance c/d *(minority Cr)* | Sales Ledger list | **Set-off / Contra** *(transferred to PLCA)* | **General Journal** |

### B. Purchases Ledger Control Account *(Total Creditors — Normal CREDIT Balance!)*
Think of it as one giant **Creditor Account**: anything that makes us owe suppliers **MORE** goes on **Credit**; anything that reduces what we owe suppliers goes on **Debit**!

| **DEBIT Side** *(Decreases what we owe)* | Source Document / Book | **CREDIT Side** *(Increases what we owe)* | Source Document / Book |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Opening Balance b/d *(minority Dr)* | Previous Control A/c | Opening Balance b/d *(Cr)* | Previous Control A/c |
| **Cash & Cheques paid to Creditors** | **Cash Book (Cr)** | **Credit Purchases** | **Purchases Journal** |
| **Discount Received** | **Cash Book (Cr)** | Interest charged by suppliers | General Journal |
| **Returns Outwards** *(Purchases Returns)* | **Returns Outwards Journal** | Cash refunds received from suppliers | Cash Book (Dr) |
| **Set-off / Contra** *(transferred from SLCA)* | **General Journal** | Closing Balance c/d *(minority Dr)* | Purchases Ledger list |

:::warning Classic Exam Distractors — NEVER Put These Inside a Control Account!
- **Cash Sales** & **Cash Purchases** *(Control accounts track **CREDIT** debtors and creditors ONLY!)*
- **Provision for Doubtful Debts** *(Provision is an estimate in the General Ledger — never entered in individual customer accounts or the Sales Ledger Control Account!)*
- **Trade Discounts** *(Never recorded anywhere in the ledger!)*
:::

:::tip What Are "Minority Balances" and "Set-Off (Contra)"?
- **Minority Credit Balance in Sales Ledger Control:** Happens when a debtor **overpays** their invoice, returns goods *after* already paying in full, or pays a deposit in advance (now *we* owe *them* a small refund — Current Liability!).
- **Set-Off (Contra Entry):** When the same firm is **BOTH** our credit customer **AND** our credit supplier, we cancel out the smaller balance between both ledgers: **Cr Sales Ledger Control** and **Dr Purchases Ledger Control**!
:::
