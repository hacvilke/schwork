## 1. The 2-Stage Adjusted Cash Book Method (Memorize Both Templates!)

### Stage 1: Adjusted Cash Book (Bank Column Only)
1. Start with the unadjusted `Balance b/d` (**Debit** if positive cash; **Credit** if Bank Overdraft!).
2. **Debit** missing bank receipts *(Credit Transfers, Dividends, Bank Interest, Cash Book receipt understatements)*.
3. **Credit** missing bank payments *(Bank Charges, Overdraft Interest, Standing Orders, Direct Debits, Dishonored Cheques, Cash Book payment understatements)*.
4. Balance off to obtain the **Adjusted Cash Book `Balance c/d` → `Balance b/d`** — **THIS is the exact Bank figure reported on the Balance Sheet!**

### Stage 2: Bank Reconciliation Statement (as at Month-End)

<FormulaCard
  title="Universal Bank Reconciliation Formula (Works for BOTH Positive Cash AND Overdrafts!)"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Treat a Positive Balance as (+) and an Overdraft as (−) — then the EXACT SAME formula works 100% of the time!"
>

$$
\text{Balance per Adjusted Cash Book} \; (+ \text{ if Dr}, \; - \text{ if Cr Overdraft})
$$

$$
+\; \text{Unpresented Cheques} \quad -\; \text{Uncredited Deposits (Late Lodgements)}
$$

$$
=\; \text{Balance per Bank Statement} \; (+ \text{ if Cr}, \; - \text{ if Dr Overdraft})
$$

</FormulaCard>

*(Note: If a question ever asks you to start from the **Bank Statement Balance** and work backward to the Adjusted Cash Book, simply reverse the two signs: **`− Unpresented Cheques`** and **`+ Uncredited Deposits`**!)*

### Why Do Businesses Reconcile Their Bank Account Every Month?
1. **Detects bank errors &amp; Cash Book errors early** (while disputes can still be corrected!).
2. **Deters cash theft &amp; cheque fraud** (missing deposits or unauthorized cheques stand out immediately!).
3. **Identifies stale unpresented cheques** *(usually cheques uncashed for &gt; 6 months)* and bounced customer cheques quickly so credit control can act.
4. **Supplies the true updated Bank Balance** required for the Balance Sheet!
