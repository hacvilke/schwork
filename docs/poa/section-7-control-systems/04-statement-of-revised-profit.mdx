:::info Essential Understanding
After you fix errors in the General Journal and clear the Suspense Account, the owner immediately asks: **"Wait — my Draft Income Statement said I earned $28,400 Net Profit! What is my REAL profit after fixing all these mistakes?"** You do NOT need to redraw the entire Income Statement from scratch! You simply prepare a **Statement of Revised (Corrected) Net Profit** — and one crystal-clear rule tells you in 2 seconds whether any error pushes profit **UP**, **DOWN**, or leaves it **UNCHANGED**!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | **Statement of Revised Net Profit**, identifying **Nominal (Income Statement) accounts** vs. **Balance Sheet accounts**, and handling **Inventory valuation errors** |
| **Exam Focus** | Using the **Nominal Account Cr (+Profit) / Dr (−Profit)** rule reliably, explicitly marking Balance-Sheet-only errors as **"No Effect"**, and never flipping the sign on **Opening vs. Closing Inventory** mistakes |
| **Learning Outcome** | Prepare an accurate vertical Statement of Revised Net Profit starting from any Draft Net Profit and list of discovered errors |

## 1. The 2-Second Profit-Impact Filter

Look straight at the **Correcting Journal Entry** you just wrote in Lesson 7.2/7.3:
- **Does the entry touch a NOMINAL (Income Statement) account** *(Sales, Purchases, Returns, Carriage, Discount Allowed/Received, Rent, Wages, Repairs, Bad Debts, Depreciation, Other Income)*?
  - If the correcting entry **CREDITS** that Nominal Account → **ADD to Draft Profit (`+`)**! *(Either Revenue went UP, or an Expense/Purchase went DOWN!)*
  - If the correcting entry **DEBITS** that Nominal Account → **DEDUCT from Draft Profit (`−`)**! *(Either an Expense/Purchase went UP, or Revenue went DOWN!)*
- **Does the entry touch ONLY Balance Sheet accounts** *(Debtors, Creditors, Bank, Cash, Machinery, Drawings, Capital, Loans, Suspense)*?
  - → **NO EFFECT on Net Profit (`$0`)!** *(Always write `"No Effect"` in your workings so the examiner sees you checked it!)*

### Watch Out for Inventory Valuation Errors! (Classic Exam Trap)

| Inventory Mistake Discovered | Effect on COGS | Effect on Draft Profit | How to Fix Draft Profit |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Closing Inventory OVERVALUED** *(too high)* | COGS was too LOW | Profit was **TOO HIGH** | **DEDUCT (`−`)** from Draft Profit! |
| **Closing Inventory UNDERVALUED** *(too low)* | COGS was too HIGH | Profit was **TOO LOW** | **ADD (`+`)** to Draft Profit! |
| **Opening Inventory OVERVALUED** *(too high)* | COGS was too HIGH | Profit was **TOO LOW** | **ADD (`+`)** to Draft Profit! |
| **Opening Inventory UNDERVALUED** *(too low)* | COGS was too LOW | Profit was **TOO HIGH** | **DEDUCT (`−`)** from Draft Profit! |

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Cr Nominal A/c** → **ADD (`+`)** to Draft Profit; **Dr Nominal A/c** → **DEDUCT (`−`)** from Draft Profit; **Balance Sheet only** → **No Effect (`$0`)**!
- **Closing Stock overvalued** → **DEDUCT**; **Opening Stock overvalued** → **ADD**!
:::
