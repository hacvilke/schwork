:::info Essential Understanding
At the end of every month or financial year, open T-accounts must be neatly wrapped up so their final amounts can enter the **Trial Balance** and **Financial Statements**. **Permanent accounts** (Assets, Liabilities, Capital) are **balanced off** using **`Balance c/d`** and **`Balance b/d`** so their value rolls forward into next year, while **Nominal (temporary) accounts** (Sales, Purchases, Rent, Wages) are **closed off** to the **Income Statement** so Year 2 starts fresh at $0!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Exact 5-step **`c/d` → `b/d`** balancing procedure + distinguishing **balancing** (permanent accounts) from **closing** (nominal accounts & Drawings) |
| **Exam Focus** | Ruling up balanced T-accounts with matching total lines on the same horizontal row, bringing down `Balance b/d` on the right side, and closing Nominal accounts to the Income Statement |
| **Learning Outcome** | Balance any permanent ledger account cleanly and close any nominal account accurately at year-end |

## 1. Balancing vs. Closing — Know Which Action to Take!

| Feature | **Balancing Off** (`c/d` → `b/d`) | **Closing Off** (Transfer to Income Statement / Capital) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Which Accounts?** | **Permanent accounts:** Real Accounts (Assets) & Personal Accounts (Debtors, Creditors, Loans, Capital) | **Temporary accounts:** All **Nominal Accounts** (Sales, Purchases, Returns, Expenses, Other Income) + **Drawings** *(closed to Capital)* |
| **When Done?** | End of every month and end of financial year | End of the **financial year** (after extracting the Trial Balance!) |
| **Final Line Above Totals** | **`Balance c/d`** *(carried down)* on the shorter side | **`Income Statement`** *(or `Capital` for Drawings)* on the shorter side |
| **Line Below Totals (Next Year)** | **`Balance b/d`** *(brought down)* on the **opposite (larger) side** dated Day 1 of new period | **NONE!** Account starts the brand-new year completely empty at **$0**! |

:::note What If Both Sides Are Already Equal — Or Only ONE Entry Exists?
- **Already Equal:** If Dr total = $1,000 and Cr total = $1,000, simply rule double-underlined totals of $1,000 on both sides — **no `c/d` or `b/d` needed** (the account is settled at $0!).
- **Single Entry on One Side:** If an Equipment account only has `Jan 4 Bank $5,000` on the Debit side, you still write `Dec 31 Balance c/d $5,000` on the Credit side, total $5,000 = $5,000, and bring down `Jan 1 Balance b/d $5,000` on the Debit side!
:::

---

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **`c/d`** = Last day of month/year · **Shorter side** (pads totals to match).
- **`b/d`** = **Day 1 of next period** · **Larger side** (names the TRUE Debit or Credit balance!).
- **Permanent (Assets/Liabilities/Capital)** → **Balance (`c/d` → `b/d`)**; **Nominal (Revenue/Expenses)** → **Close to Income Statement**!
:::
