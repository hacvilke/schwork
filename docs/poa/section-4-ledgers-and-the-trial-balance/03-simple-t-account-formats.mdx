:::info Essential Understanding
A **Ledger Account** is simply a dedicated page keeping all increases and decreases for **ONE single item** (like Bank, Rent, or Customer *K. Brown*) in one place. Accountants use two clean layouts: the classic two-sided **T-Account** (shaped like a giant capital **T** with Debit on the left and Credit on the right) and the modern **Running-Balance (3-Column) Account** used by banks and accounting software.
:::

## 1. Anatomy of Both Standard Ledger Formats

### Format 1: Classic T-Account (Horizontal / Two-Sided Style)
Every T-Account has **one centered account title**, **`Dr`** in the top-left corner, **`Cr`** in the top-right corner, and **four identical columns on each side**:
- **Left side (Debit):** `Date | Details | Folio | $`
- **Right side (Credit):** `Date | Details | Folio | $`

### Format 2: Running-Balance Account (Three-Column / Vertical Style)
Instead of splitting left and right halves, every entry goes straight down in chronological order across six columns:
- `Date | Details | Folio | Debit ($) | Credit ($) | Balance ($)` *(labeled `Dr` or `Cr` on every row!)*

### The #1 Golden Presentation Rule: **The Opposite Account Name Rule**
Inside the **Details** column of any ledger account, **NEVER write the account’s own name!** Always write the **name of the OTHER account** that completes the double entry!
- *Example:* Paid $800 Rent by cheque (`Dr Rent $800`, `Cr Bank $800`):
  - Inside the **Rent Account** (on the Debit side), write Details = **`Bank`** (NOT "Rent"!).
  - Inside the **Bank Account** (on the Credit side), write Details = **`Rent`** (NOT "Bank"!).
- *Why?* Writing the cross-reference lets an auditor trace instantly WHERE the matching half of the double entry lives!

**Dr &emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp; R. Thomas Account (Sales Ledger) &emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp; Cr**

| May 5 | Sales | 900 | May 18 | Bank | 800 |
| May 24 | Sales | 450 | **May 31** | **Balance c/d** | **1,000** |
| | **Total** | **1,950** | | **Total** | **1,950** |

**2. Three-Column Running-Balance Format (5 marks):**

**R. Thomas Account (Sales Ledger)**

| Date | Details | Debit ($) | Credit ($) | Balance ($) |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| May 1 | Balance b/d | — | — | **600 Dr** |
| May 5 | Sales | 900 | — | **1,500 Dr** |
| May 9 | Returns Inwards | — | 150 | **1,350 Dr** |
| May 18 | Bank | — | 800 | **550 Dr** |
| May 24 | Sales | 450 | — | **1,000 Dr** |

*(Check: Both layouts end cleanly at **$1,000 Dr**, meaning R. Thomas owes us $1,000 at May 31!)*

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Left = `Dr`**, **Right = `Cr`**.
- **Opposite Account Name Rule:** Inside Account A, write Account B in Details (never write Account A inside itself!).
- **Running-Balance Format:** Always tag every Balance figure with **`Dr`** or **`Cr`**!
:::
