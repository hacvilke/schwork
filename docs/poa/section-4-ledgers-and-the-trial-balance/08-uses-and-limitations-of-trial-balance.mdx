When a Trial Balance agrees (`Total Debits = Total Credits`), it feels wonderful — **but do not celebrate too soon!** While the Trial Balance is an essential check on **arithmetical accuracy** and a handy launchpad for preparing financial statements, it has **one huge limitation**: **six specific types of accounting errors can hide right inside a perfectly balanced Trial Balance!**

## 1. Three Core Uses of a Trial Balance

1. **Tests Arithmetical Accuracy of Double Entry:** Confirms that for every Debit recorded, an equal Credit was posted across the ledgers (immediately catching one-sided entries, unequal postings, or ledger addition errors).
2. **Summarizes All Balances in One Place:** Gathers every single account balance from Sales, Purchases, Cash, and General Ledgers onto one compact schedule — making drafting the **Income Statement** and **Balance Sheet** fast and organized.
3. **Helps Locate Errors Early:** If Debits ≠ Credits, accountants investigate immediately *before* printing misleading financial statements for owners, banks, or tax authorities.

### The Crucial Limitation: Six Errors That Do NOT Affect Trial Balance Agreement

Why can a Trial Balance STILL balance when mistakes occurred? Because **as long as Total Debits and Total Credits are altered by the exact same dollar amount** (even if the amount, side, or account name is completely wrong!), the two columns STILL match!

| # | Error Type | Plain-English Meaning | Classic Example | Why `Dr` Still = `Cr` |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Error of Omission** | A transaction is **completely left out** of the books (neither Dr nor Cr entered) | Forgot to record a $600 credit sale to *K. Lee* at all | Both sides are missing $600 (`$0 = $0`) |
| **2** | **Error of Commission** | Posted to the **wrong person’s account** of the **SAME class** | Debited *J. Brown* instead of *K. Brown* for a $400 credit sale | Both are Personal Debtor accounts; total Debits unchanged! |
| **3** | **Error of Principle** | Posted to the **wrong CLASS of account** (violates accounting principles!) | Debited **Motor Expenses** (Nominal) instead of **Motor Vehicles** (Real Asset) when buying a $12,000 van | Both are Debit accounts, so Debits still match Credits — yet **Profit & Assets are badly distorted!** |
| **4** | **Error of Original Entry** | Wrong dollar figure entered in the **Book of Original Entry** and posted identically to BOTH sides | Recorded a $540 invoice as **$450** on BOTH Dr Purchases and Cr Supplier | Both sides are off by the same $90 (`$450 = $450`) |
| **5** | **Complete Reversal of Entries** | Right accounts and right amount, but **Debit and Credit completely swapped**! | Received $300 cash from customer *R. Hall*: mistakenly **Dr R. Hall $300 / Cr Cash $300**! | Dr $300 still equals Cr $300 — even though balances are off by **$600** (double!) |
| **6** | **Compensating Errors** | **Two unrelated errors** accidentally cancel each other out by the exact same dollar amount | Overcast (added too high) **Sales** (Cr) by $50 AND overcast **Rent** (Dr) by $50 | Extra +$50 Dr cancels extra +$50 Cr! |

## 4. Worked Example

<WorkedExample
  title="Diagnosing Errors: Does the Trial Balance Agree — And Which Error Is It?"
  marks={12}
  problem={
    <>
      <p>
        <strong>Question:</strong> Inspect the six accounting errors discovered at
        <strong> Bayshore Traders</strong> before year-end. For EACH error: (i) state whether
        the <strong>Trial Balance will STILL AGREE (`Yes` or `No`)</strong>, and (ii)
        <strong> name the exact error</strong> (or explain why agreement fails).
        <em>[2 marks each = 12 marks]</em>
      </p>
      <ol>
        <li>A credit sale of $720 to <em>P. Clarke</em> was accidentally debited to <em>R. Clarke</em>’s account (Sales was credited correctly).</li>
        <li>The purchase of a $9,500 delivery van by cheque was debited to <em>Motor Repairs Expense</em> and credited to Bank.</li>
        <li>A cash payment of $400 for Wages was credited to Cash, but the clerk completely forgot to post anything to the Wages Account.</li>
        <li>A credit purchase invoice of $630 from <em>Island Supply</em> was misread and recorded as $360 in the Purchases Journal and posted as $360 to both accounts.</li>
        <li>A $500 cheque received from customer <em>D. Reid</em> was debited to <em>D. Reid</em> and credited to <em>Bank</em>.</li>
        <li>The <em>Electricity Expense</em> column was added $80 too high, and the <em>Commission Received</em> account was also added $80 too high.</li>
      </ol>
    </>
  }
>

1. **Debited *R. Clarke* instead of *P. Clarke* ($720) — (2 marks):**
   - *Trial Balance still agrees?* **Yes** *(1 mark)*
   - *Error type:* **Error of Commission** *(wrong personal account within the same class — both are Debtors)* *(1 mark)*.

2. **$9,500 delivery van debited to *Motor Repairs Expense* — (2 marks):**
   - *Trial Balance still agrees?* **Yes** *(1 mark)*
   - *Error type:* **Error of Principle** *(wrong class of account — Nominal Expense used instead of Real Non-Current Asset)* *(1 mark)*.

3. **$400 Wages credited to Cash only (nothing posted to Wages) — (2 marks):**
   - *Trial Balance still agrees?* **No** *(Credits exceed Debits by $400)* *(1 mark)*
   - *Reason:* **Single-sided entry (Partial Omission)** — breaks double-entry equality *(1 mark)*.

4. **$630 purchase recorded & posted as $360 on both sides — (2 marks):**
   - *Trial Balance still agrees?* **Yes** *(1 mark)*
   - *Error type:* **Error of Original Entry** *(wrong starting amount posted equally to both Debit and Credit)* *(1 mark)*.

5. **Cheque received from *D. Reid* posted as Dr *D. Reid* / Cr *Bank* — (2 marks):**
   - *Trial Balance still agrees?* **Yes** *(1 mark)*
   - *Error type:* **Complete Reversal of Entries** *(correct accounts and amount, completely swapped sides)* *(1 mark)*.

6. **Electricity (Dr) +$80 too high AND Commission Received (Cr) +$80 too high — (2 marks):**
   - *Trial Balance still agrees?* **Yes** *(1 mark)*
   - *Error type:* **Compensating Errors** *(two independent $80 mistakes on opposite sides cancel out)* *(1 mark)*.

</WorkedExample>
