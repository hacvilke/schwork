# Principles of Accounts (POA)

Welcome to **Schwork Principles of Accounts** — structured like clean technical documentation for Jamaican high school students (Grades 10–11).

Every section puts **deep, textbook-quality study notes** front and centre — connecting **classic past-paper foundations** with **updated syllabus topics**, complete **worked examples in Jamaican dollars (J\$)**, and **hover/tap formula hints** — while optional **Quick Checks**, **True/False drills**, and **"Explain in Your Own Words"** activities stay neatly tucked inside a collapsed **Extra Practice** drawer at the bottom of each lesson.

---

## How the POA Exam Is Structured

| Paper | Format | What It Tests |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paper 1** | 60 Multiple-Choice Questions (1 hr 30 min) | Definitions, accounting principles, ethics, quick calculations, double entry, and interpreting accounts. |
| **Paper 2** | 5 Structured / Problem-Solving Questions (3 hrs) | Full journals, ledgers, trial balances, financial statements, adjustments, control systems, partnerships, companies, and payroll. |
| **Paper 3 / SBA** | School-Based Assessment (or Paper 3/2 Private) | Practical business accounting project: source documents, books of original entry, ledgers, trial balance, and financial statements. |

## Tips for Studying POA on Your Phone

- **Hover or tap any highlighted formula** (marked with `?`) to pop up a short plain-English explanation.
- **Swipe tables left and right** on narrow phone screens — columns stay intact without shrinking text.
- **Check off your objectives** at the top of each lesson; your progress saves automatically on your device with no account needed.
- **Practice theory first, then numbers**: Paper 1 and early parts of Paper 2 award quick, reliable marks for definitions, ethics, users, and accounting principles.
