# Principles of Accounts (POA)

Welcome to **Schwork Principles of Accounts** — a structured, topic-by-topic study guide covering all **11 Syllabus Sections (88 topics)** plus a cumulative **100-Point Final Capstone Project**.

Every single lesson follows one consistent, clear learning layout:
1. **Essential Understanding & Learning Objectives** (with a mastery progress bar)
2. **Structured Study Notes & Comparison Tables**
3. **2 Visual Diagrams** — 1 zoomable reference diagram + 1 interactive visual explorer
4. **1 Step-by-Step Worked Example** (exam-style scenario & mark breakdown)
5. **1 Interactive True / False Check** (with icon + text feedback and explanations)
6. **1 Hands-On Student Project** (scenario, checkable steps, deliverables, and self-check rubric)

Once you complete all 11 sections, test your full-course mastery in the **[POA Final Capstone Project: End-to-End Business Accounting Simulation](/docs/poa/final-capstone-project)**!
