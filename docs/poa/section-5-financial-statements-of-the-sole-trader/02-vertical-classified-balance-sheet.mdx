:::info Essential Understanding
Whereas you met the basic Balance Sheet in Section 2, **Sole-Trader Final Accounts** use the full **3-Column Vertical Classified Balance Sheet** (also called the *Statement of Financial Position*). It shows **Cost, Accumulated Depreciation, and Net Book Value (NBV)** for Non-Current Assets, deducts **Current Liabilities** from **Current Assets** to spotlight **Working Capital**, and proves that **Total Net Assets = Capital Employed** (`Financed By`) to the exact penny!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Full **3-column Vertical Balance Sheet** (`Cost | Accum. Depr. | NBV`), Working Capital block, Closing Capital bridge, and exact matching totals |
| **Exam Focus** | Building a complete sole-trader vertical Balance Sheet straight from a Trial Balance + Net Profit calculated in the Income Statement |
| **Learning Outcome** | Prepare a clean, properly classified 3-column Vertical Balance Sheet that balances accurately every time |

## 1. Two Valid Vertical Presentation Styles (Know Both!)

Both modern vertical layouts show the exact same facts and obey the accounting equation — just notice **where Non-Current Liabilities (e.g. a 5-Year Bank Loan)** sits:

| Vertical Layout Style | Top Half Formula | Bottom Half ("Financed By") Formula | When Used |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Style A: Capital Employed Style** *(Most Popular in POA!)* | `Non-Current Assets + Working Capital = Total Net Assets` | `Closing Capital + Non-Current Liabilities = Total Capital Employed` | Clearly highlights **Capital Employed** (needed immediately for **ROCE** ratio in Topic 5.6!) |
| **Style B: Net Worth Style** | `Non-Current Assets + Working Capital − Non-Current Liabilities = Net Assets` | `Closing Capital (Owner’s Equity)` | Directly displays the owner’s exact personal **Net Worth (`A − L = C`)** |

:::tip Why Three Money Columns Make Your Balance Sheet Spotless!
- In the **Non-Current Assets** block, the 3 money columns are labeled **`Cost ($)`**, **`Accum. Depr. ($)`**, and **`Net Book Value ($)`**.
- Directly below Non-Current Assets, those same 3 columns smoothly double as **Inner working column**, **Subtotal column**, and **Outer Final Total column** — keeping every subtotal crystal-clear for examiners!
:::

## 5. True or False Check

<TrueFalseQuiz
  title="True or False: Vertical Classified Balance Sheets"
  items={[
    {
      statement: 'Opening Inventory from the Trial Balance is the stock figure shown under Current Assets on the Balance Sheet.',
      answer: false,
      explanation: 'ALWAYS use CLOSING Inventory (the physical stock still owned on the Balance Sheet date, 31 Dec) under Current Assets — Opening Inventory belongs only in the Income Statement!',
    },
    {
      statement: 'Net Book Value (NBV) of a Non-Current Asset equals Original Cost minus Accumulated Depreciation.',
      answer: true,
      explanation: 'NBV = Cost − Accumulated Depreciation (Provision for Depreciation), showing the remaining undepreciated carrying value of the asset.',
    },
    {
      statement: 'Provision for Doubtful Debts is deducted directly from Trade Receivables under Current Assets on the Balance Sheet.',
      answer: true,
      explanation: 'Under the Prudence concept, Trade Receivables are shown at their realistic net realizable value (Trade Receivables − Provision for Doubtful Debts).',
    },
    {
      statement: 'In a Vertical Classified Balance Sheet, Total Net Assets must equal Closing Capital plus Non-Current Liabilities (Capital Employed).',
      answer: true,
      explanation: 'Because Assets − Current Liabilities = Capital + Non-Current Liabilities, Total Net Assets ALWAYS equals Total Capital Employed.',
    },
    {
      statement: 'Prepaid Insurance is classified as a Current Liability on the Balance Sheet.',
      answer: false,
      explanation: 'Prepaid expenses are benefits ALREADY PAID for in advance that belong to next year — making Prepaid Insurance a CURRENT ASSET!',
    },
  ]}
/>

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- Always use **`Balance Sheet as at [Exact Date]`** (never "for the year ended"!).
- **Non-Current Assets** → `Cost − Accum. Depr. = NBV`!
- **Trade Receivables** → subtract **Provision for Doubtful Debts** right inside Current Assets!
- **Prepaid Expenses** = Current **Asset**; **Accrued Expenses** = Current **Liability**!
:::
