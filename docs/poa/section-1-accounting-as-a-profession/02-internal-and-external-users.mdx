:::info Essential Understanding
Financial statements are never prepared in a vacuum. Different stakeholders read the **same set of accounts** to answer completely different questions — an investor looks for **profitability**, a lender looks for **liquidity and repayment security**, and a tax authority checks **taxable income**.
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Separating **Internal Users** (inside the organization) from **External Users** (outside parties) |
| **Exam Focus** | Matching each user group to the *exact* financial information they need and *why* |
| **Learning Outcome** | Classify stakeholders accurately and justify how financial reports inform each group's decisions |

## 1. Internal vs. External Users

Every person or organization that relies on financial information falls into one of two broad categories:

- **Internal Users** operate **inside** the business. They have access to both published financial statements and detailed confidential **management reports** (weekly sales breakdowns, departmental cost budgets, inventory turnover logs) used to run day-to-day operations.
- **External Users** sit **outside** the daily management of the business. They rely primarily on **published financial statements** (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement) and auditor reports to make lending, investing, buying, or regulatory decisions.

### Complete Stakeholder Reference Table

| Stakeholder Group | Category | Key Information Needed | Main Decision / Reason |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Owners / Proprietors** | Internal | Net profit, return on capital, drawings, asset growth | Decide whether the business is worth continuing, expanding, or restructuring |
| **Managers & Directors** | Internal | Departmental costs, budgets vs. actuals, cash forecasts | Plan operations, set selling prices, cut waste, and evaluate staff/branch performance |
| **Employees & Trade Unions** | Internal | Profitability, stability, payroll trends | Assess job security, bonus capacity, pension safety, and fair wage bargaining |
| **Investors / Shareholders** | External | Earnings per share, dividends, long-term profit growth | Decide whether to **buy, hold, or sell** shares in the company |
| **Banks & Lenders (Creditors)** | External | Cash flow, liquidity ratios, collateral, existing debt | Decide whether to approve a loan, what interest rate to charge, and repayment risk |
| **Suppliers (Trade Payables)** | External | Short-term liquidity and payment history | Decide whether to grant **trade credit** (e.g. 30-day payment terms) or demand cash on delivery |
| **Government & Tax Authorities** | External | Taxable profit, sales revenue, payroll deductions | Assess accurate income/corporate taxes, verify statutory compliance, and compile national statistics |
| **Customers** | External | Business stability and continuity | Ensure reliable ongoing supply, warranty fulfillment, and after-sales service |
| **Financial Analysts & Advisors** | External | Full multi-year financial statements and ratios | Advise clients on investments, mergers, or credit ratings |

## 4. Worked Example

<WorkedExample
  title="Classifying Users & Matching Financial Decisions"
  marks={8}
  problem={
    <>
      <p>
        <strong>Scenario:</strong> GreenWave Solar Ltd published its annual financial
        statements showing rising revenue, strong net profit, and healthy cash reserves.
        Four parties immediately requested copies of the statements:
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>(a) The Operations Manager of GreenWave Solar Ltd</li>
        <li>(b) Apex Commercial Bank, reviewing a 5-year warehouse loan request</li>
        <li>(c) SunParts Wholesale, which supplies panels on 45-day credit terms</li>
        <li>(d) The National Tax Authority</li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        For each party, state whether they are an <strong>Internal</strong> or{' '}
        <strong>External</strong> user, and explain <strong>one specific decision</strong> they
        will make using GreenWave Solar Ltd’s accounting information. <em>[2 marks each = 8 marks]</em>
      </p>
    </>
  }
>

1. **(a) Operations Manager — Internal User (2 marks):**
   - *Classification:* Internal user *(1 mark)*.
   - *Decision:* Uses cost and revenue figures to control installation expenses, set department budgets, or decide whether to hire additional technicians for busy regions *(1 mark)*.

2. **(b) Apex Commercial Bank — External User (2 marks):**
   - *Classification:* External user *(1 mark)*.
   - *Decision:* Evaluates cash flow, existing liabilities, and collateral assets to decide whether GreenWave Solar Ltd can reliably repay the 5-year loan plus interest *(1 mark)*.

3. **(c) SunParts Wholesale — External User (2 marks):**
   - *Classification:* External user *(1 mark)*.
   - *Decision:* Inspects short-term liquidity (working capital) to decide whether it is safe to keep supplying solar panels on 45-day credit terms or adjust the credit limit *(1 mark)*.

4. **(d) National Tax Authority — External User (2 marks):**
   - *Classification:* External user *(1 mark)*.
   - *Decision:* Reviews net profit and revenue figures to verify that the company has calculated and paid the correct corporate and sales taxes legally owed *(1 mark)*.

</WorkedExample>
