:::info Essential Understanding
Before you can build a club’s **Income and Expenditure Account**, you MUST prepare **three high-mark mini feeder accounts**: (1) the **Subscriptions Account** *(adjusting cash received for Opening &amp; Closing Arrears and Advances!)*, (2) a **Bar / Refreshments Trading Account** *(often using a Bar Payables Control Account to find Credit Purchases first!)*, and (3) a **Fundraising Event Account** *(e.g., Gala Dinner or Raffle)*. Mastering that **Subscriptions T-Account** alone is worth **5–8 guaranteed marks** on Paper 2!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | The **Subscriptions T-Account** (`Arrears = Asset`, `Advance = Liability`), **Bar / Café Trading Account** *(including Barman’s Wages + Payables Control!)*, and **Fundraising Event Surplus** |
| **Exam Focus** | Never mixing up **Arrears (`Dr b/d` Asset)** vs. **Advance (`Cr b/d` Liability)**, converting **Cash Paid to Bar Suppliers → Credit Purchases**, and transferring **ONLY Net Bar Profit** into Income &amp; Expenditure |
| **Learning Outcome** | Prepare accurate Subscriptions Accounts, Bar/Refreshments Trading Accounts, and Fundraising Event Accounts ready to feed into Income &amp; Expenditure |

## 1. Mastering the Subscriptions T-Account *(The #1 Club Exam Weapon!)*

Why do students struggle with Subscriptions? Because they try to memorize signs blindly instead of remembering **TWO crystal-clear facts**:
1. **Subscriptions in Arrears *(Owing by members)* = CURRENT ASSET (`DEBIT` balance!)** → Opens **`Dr b/d`**, Closes **`Cr c/d`** *(then brought down **`Dr b/d`**)*.
2. **Subscriptions in Advance *(Prepaid by members)* = CURRENT LIABILITY (`CREDIT` balance!)** → Opens **`Cr b/d`**, Closes **`Dr c/d`** *(then brought down **`Cr b/d`**)*.

<FormulaCard
  title="Subscriptions T-Account & Quick Formula Check"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Always draw the T-account — AND double-check with the formula below!"
>

| Debit (`Dr`) — Subscriptions Account | Credit (`Cr`) — Subscriptions Account |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1 Jan:** Balance b/d — **Opening Arrears** *(Asset)* | **1 Jan:** Balance b/d — **Opening Advance** *(Liability)* |
| **31 Dec:** **Income &amp; Expenditure A/c** *(BALANCING FIGURE!)* | **Year:** **Bank / Cash** *(Total Cash Received!)* |
| **31 Dec:** Balance c/d — **Closing Advance** *(Liability)* | **31 Dec:** Balance c/d — **Closing Arrears** *(Asset)* |

$$
\text{I\&E Subscriptions} = \text{Cash Received} - \text{Open Arrears} + \text{Open Advance} + \text{Close Arrears} - \text{Close Advance}
$$

</FormulaCard>

### Club Bar / Café Trading Account *(Don’t Forget Two Classic Add-Ons!)*
1. **Find True Credit Bar Purchases FIRST** if Bar Suppliers are owed money at the start/end of the year:
   $$\text{Bar Credit Purchases} = \text{Cash Paid to Bar Suppliers} + \text{Closing Bar Payables} - \text{Opening Bar Payables}$$
2. **Deduct Direct Bar Expenses** *(especially **Barman’s Wages** or Bar Electricity!)* right inside the Bar Trading Account so you transfer **Net Bar Profit** as ONE clean line to Income &amp; Expenditure!

## 5. True or False Check

<TrueFalseQuiz
  title="True or False: Subscriptions, Bar Trading & Fundraising Accounts"
  items={[
    {
      statement: 'Subscriptions in Arrears (owing by members at year-end) are a Current Asset and sit on the Credit side as Balance c/d (brought down Debit!).',
      answer: true,
      explanation: 'Correct! Arrears = money owed TO the club = Current Asset (Debit balance)!',
    },
    {
      statement: 'Subscriptions received in Advance at year-end are a Current Asset because the club already collected the cash.',
      answer: false,
      explanation: 'Subscriptions in Advance are a CURRENT LIABILITY — the club owes those members next year’s club access in exchange for their prepaid money!',
    },
    {
      statement: 'If Cash Paid to Bar Suppliers is $15,000, Opening Bar Payables are $1,000, and Closing Bar Payables are $1,800, then Bar Credit Purchases equal $15,800.',
      answer: true,
      explanation: '$15,000 + $1,800 − $1,000 = $15,800 exact!',
    },
    {
      statement: 'After deducting Barman’s Wages inside the Bar Trading Account to find Net Bar Profit, you should deduct Barman’s Wages a second time in the Income & Expenditure Account.',
      answer: false,
      explanation: 'NEVER double-count! Once Barman’s Wages are subtracted in the Bar Trading Account, ONLY Net Bar Profit transfers to Income & Expenditure!',
    },
    {
      statement: 'If members owe more Subscriptions at the end of the year than at the start (with zero advances), Subscriptions Income in I&E will be HIGHER than cash collected.',

      explanation: 'Spot on! You earned those unpaid subscriptions during the year, so Closing Arrears are ADDED to cash received!',

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Subscriptions T-Account:** **Arrears = Asset (`Open Dr / Close Cr`)**; **Advance = Liability (`Open Cr / Close Dr`)**; **Cash = `Cr`**; **I&amp;E = `Dr` balancing figure**!
- **Bar Credit Purchases:** `Cash Paid + Closing Payables − Opening Payables`. Deduct Barman’s Wages **ONCE** inside the Bar Trading Account!
:::
