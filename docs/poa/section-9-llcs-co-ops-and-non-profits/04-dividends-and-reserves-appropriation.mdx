:::info Essential Understanding
At the end of a profitable year, a company’s Board of Directors faces one big decision: **how much Net Profit should be PAID OUT to shareholders as Dividends, and how much should be PLOWED BACK into the company as Reserves (General Reserve & Retained Earnings)?** You show that exact split in the **Company Profit and Loss Appropriation Account** — right after deducting Debenture Interest and Corporation Tax!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | **LLC Appropriation Account**, **Interim vs. Final (Proposed) Dividends**, **Revenue Reserves vs. Capital Reserves**, and **Retained Earnings b/f → c/f** |
| **Exam Focus** | Computing `%` and cents-per-share dividends on **ISSUED capital**, distinguishing **Interim Paid** *(Approp. ONLY)* from **Proposed Unpaid** *(Approp. + Current Liability!)*, and subtracting Debenture Interest &amp; Tax **BEFORE** Appropriation |
| **Learning Outcome** | Calculate Preference and Ordinary Dividends accurately and construct a complete Limited Liability Company Appropriation Account |

## 1. Calculating Dividends & Distinguishing Interim vs. Proposed

<FormulaCard
  title="Two Dividend Calculation Formulas (Always Use ISSUED Shares!)"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Read whether the dividend is quoted as a PERCENTAGE (%) or as CENTS PER SHARE ($0.XX)!"
>

$$
\text{Percentage Dividend} = \text{Rate \%} \times \text{Issued Share Capital (\$)}
$$

$$
\text{Per-Share Dividend} = \text{Dividend per Share (\$)} \times \text{Number of Issued Shares}
$$

</FormulaCard>

### Interim Dividend vs. Final Proposed Dividend (Crucial Balance Sheet Rule!)

| Dividend Type | When Decided &amp; Paid | Appropriation Account? | Balance Sheet Current Liability? |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Interim Dividend** | Paid **mid-year** in cash *(already inside Trial Balance!)* | **YES** *(deducted)* | **NO!** *(Already paid out of Bank!)* |
| **Final (Proposed) Dividend** | Recommended at **year-end** *(in Additional Notes — still unpaid!)* | **YES** *(deducted)* | **YES — Current Liability!** *(Owed to shareholders!)* |

### Revenue Reserves vs. Capital Reserves (Paper 1 &amp; Paper 2 Favorite!)

| Type of Reserve | How Created | Can Pay Cash Dividends? | Classic Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Revenue Reserves** | Plowed back out of **trading profits** (via Appropriation Account) | **YES** *(distributable!)* | **General Reserve**, **Retained Earnings (Unappropriated Profit)**, Foreign Exchange / Expansion Reserve |
| **Capital Reserves** | Created from **non-trading capital gains** (NOT trading profits!) | **NO — Forbidden by Law!** | **Share Premium**, **Revaluation Reserve** (revaluing Premises upward) |

**Why transfer profit to a General Reserve?**
1. **Funds future expansion** (buying new factories/equipment) **without borrowing** or paying bank loan interest.
2. **Protects Working Capital** by keeping cash inside the company instead of paying 100% of profit out as dividends.
3. **Provides a rainy-day buffer** to keep dividends smooth or absorb losses in future economic downturns!

### 1. Kingston Tech Distributors Ltd — Profit & Loss Appropriation Account *(9 marks)*
*Pre-Appropriation Check:*
- Full-year Debenture Interest = `8% × $50,000` = **`$4,000`** *(deducted in Income Statement!)*
- Profit before Tax = `$82,000 − $4,000` = **`$78,000`**
- Less: Corporation Tax = `($13,000)` → **Net Profit After Tax = `$65,000`** *(2 marks)*

**Profit and Loss Appropriation Account for the year ended 31 December 2026** *(7 marks)*

| Details | $ | $ | $ |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Net Profit After Tax for the Year** | | | **65,000** |
| **Add: Retained Earnings Brought Forward (1 Jan)** | | | **18,000** |
| *Total Profit Available for Appropriation* | | | *83,000* |
| **Less: Appropriations** | | | |
| Transfer to General Reserve | | 15,000 | |
| *6% Preference Dividend (`6% × $100,000 = $6,000`):* | | | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Interim Paid | 3,000 | | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Final Proposed *(unpaid)* | 3,000 | 6,000 | |
| *Ordinary Dividend:* | | | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Interim Paid *(`$0.04 × 150,000 shares`)* | 6,000 | | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Final Proposed *(`8% × $150,000`)* | 12,000 | 18,000 | **(39,000)** |
| **Retained Earnings Carried Forward (31 Dec)** | | | **$44,000** |

### 2. Year-End Balance Sheet Impacts *(3 marks — 1 mark each)*
- **Closing General Reserve *(Revenue Reserve)*:** `$20,000 opening + $15,000 transfer` = **`$35,000`**.
- **Closing Retained Earnings *(Revenue Reserve)*:** **`$44,000`**.
- **Current Liabilities Created *(Unpaid Items ONLY!)*:** Accrued Debenture Interest `$2,000` + Corporation Tax Payable `$13,000` + Proposed Preference Dividend `$3,000` + Proposed Ordinary Dividend `$12,000` = **`$30,000`** *(Interim dividends `$3,000` &amp; `$6,000` were already paid — NOT liabilities!)*.

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Before Appropriation:** Deduct **Debenture Interest** &amp; **Corporation Tax** in the Income Statement!
- **Inside Appropriation:** `Net Profit + Retained b/f − Reserve Transfer − Pref Div − Ord Div = Retained c/f`.
- **Interim** = Approp. ONLY; **Proposed** = Approp. **AND** Current Liability!
:::
