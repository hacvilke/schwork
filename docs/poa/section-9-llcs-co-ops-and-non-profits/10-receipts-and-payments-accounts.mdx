:::info Essential Understanding
Every club’s Honorary Treasurer begins the year-end accounting process with a **Receipts and Payments Account** — which is literally a **summarized Cash Book** for the entire year! It follows **STRICT CASH-BASIS ACCOUNTING**: if cash actually moved into or out of the club’s tin or bank account during the year *(whether Capital or Revenue, whether for 2025, 2026, or 2027!)*, it goes IN; if NO cash moved *(Depreciation, Accrued Expenses, Unpaid Subscriptions)*, it stays **100% OUT**!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Constructing a **Receipts and Payments Account** (`Dr = Opening Cash + ALL Cash In`, `Cr = ALL Cash Out + Closing Cash c/d`) and contrasting it cleanly against the **Income and Expenditure Account** |
| **Exam Focus** | NEVER putting **Depreciation**, **Accruals**, or **Prepayments** inside Receipts &amp; Payments, ALWAYS including **Capital purchases/sales** if paid/received in cash, and explaining why **Closing Cash ≠ Annual Surplus** |
| **Learning Outcome** | Prepare a balanced Receipts and Payments Account and explain its exact differences from an Income and Expenditure Account |

## 1. Receipts & Payments Account vs. Income & Expenditure Account *(Exam Gold!)*

| Feature | Receipts &amp; Payments Account | Income &amp; Expenditure Account |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Accounting Basis** | **CASH BASIS** *(actual money received/paid ONLY!)* | **ACCRUAL BASIS** *(earned income &amp; incurred expenses!)* |
| **Nature of Account** | **Real Account** *(Summary of Cash Book)* | **Nominal Account** *(Non-profit Income Statement)* |
| **Opening &amp; Closing Balances** | Starts with **Opening Cash/Bank**; ends with **Closing Cash/Bank** | **NO** Opening/Closing cash balances! |
| **Capital vs. Revenue Items** | Includes **BOTH Capital AND Revenue** cash flows *(e.g. buying new equipment!)* | **REVENUE items ONLY!** *(Excludes Capital asset purchases!)* |
| **Time Period Covered** | Includes cash for **PAST, CURRENT, AND FUTURE** years! | Strictly **CURRENT YEAR ONLY!** *(Adjusted for accruals/prepayments!)* |
| **Non-Cash Items *(Depr.)*** | **EXCLUDED!** *(Zero depreciation or bad debts!)* | **INCLUDED!** *(Charges annual depreciation &amp; provisions!)* |
| **Final Balancing Figure** | **Closing Cash / Bank Balance** *(or Bank Overdraft!)* | **Surplus** or **Deficit** *(transferred to Accumulated Fund!)* |

## 4. Worked Example

<WorkedExample
  title="Preparing a Receipts & Payments Account & Diagnosing Cash vs. Surplus (Harborview Tennis Club)"
  marks={12}
  problem={
    <>
      <p>
        <strong>Scenario:</strong> The Honorary Treasurer of <strong>Harborview Tennis Club</strong>
        provides the following cash summary for the year ended <strong>31 December 2026</strong>:
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Opening Bank Balance (1 Jan 2026):</strong> <strong>$4,200</strong></li>
        <li><strong>Cash Received during 2026:</strong> Member Subscriptions collected **`$16,400`** *(includes `$600` for 2025 arrears, `$14,800` for 2026, and `$1,000` prepaid for 2027!)*; Annual Fundraising Dinner tickets **`$4,800`**; General Donations **`$2,600`**; Cash proceeds from selling an old tennis ball machine **`$1,000`**.</li>
        <li><strong>Cash Paid during 2026:</strong> Court Rent **`$7,200`** *(includes `$800` prepaid for Jan 2027)*; Groundsman’s Wages **`$6,400`**; Fundraising Dinner Catering **`$2,100`**; Purchase of NEW Tennis Nets &amp; Court Equipment **`$5,500`**; Electricity &amp; Water **`$1,800`**.</li>
        <li><em>Additional Note:</em> Annual depreciation on equipment was estimated at <strong>$1,200</strong>, and <strong>$300</strong> of electricity remained unpaid on 31 December 2026.</li>
      </ul>
      <ol>
        <li>Prepare Harborview Tennis Club’s <strong>Receipts and Payments Account</strong> for the year ended 31 December 2026, showing the exact <strong>Closing Bank Balance</strong>. <em>[8 marks]</em></li>
        <li>Identify <strong>four adjustments</strong> from the data above that explain why this Receipts &amp; Payments Account CANNOT be used directly to measure the club’s 2026 Surplus. <em>[4 marks]</em></li>
      </ol>
    </>
  }
>

### 1. Harborview Tennis Club — Receipts and Payments Account *(Year ended 31 December 2026 — 8 marks)*
*(Notice: ALL actual cash inflows and outflows go in — Depreciation `$1,200` and unpaid electricity `$300` are **100% EXCLUDED** because NO cash moved!)*

| Dr — Receipts | $ | Cr — Payments | $ |
| :--- | ---: | :--- | ---: |
| Balance b/d *(Opening Bank, 1 Jan)* | 4,200 | Court Rent | 7,200 |
| Member Subscriptions *(all years cash)* | 16,400 | Groundsman’s Wages | 6,400 |
| Fundraising Dinner Tickets | 4,800 | Fundraising Dinner Catering | 2,100 |
| General Donations | 2,600 | **Purchase of New Court Equipment** | **5,500** |
| **Sale of Old Ball Machine *(proceeds)*** | **1,000** | Electricity &amp; Water | 1,800 |
| | | **Balance c/d *(Closing Bank, 31 Dec)*** | **6,000** |
| **Total** | **$29,000** | **Total** | **$29,000** |
| Balance b/d *(1 Jan 2027)* | 6,000 | | |

*(Check: Total Receipts + Open Bank = `$4,200 + $16,400 + $4,800 + $2,600 + $1,000 = $29,000`; Total Payments = `$7,200 + $6,400 + $2,100 + $5,500 + $1,800 = $23,000` → **Closing Bank = `$6,000`** exact ✓!)*

### 2. Four Reasons Closing Bank (`$6,000`) Does NOT Measure 2026 Surplus *(4 marks — 1 mark each)*
1. **Mixes Capital with Revenue:** Buying `$5,500` of new equipment and receiving `$1,000` from selling the old ball machine are **Capital items** (Balance Sheet/Disposal), NOT annual operating income/expenditure!
2. **Mixes Multiple Years’ Subscriptions:** The `$16,400` includes `$600` earned in 2025 and `$1,000` prepaid for 2027 — ONLY `$14,800` (+ any unpaid 2026 arrears) belongs to 2026!
3. **Ignores Prepayments &amp; Accruals:** Court Rent includes `$800` prepaid for 2027 (must be deducted), while `$300` of unpaid December electricity (must be added) is missing!
4. **Omits Non-Cash Expenses:** Equipment Depreciation (`$1,200`) is omitted from R&amp;P yet MUST be charged in Income &amp; Expenditure!

</WorkedExample>

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Receipts &amp; Payments = Cash Book Summary:** ALL cash in/out (**Capital + Revenue**, **any year**); **ZERO** Depreciation or Accruals!
- **Closing Balance of R&amp;P** = **Closing Cash / Bank** (NEVER Surplus!).
:::
