:::info Essential Understanding
Preparing the final accounts of a **Co-operative Society** is 90% identical to a Limited Liability Company — with **four distinct Co-op superpowers**! You replace *"Net Profit"* with **"Net Surplus"**, calculate the **Statutory Reserve (`20%–25%`)** and **Education Fund (`5%`)** strictly on **THIS YEAR’S Net Surplus**, reward active members with a **Patronage Refund** (on member purchases/usage) alongside a **capped Share Dividend**, and carry any unpaid member payouts to **Current Liabilities** on a balanced Co-op Balance Sheet!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | **Co-op Trading &amp; Income Statement** → **Appropriation of Surplus Account** *(Statutory Reserve, Education Fund, Share Dividend, Patronage Refund, Honorarium)* → **Co-op Balance Sheet** |
| **Exam Focus** | Calculating **Statutory Reserve &amp; Education Fund `%` on CURRENT-YEAR Net Surplus ONLY** *(NOT including Undistributed Surplus b/f!)*, adding transfers onto opening reserves, and placing unpaid **Dividends + Patronage Refunds + Honoraria** under **Current Liabilities** |
| **Learning Outcome** | Construct a complete Co-operative Income Statement, Appropriation of Surplus Account, and balanced Vertical Balance Sheet accurately |

## 1. The Co-op Appropriation of Surplus Order (Memorize This!)

<FormulaCard
  title="Exact Co-op Appropriation Calculation Rules"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Always check WHAT base each percentage multiplies — this is where 90% of marks are won!"
>

| Appropriation Item | Exact Calculation Base | Balance Sheet Placement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Statutory Reserve Fund** | Statutory `%` *(e.g. `25%`)* × **CURRENT-YEAR Net Surplus ONLY!** | **ADDED to Opening Statutory Reserve** *(Members’ Equity!)* |
| **2. Education Fund** | Bylaw `%` *(e.g. `5%`)* × **CURRENT-YEAR Net Surplus ONLY!** | **ADDED to Opening Education Fund** *(Equity Reserve or Fund)* |
| **3. Dividend / Interest on Shares** | Capped `%` *(e.g. `6%`)* × **Issued Members’ Share Capital** | **Current Liability** *(if proposed/unpaid!)* |
| **4. Patronage Refund *(Bonus)*** | Rebate `%` *(e.g. `5%`)* × **Total Member Qualifying Purchases / Sales** | **Current Liability** *(if proposed/unpaid!)* |
| **5. Committee Honorarium** | Fixed gift/stipend approved at AGM for volunteer officers | **Current Liability** *(if unpaid!)* |

</FormulaCard>

:::warning #1 Co-op Exam Trap — Statutory Reserve Base!
Suppose **Current-Year Net Surplus = `$30,000`** and **Undistributed Surplus b/f = `$5,000`** (Total Available = `$35,000`), and the law requires **`25%` to Statutory Reserve**:
- **CORRECT:** `25% × $30,000` *(this year’s Net Surplus ONLY!)* = **`$7,500`**!
- **WRONG:** `25% × $35,000` = `$8,750` *(NEVER tax last year’s leftover surplus a second time — it already paid its Statutory Reserve last year!)*
:::

- **Statutory Reserve &amp; Education Fund `%`** = Multiply by **CURRENT-YEAR Net Surplus ONLY**!
