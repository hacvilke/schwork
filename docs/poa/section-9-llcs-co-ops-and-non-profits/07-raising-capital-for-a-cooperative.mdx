:::info Essential Understanding
Because Co-operative Societies **cannot sell shares on the Stock Exchange** and **cap dividends on shares**, they fund their operations through a distinctive mix of **Members’ Share Capital (redeemable at par)**, **Statutory & Education Reserves** (built up from annual surplus!), **Entrance Fees**, **Member Savings Deposits** *(in Credit Unions)*, and **Development Loans or Grants**! Knowing which source is **Members’ Equity** and which is a **Liability** guarantees clean Co-op Balance Sheets!
:::

| **Learning Outcome** | Identify how Co-operative Societies raise internal and external capital, record member share transactions, and classify each funding source accurately on the Balance Sheet |

## 1. Internal vs. External Capital Sources of a Co-operative

| Capital Source | Classification on Balance Sheet | Key Characteristics &amp; Legal Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Members’ Share Capital** | **Members’ Equity** | Bought directly from the Co-op at **par value**; **capped per member** (e.g., max `20%` of total shares) so no one dominates; **withdrawable / redeemable at par** when a member leaves! |
| **Statutory Reserve Fund** | **Members’ Equity *(Capital/Statutory Reserve)*** | Required by Co-op law (typically `20%–25%` of annual Net Surplus every year); **INDIVISIBLE / NON-DISTRIBUTABLE** — protects creditors &amp; stabilizes the society permanently! |
| **Education / General Reserves** | **Members’ Equity *(Reserves)*** | Earmarked out of annual surplus for member training (Education Fund) or future expansion (General/Building Reserve). |
| **Entrance Fees** | **Income / Statutory Reserve** | Small one-time, **non-refundable** registration fee paid when joining — covers onboarding paperwork or goes straight into the Statutory Reserve! |
| **Members’ Savings Deposits** *(Credit Unions)* | **Liability** *(Current / Non-Current!)* | Savings held by members that can be withdrawn on demand/notice — earns deposit interest and is a **LIABILITY** owed back to members (NOT Share Capital!). |
| **Loans &amp; Grants** | **Loans = Liability**; **Capital Grants = Reserve/Equity** | Low-interest loans from Co-op Credit Leagues, agricultural banks, or government development agencies. |

### Why Is Co-op Share Capital "Variable" (Unlike an LLC)?
- In a **Limited Liability Company**, Issued Share Capital is **permanent** — if you want your cash back, you must sell your shares to *another investor* (the company keeps its cash!).
- In a **Co-operative Society**, new members join continuously (**raising** Share Capital), and departing members can **redeem (withdraw)** their shares directly from the Co-op at par value (**reducing** Share Capital: `Dr Members’ Share Capital / Cr Bank`)!
