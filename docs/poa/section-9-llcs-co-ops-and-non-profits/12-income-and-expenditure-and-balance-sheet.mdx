:::info Essential Understanding
Welcome to the **Section 9 Grand Finale — Full Non-Profit Final Accounts**! Every complete club/charity Paper 2 problem follows ONE unbreakable **4-Step Assembly Line**: **(1) Find Opening Accumulated Fund** via a Statement of Affairs (`Opening Assets − Opening Liabilities`), **(2) Run Feeder Accounts** (*Subscriptions, Bar Trading, Fundraising*), **(3) Build the Accrual-Basis Income &amp; Expenditure Account** (`Income − Expenditure = Surplus / Deficit`), and **(4) Prove the Balance Sheet balances (`Total Net Assets = Opening Accumulated Fund + Surplus`)** to the exact cent!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Full **4-Step Non-Profit Pipeline**: Opening Statement of Affairs (`Accumulated Fund`) → Feeder Accounts → **Income &amp; Expenditure Account** → **Balanced Non-Profit Balance Sheet** |
| **Exam Focus** | Never omitting **Opening Bank, Opening Inventory, Opening Subs Arrears/Advances, or Opening Payables/Accruals** when computing **Opening Accumulated Fund**, excluding **Capital asset purchases** from I&amp;E, and updating **Closing Bank** &amp; **Equipment NBV** cleanly |
| **Learning Outcome** | Construct a complete, balanced set of final accounts for a Non-Profit Organization from opening balances and a Receipts &amp; Payments summary |

## 1. The 4-Step Non-Profit Final Accounts Master Formula

<FormulaCard
  title="Opening → Closing Accumulated Fund Bridge"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Just like Single Entry (Section 7), the Opening Statement of Affairs unlocks the entire Balance Sheet!"
>

$$
\text{Opening Accumulated Fund} = \text{Total Opening Assets (1 Jan)} - \text{Total Opening Liabilities (1 Jan)}
$$

$$
\text{Closing Accumulated Fund (31 Dec)} = \text{Opening Accumulated Fund} + \text{Surplus} \text{ (or } - \text{Deficit)}
$$

$$
\textbf{Balance Sheet Proof:}\quad \text{Total Net Assets (31 Dec)} = \text{Closing Accumulated Fund} \text{ (+ Specific Funds / Loans)}
$$

</FormulaCard>

### Five Checklist Traps That Guarantee a First-Try Balance!
1. **Include Opening Bank &amp; Opening Subs Arrears/Advance in Opening Accumulated Fund!** *(Missing just ONE opening item throws your final Balance Sheet off by that exact amount!)*
2. **Exclude Capital Equipment Purchases (`$8,000`) from Income &amp; Expenditure!** Instead, **ADD** `$8,000` to Opening Equipment NBV (`$16,000 + $8,000 = $24,000`), charge Depreciation (`$3,600`) in I&amp;E, and show Closing NBV (`$20,400`) on the Balance Sheet!
3. **Adjust Operating Expenses for BOTH Opening &amp; Closing Accruals/Prepayments!** E.g., `Electricity Paid ($3,200) − Opening Accrued ($400) + Closing Accrued ($600) = $3,400` in I&amp;E!
4. **Never Double-Count Bar Wages or Tournament Costs!** If deducted inside the Bar Trading or Tournament Account, do NOT put them again in I&amp;E Expenditure!
5. **Check EVERY Closing Current Asset &amp; Liability:**
   - **Closing Current Assets:** Bar Inventory + **Subscriptions in Arrears** + Prepaid Expenses + **Closing Bank**!
   - **Closing Current Liabilities:** Bar Trade Payables + **Subscriptions in Advance** + Accrued Expenses!

### Step 1: Statement of Affairs as at 1 January 2026 *(Opening Accumulated Fund — 3 marks)*
- **Opening Assets:** Clubhouse `$60,000` + Sports Equipment `$16,000` + Bar Inventory `$3,000` + Subscriptions in Arrears `$800` + Opening Bank `$5,200` = **`$85,000`**
- **Less Opening Liabilities:** Bar Trade Payables `$2,400` + Subscriptions in Advance `$600` + Electricity Accrued `$400` = **`($3,400)`**
- **Opening Accumulated Fund (1 Jan 2026)** = `$85,000 − $3,400` = **`$81,600`**!

---

### Step 2: Feeder Accounts *(Workings W1 & W2 — 5 marks)*
- **W1 — Subscriptions for 2026:**
  `Cash Received ($22,000) − Open Arrears ($800) + Open Advance ($600) + Close Arrears ($1,300) − Close Advance ($900)` = **`$22,200`** *(2 marks)*
- **W2 — Bar Trading Account (2026):** *(3 marks)*
  - Bar Credit Purchases = `$19,400 Paid + $3,000 Close Payables − $2,400 Open Payables` = **`$20,000`**
  - Bar COGS = `$3,000 Open Stock + $20,000 Purchases − $3,600 Close Stock` = **`$19,400`**
  - Bar Gross Profit = `$38,000 Sales − $19,400 COGS` = `$18,600` − Barman’s Wages `$6,200` = **Net Bar Profit = `$12,400`**!

:::tip Section 9 Complete — Quick Exam Checklist!
- **Limited Liability Companies:** Authorized = Memo ONLY; Debenture Interest + Tax BEFORE Appropriation; Interim = Approp. only; Proposed = Approp. + CL!
- **Co-operative Societies:** `1 Member = 1 Vote`; Statutory &amp; Education `%` on **CURRENT-YEAR Net Surplus ONLY**; Patronage Refund on Member Purchases!
- **Non-Profit Organizations:** `Opening Accumulated Fund = Opening Assets − Opening Liabilities`; **R&amp;P** = Cash Basis; **I&amp;E** = Accrual Basis (`Surplus / Deficit`); **Closing Accumulated Fund = Opening + Surplus** = **Total Net Assets**!
:::
