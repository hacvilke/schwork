:::info Essential Understanding
Recording the issue of **Ordinary Shares**, **Preference Shares**, and **Debentures** in the General Journal is refreshingly clean once you memorize ONE golden law: **Share Capital Accounts are ALWAYS credited at PAR (Nominal) value!** If investors pay MORE than par value *(say `$2.60` for a `$2.00` share)*, the extra `$0.60` per share is credited separately to a **Capital Reserve** called **Share Premium**!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Journal &amp; Ledger entries for issuing **Shares at Par**, **Shares at a Premium** (`Share Premium`), **Debentures**, and **Accrued/Paid Debenture Interest** |
| **Exam Focus** | Never journalizing **Authorized Capital**, splitting **Par Value vs. Share Premium** cleanly, and recording the **2-step Application Account** flow when requested |
| **Learning Outcome** | Prepare accurate General Journal entries, ledger accounts, and Balance Sheet equity extracts for any share or debenture issue |

## 1. The Four Core Capital-Raising Journal Templates

<FormulaCard
  title="Share & Debenture Issue Journal Templates (Memorize All 4!)"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Bank receives FULL cash collected; Share Capital gets ONLY Par Value; any excess above Par goes to Share Premium!"
>

| Transaction | Debit (`Dr`) | Credit (`Cr`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Shares Issued at PAR** *(Direct)* | `Bank` *(Shares × Par)* | `Ordinary (or Preference) Share Capital` *(Shares × Par)* |
| **2. Shares Issued at PAR** *(2-Step via Application)* | (a) `Bank`<br/>(b) `Share Application` | (a) `Share Application`<br/>(b) `Ordinary / Pref Share Capital` |
| **3. Shares Issued at a PREMIUM** *(e.g. `$2.00` par sold at `$2.60`)* | `Bank` *(Shares × Issue Price `$2.60`)* | `Ordinary Share Capital` *(Shares × Par `$2.00`)*<br/>**`Share Premium`** *(Shares × `$0.60` excess!)* |
| **4. Issuing Debentures** *(e.g. `$60,000` `8%` Debentures)* | `Bank` *(`$60,000`)* | **`8% Debentures`** *(`$60,000` — Non-Current Liability!)* |

</FormulaCard>

### What Is Share Premium & Can It Pay Dividends?
- **Share Premium** is the extra cash above nominal (par) value paid by investors who eagerly want shares in a strong, reputable company.
- It is classified as a **Capital Reserve** (inside Shareholders’ Equity on the Balance Sheet) — and by law it **CANNOT be distributed to shareholders as cash dividends**! *(It can be used to issue bonus shares or write off preliminary/share-issue expenses.)*

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Share Capital A/c** = **ALWAYS Par Value**; excess above Par = **`Share Premium`** (Capital Reserve).
- **NEVER** journalize **Authorized Capital**!
- **Debentures** = **Non-Current Liability**; full-year **Debenture Interest** = **Income Statement Expense**!
:::
