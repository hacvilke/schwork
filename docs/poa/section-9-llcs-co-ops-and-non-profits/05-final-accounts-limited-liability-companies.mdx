:::info Essential Understanding
You are now ready for the **Limited Liability Company Final Accounts Capstone**! Just like Partnerships, the entire Trading &amp; Operating section and the top half of the Balance Sheet stay right in your comfort zone — you simply combine the **Income Statement (with Debenture Interest & Tax)**, the **LLC Appropriation Account**, and the **Corporate Balance Sheet ("Financed By": Authorized Memo, Issued Share Capital, Capital & Revenue Reserves, and Debentures)** into one seamless, balanced financial package!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Full 3-part **LLC Final Accounts Package**: Income Statement → Appropriation Account → Vertical Classified Balance Sheet (`Total Net Assets = Total Capital Employed`) |
| **Exam Focus** | Ruling a **double-underline below Authorized Capital** so it is NEVER summed, updating **General Reserve (`Opening + Transfer`)**, listing **Proposed Dividends + Accrued Debenture Interest + Tax** under Current Liabilities, and keeping **Debentures under Non-Current Liabilities** |
| **Learning Outcome** | Construct a complete, balanced set of Limited Liability Company final accounts from a balanced Trial Balance and year-end notes |

## 1. The LLC Balance Sheet "Financed By" Blueprint

Look at the exact sequence examiners expect in the bottom half of every Limited Liability Company Balance Sheet:

| Section | Exact Line Items Included | Added to Totals? |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Authorized Share Capital** | E.g. `400,000` Ordinary/Pref Shares — **double-underlined immediately!** | **NO! (Memo ONLY)** |
| **Issued &amp; Paid-Up Share Capital** | Ordinary Share Capital + Preference Share Capital | **YES** |
| **Capital Reserves** | Share Premium + Revaluation Reserve | **YES** |
| **Revenue Reserves** | **Closing General Reserve** (`Opening + Transfer`) + **Retained Earnings c/f** | **YES** |
| **= Shareholders’ Funds *(Total Equity)*** | Sum of Issued Share Capital + All Reserves | *(Subtotal)* |
| **Non-Current Liabilities** | `8%` Debentures + Long-Term Bank Loans / Mortgages | **YES** |
| **= Total Capital Employed** | **Shareholders’ Funds + Non-Current Liabilities** *(MUST = Total Net Assets!)* | **GRAND TOTAL** |

## 5. True or False Check

<TrueFalseQuiz
  title="True or False: Limited Liability Company Final Accounts"
  items={[
    {

      explanation: 'Yes — always rule a double line right under Authorized Share Capital so you never accidentally add it into Issued Capital!',
    },
    {
      statement: 'When a company transfers $10,000 to its General Reserve at year-end, the Balance Sheet shows ONLY $10,000 (not the opening General Reserve).',

    {

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Authorized Capital** = **Memo ONLY** (double-underline, NEVER add!).
- **Shareholders’ Funds** = `Issued Share Capital + Capital Reserves + Revenue Reserves`.
- **Total Capital Employed** = `Shareholders’ Funds + Debentures/Non-Current Liabilities` = **`Total Net Assets`**!
:::
