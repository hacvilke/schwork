## 1. How a Factory's Accounts Differ From a Retail Shop's Accounts

A retail store buys finished goods from wholesalers and resells them immediately. A **manufacturing firm** (like Purity Bakery, a garment factory, or a Jamaican jam processor) buys **raw materials**, runs factory machines and workers, and converts them into **finished products**!

Because production is happening on the factory floor while sales are happening in the showroom, a manufacturer prepares an **extra statement BEFORE the Trading Account** called the **Manufacturing Account** — and tracks **THREE separate types of Stock**:

1. **Stock of Raw Materials** *(unprocessed ingredients — e.g. fruit, sugar, jars, timber)* → goes in the **Manufacturing Account** (and Current Assets on Balance Sheet).
2. **Stock of Work in Progress (WIP)** *(partially finished goods still on the factory assembly line at year-end)* → goes in the **Manufacturing Account** (and Current Assets on Balance Sheet).
3. **Stock of Finished Goods** *(completed products ready for sale)* → goes in the **Trading Account** (and Current Assets on Balance Sheet).

## 2. Classifying Manufacturing Costs (Direct vs Indirect vs Non-Factory!)

Every single cost in a manufacturing business fits into **one of three buckets** — mixing up Factory Overheads with Office/Selling Expenses is the #1 mistake on Paper 2!

| Cost Category | Plain-English Definition | Classic Exam Examples | Exact Statement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Direct Costs** *(Sum = **Prime Cost**)* | Costs that can be traced **directly and economically** to each finished unit made. | • **Direct Materials** (Raw Materials Consumed, including Carriage Inwards on Raw Materials)<br/>• **Direct Labour** (factory machine operators, bakers, tailors — hands-on makers!)<br/>• **Direct Expenses** (**Royalties** per unit, special machine hire for one product, patent fees) | **Top Half of Manufacturing Account** → **Prime Cost** |
| **2. Indirect Factory Costs** *(**Factory Overheads**)* | General costs of running the **factory building and machinery** that benefit production overall but cannot be tied to one single unit. | • Factory rent, rates & factory insurance<br/>• Factory electricity, power & fuel<br/>• **Indirect factory wages** (supervisors, cleaners, security guards, storekeepers)<br/>• Factory machinery repairs, lubricants & **Depreciation of Factory Machinery** | **Bottom Half of Manufacturing Account** *(added to Prime Cost)* |
| **3. Non-Factory Expenses** *(Office, Admin, Selling & Finance)* | Costs of running the **administrative office**, marketing, and delivering sold goods to customers. | Office salaries, office rent, stationery, advertising, sales staff commission, **Carriage Outwards**, Bad Debts, **Depreciation of Office Equipment & Delivery Vans**, loan interest. | **Profit & Loss Account ONLY** *(NEVER put inside the Manufacturing Account!)* |

### Apportioning Shared Costs Between Factory & Office (Common Exam Skill!)
Frequently, one building houses *both* the factory and the administrative office, so one bill covers both (e.g. *Total Rent = J\$40,000, split 80% Factory / 20% Office*; or *Electricity J\$24,000, split 3:1 Factory to Office*):
- Put the **Factory share** ($80\% \times 40{,}000 =$ **J\$32,000**) inside **Factory Overheads** in the Manufacturing Account.
- Put the **Office share** ($20\% \times 40{,}000 =$ **J\$8,000**) under **Operating Expenses** in the Profit & Loss Account!

## 3. Core Manufacturing & Costing Formulae

<FormulaHint

$$
\text{Raw Materials Consumed} = \text{Opening RM} + (\text{Purchases of RM} - \text{Returns Out}) + \text{Carriage Inwards} - \text{Closing RM}
$$

</FormulaHint>

<FormulaHint
  block
  label="2. Prime Cost & Cost of Production"
  short="Prime Cost adds all Direct Costs; Cost of Production adds Factory Overheads plus Opening WIP minus Closing WIP!"
>

$$
\text{Prime Cost} = \text{Raw Materials Consumed} + \text{Direct Labour} + \text{Direct Expenses}
$$

$$
\text{Cost of Production} = \text{Prime Cost} + \text{Factory Overheads} + \text{Opening WIP} - \text{Closing WIP}
$$

</FormulaHint>

<FormulaHint
  block
  label="3. Unit Cost & Cost-Plus (Mark-Up) Pricing"
  short="Divide total Cost of Production by total completed units to get Unit Cost (absorption cost per unit), then add your mark-up % to find the Selling Price!"
>

$$
\text{Unit Cost} = \frac{\text{Cost of Production}}{\text{Number of Units Produced}} \qquad \text{Selling Price} = \text{Unit Cost} + (\text{Mark-Up \%} \times \text{Unit Cost})
$$

</FormulaHint>

</FormulaCard>

### Basic Costing Principles (Syllabus Objective 10.5!)
- **Absorption Costing:** Charging **both** Direct Costs AND a fair share of Factory Overheads into the total cost of every unit produced (that is literally what our Manufacturing Account computes!).
- **Cost-Plus / Mark-Up Pricing:** Adding a chosen percentage profit (**Mark-Up %**) on top of **Unit Cost** to guarantee every sale covers production costs and leaves profit! *(Careful: **Mark-Up** is a % of **Cost**, whereas **Margin** is a % of **Selling Price**!)*

### (a) Manufacturing Account for the year ended 31 December 2025

| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |

| **Cost of Raw Materials Consumed** | | | **200,000** |
| **Add: Direct Labour & Direct Expenses** | | | |
| Direct Factory Wages | | 110,000 | |
| Royalties *(Direct Expense)* | | 10,000 | 120,000 |
| **PRIME COST** | | | **320,000** |
| | | | |
| **ADD: FACTORY OVERHEADS** | | | |
| Factory Rent *(80% × 40,000)* | | 32,000 | |
| Factory Power & Fuel | | 18,000 | |
| Indirect Factory Wages | | 24,000 | |

| *Total Manufacturing Cost* | | | *410,000* |

### (b) Unit Cost & Cost-Plus Selling Price
- **Unit Cost of Production:** $\frac{400{,}000}{20{,}000} =$ **J\$20.00 per jar**
- **Selling Price (25% Mark-Up on Cost):** $20.00 + (25\% \times 20.00) = 20.00 + 5.00 =$ **J\$25.00 per jar**

### (c) Trading & Profit & Loss Extract + Balance Sheet Stock Extract
- **Trading Account:** Sales **J\$500,000** − Cost of Sales (`Opening Finished Goods 30,000 + Cost of Production 400,000 − Closing Finished Goods 50,000 = 380,000`) = **Gross Profit J\$120,000**
- **Profit & Loss Expenses:** Office Salaries **J\$48,000** + Office Rent *(20% × 40,000)* **J\$8,000** = **J\$56,000** → **Net Profit = J\$64,000**!
- **Balance Sheet — Current Assets (Closing Stock Section):** Raw Materials **J\$40,000** + Work in Progress **J\$25,000** + Finished Goods **J\$50,000** = **Total Closing Stock J\$115,000**!
