:::info Essential Understanding
Why do accountants NEVER credit the **Machinery Account** directly when recording depreciation? Because the **Historical Cost Concept** says we should always be able to see what the asset **originally cost**! Instead, accountants create a companion **Contra-Asset account** called **Provision for Depreciation (Accumulated Depreciation)** — keeping the Asset Account untouched at original cost while accumulating all past depreciation charges neatly on the Credit side.
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | **Provision for Depreciation Account** (Credit balance / Contra-Asset), **Annual Adjusting Journal Entry**, **Income Statement** (THIS year’s charge ONLY) vs. **Balance Sheet** (TOTAL Accumulated Depreciation) |
| **Exam Focus** | Constructing multi-year Asset and Provision for Depreciation T-accounts and NEVER confusing annual depreciation expense with total accumulated depreciation |
| **Learning Outcome** | Record journal entries, post multi-year Provision for Depreciation ledger accounts, and display Cost, Accumulated Depreciation, and NBV accurately on the Balance Sheet |

## 1. The Three Accounts Behind Every Depreciating Asset

Every depreciating Non-Current Asset uses a clean 3-account system:

| Ledger Account | Type &amp; Normal Balance | What Goes Inside It? | Where Does It Go at Year-End? |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Asset Account** *(e.g. Machinery)* | **Asset (Debit)** | Original cost of all assets owned (plus new purchases at cost) | **Balance Sheet → Cost Column** |
| **2. Provision for Depreciation A/c** | **Contra-Asset (Credit)** | **Running total** of EVERY year’s depreciation since purchase (`Bal b/d` + this year’s charge) | **Balance Sheet → Accum. Depr. Column** *(subtracted from Cost!)* |
| **3. Depreciation Expense A/c** *(or direct to Income Stmt)* | **Expense (Debit)** | **ONLY this year’s** depreciation charge | **Income Statement → Operating Expenses** *(closed to $0 every year!)* |

<FormulaCard
  title="Standard Annual Depreciation Journal Entry (Memorize!)"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Debit the Expense (Income Statement) and Credit the Provision for Depreciation (Contra-Asset) — NEVER touch the Asset Account!"
>

**Two-Step Style:**
1. `Dr Depreciation Expense` — *This year’s charge*
   `Cr Provision for Depreciation — [Asset]` — *Adds to cumulative depreciation*
2. `Dr Income Statement` — *Closes annual expense to profit*
   `Cr Depreciation Expense`

**Combined Direct Style *(Most Common on Paper 2)*:**
- **`Dr Income Statement (Depreciation — [Asset])`**
- **`Cr Provision for Depreciation — [Asset]`**

</FormulaCard>

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Asset A/c** = ALWAYS at **Original Cost** (`Dr`).
- **Provision for Depreciation A/c** = ALWAYS a **Contra-Asset (`Cr`)** holding cumulative depreciation.
- **Income Statement** = **THIS YEAR ONLY**; **Balance Sheet** = **TOTAL ACCUMULATED**!
:::
