## 1. The Golden "ONCE vs. TWICE" Exam Method

Before writing a single line of your Income Statement on exam day, spend **90 seconds** marking up the question paper with a pencil:
1. **Items INSIDE the Trial Balance → Use ONCE!**
   - Put **`IS`** beside revenues, purchases, opening inventory, returns, carriage, and expenses.
   - Put **`BS`** beside fixed assets, accumulated depreciation, receivables, bank/cash, payables, loans, capital, and drawings.
   - *(Note: Old Provision for Doubtful Debts &amp; Opening Accumulated Depreciation are used as comparison/starting points for the adjustments!)*
2. **Items in the ADDITIONAL NOTES BELOW the Trial Balance → Use TWICE! (`✓ IS` + `✓ BS`)**
   - Because footnote adjustments have **NOT yet been double-entered** into the Trial Balance, YOU must enter BOTH sides of the double entry — once in the **Income Statement** and once in the **Balance Sheet**!
   - Put two tiny check-boxes `[ ][ ]` beside every note. Tick box 1 when you post it to the Income Statement, and tick box 2 when you post it to the Balance Sheet!

### Step 0: Quick Adjustment Workings *(Always show these!)*
- **Adjusted Electricity:** `$3,400 + $600 accrued` = **$4,000** *(IS Exp; BS CL $600)*
- **Adjusted Insurance:** `$2,800 − $400 prepaid` = **$2,400** *(IS Exp; BS CA $400)*
- **Adjusted Rent Received:** `$4,400 − $400 advance` = **$4,000** *(IS Other Income; BS CL $400)*
- **Bad &amp; Doubtful Debts:** Total Bad Debts = `$400 + $600 = $1,000` (IS Exp). Remaining Receivables = `$16,600 − $600 = $16,000` → New 5% Provision = **$800** *(BS deduct $800 → Net AR $15,200)*; Change = `$800 − $500` = **+$300 Increase** *(IS Exp)*.
- **Depreciation:**
  - *Delivery Van (20% RB):* `20% × ($30,000 − $6,000) = 20% × $24,000` = **$4,800** *(IS Exp; New Accum. Depr. = `$6,000 + $4,800 = $10,800`)*
  - *Shop Fittings (10% SL):* `10% × $15,000` = **$1,500** *(IS Exp; New Accum. Depr. = `$3,000 + $1,500 = $4,500`)*

## 5. True or False Check

  title="True or False: Full Adjusted Final Statements"

    },

      explanation: 'Because the $400 inside the Trial Balance was ALREADY subtracted from Receivables earlier in the year — only the additional $600 needs to be subtracted now!',
