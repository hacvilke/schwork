:::info Essential Understanding
Once you understand the Accrual Concept, examiners expect you to **write the General Journal entries** and **construct the Expense and Revenue T-Accounts** — complete with **opening balances (`Bal b/d`)**, **cash paid/received (`Bank`)**, **year-end transfers to the Income Statement**, and **closing balances (`Bal c/d` → `Bal b/d`)**! Master the 4 quadrants once, and every single ledger question becomes automatic.
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Double-entry journal entries &amp; full T-accounts for **Accrued Expenses**, **Prepaid Expenses**, **Accrued Revenues**, and **Unearned (Prepaid) Revenues** |
| **Exam Focus** | Knowing WHICH side `Bal b/d` starts on (Asset = Debit `b/d`; Liability = Credit `b/d`), computing the exact transfer to the **Income Statement**, and bringing down the new year’s opening balance cleanly |
| **Learning Outcome** | Record journal entries and close off Expense and Revenue ledger accounts accurately when BOTH opening and closing accruals/prepayments exist |

## 1. The Master Side-Placement Cheat Table (Never Guess Again!)

Remember ONE unbreakable rule from Section 2 &amp; Section 4:
- Every **Asset** starts the year with a **DEBIT `Bal b/d`** *(so its closing `Bal c/d` sits on the **Credit** side!)*
- Every **Liability** starts the year with a **CREDIT `Bal b/d`** *(so its closing `Bal c/d` sits on the **Debit** side!)*

| Adjustment Type | Asset or Liability? | Opening `Bal b/d` (Jan 1) | Closing `Bal c/d` (Dec 31) | Balance Sheet Placement |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Accrued Expense** *(owing)* | **Current Liability** | **Credit** | **Debit** *(b/d Cr next yr)* | **Current Liability** |
| **Prepaid Expense** *(advance)* | **Current Asset** | **Debit** | **Credit** *(b/d Dr next yr)* | **Current Asset** |
| **Accrued Revenue** *(owing to us)* | **Current Asset** | **Debit** | **Credit** *(b/d Dr next yr)* | **Current Asset** |
| **Unearned Revenue** *(paid early to us)* | **Current Liability** | **Credit** | **Debit** *(b/d Cr next yr)* | **Current Liability** |

<FormulaCard
  title="Quick Formula Check for Any Expense or Revenue Ledger Account"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Always double-check your T-account Income Statement transfer with this 1-line mental math!"
>

$$
\text{Expense to Income Stmt} = \text{Cash Paid} - \text{Opening Accrual} + \text{Closing Accrual} + \text{Opening Prepayment} - \text{Closing Prepayment}
$$

$$
\text{Revenue to Income Stmt} = \text{Cash Received} - \text{Opening Accrued Rev.} + \text{Closing Accrued Rev.} + \text{Opening Unearned Rev.} - \text{Closing Unearned Rev.}
$$

</FormulaCard>

| Date (Dr) | Details | $ | Date (Cr) | Details | $ |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | :--- | :--- | ---: |

| | | **4,400** | | | **4,400** |
| Jan 1, 2027 | Balance b/d *(Asset)* | 900 | | | |

### 3. Rent Receivable Account *(Revenue Account — 4 marks)*

| Date (Dr) | Details | $ | Date (Cr) | Details | $ |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | :--- | :--- | ---: |
| Dec 31, 2026 | **Income Statement** | **7,000** | Jan 1, 2026 | Balance b/d *(Advance)* | 500 |

## 5. True or False Check

<TrueFalseQuiz
  title="True or False: Prepayments & Accruals Ledger Accounts"
  items={[
    {
      statement: 'An opening Prepaid Expense balance on 1 January is entered on the DEBIT side of the Expense account.',
      answer: true,
      explanation: 'A Prepaid Expense is a Current Asset — and all Assets start with a Debit Balance b/d!',
    },
    {
      statement: 'An opening Accrued Expense balance on 1 January is entered on the DEBIT side of the Expense account.',
      answer: false,
      explanation: 'An Accrued Expense is a Current Liability, so its opening Balance b/d on 1 January sits on the CREDIT side!',
    },
    {
      statement: 'If $5,000 cash was paid for Wages, Opening Accrued Wages was $400, and Closing Accrued Wages is $700, the Wages Expense transferred to the Income Statement is $5,300.',
      answer: true,
      explanation: 'Subtract last year’s $400 and add this year’s unpaid $700: $5,000 − $400 + $700 = $5,300!',
    },
    {
      statement: 'Rent Received in Advance at 31 December appears as a Current Asset on the landlord’s Balance Sheet.',

  ]}

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Asset = Dr `b/d`** (*Prepaid Expense* &amp; *Accrued Revenue*).

:::
