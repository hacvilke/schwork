## 1. Bad Debts vs Provision for Doubtful Debts

Selling on credit helps businesses grow sales quickly — but it carries a real risk: **not every customer pays!** Businesses track overdue invoices using an **Aging of Debtors Schedule** (sorting unpaid bills into *0–30 days*, *31–60 days*, *61–90 days*, and *over 90 days* — the older a debt gets, the higher the risk it will never be collected!).

Accountants separate credit losses into **two distinct categories**:

| Feature | **Bad Debts** *(Certain Loss!)* | **Provision for Doubtful Debts** *(Estimated Future Loss!)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | Money owed by a specific debtor who **definitely CANNOT or WILL NOT pay** (e.g. declared bankrupt, shut down, died insolvent, disappeared, or cannot be traced). | A cautious **estimate** (normally a percentage of remaining good Debtors, e.g. **5%**) set aside for customers who **MIGHT fail to pay** in the future. |
| **Underlying Concepts** | **Prudence & Matching:** Recognise losses immediately and never show worthless debts as Assets! | **Prudence & Matching:** Never overstate Debtors or Net Profit; match estimated credit losses to the year of the credit sales! |
| **Double Entry** | 1. `Dr Bad Debts` / `Cr Customer Account` *(wipes out that debtor's account to J\$0!)*<br/>2. Year-end: `Dr Profit & Loss` / `Cr Bad Debts` | **Do NOT touch any individual customer's account!** Adjust the **Provision for Doubtful Debts Account** (Credit balance) against Profit & Loss! |

:::warning Crucial Exam Order Rule!
If a question requires you to **BOTH** write off a new Bad Debt (e.g. J\$4,000) **AND** calculate a Provision for Doubtful Debts (e.g. 5%):
- **ALWAYS subtract the new Bad Debt from Debtors FIRST**, and then multiply the percentage by the **remaining good Debtors**!
- *(Why? You already wrote the J\$4,000 off as 100% bad — you don't estimate 5% doubtful on a debt that is already gone!)*
:::

### Bonus: What If a "Bad Debt" Customer Unexpectedly Pays Months Later? (**Bad Debts Recovered**)
Occasionally, a debtor whose account you wrote off last year turns their finances around and sends a cheque! Record it in **two clean Journal steps** so their credit history is updated:
1. **Reinstate the debt:** `Dr Customer Account` / `Cr Bad Debts Recovered`
2. **Record the cheque received:** `Dr Bank (or Cash)` / `Cr Customer Account`
- At year-end, **Bad Debts Recovered** is closed to the **Profit & Loss Account under Add: Other Revenue** (increasing Net Profit!).

## 2. The Four Rules for Provision for Doubtful Debts (Memorise!)

This is one of the highest-yield topics in POA — master all four cases cleanly:

1. **Year 1 — Creating a Provision:** The entire new provision is an **Operating Expense** in the Profit & Loss Account (`Dr P&L`, `Cr Provision for Doubtful Debts`).
2. **INCREASING an Existing Provision** *(New Provision > Old Provision)*: ONLY the **INCREASE** (`New − Old`) is charged as an **Operating Expense** in the Profit & Loss Account (`Dr P&L`, `Cr Provision`).
3. **DECREASING an Existing Provision** *(New Provision < Old Provision)*: ONLY the **DECREASE** (`Old − New`) is treated as **Other Revenue (Added to Gross Profit)** in the Profit & Loss Account (`Dr Provision`, `Cr P&L`)!
4. **On the Balance Sheet (EVERY single year):** Always show `Debtors (after bad debts) − FULL Closing Provision = Net Debtors` under **Current Assets**!

## 3. Master Dual-Effect Cheat Sheet (ALL Section 6 Adjustments!)

Every single year-end adjustment in Section 6 follows the **Dual Aspect** rule — it hits **BOTH** the Profit & Loss Account **AND** the Balance Sheet! Keep this master map right beside you when preparing Adjusted Final Accounts:

| Year-End Adjustment | Effect in **Profit & Loss Account** | Effect on **Balance Sheet** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Accrued Expense** | **Add** to Expense | **Current Liability** |
| **Prepaid Expense** | **Subtract** from Expense | **Current Asset** |
| **Accrued Revenue** | **Add** to Revenue | **Current Asset** |
| **Prepaid Revenue** | **Subtract** from Revenue | **Current Liability** |
| **Depreciation (This Year)** | **Expense** *(this year's charge ONLY!)* | **Add to Accumulated Provision** → subtract from Fixed Asset Cost to get **NBV** |
| **Additional Bad Debt** *(note below TB)* | **Add** to Bad Debts Expense *(or new Expense)* | **Subtract** from Debtors under Current Assets |
| **Provision for Doubtful Debts** | **Increase** = Expense; **Decrease** = Add to Gross Profit (Other Revenue) | **Subtract FULL Closing Provision** from Debtors under Current Assets |
