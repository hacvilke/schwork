## 1. Bad Debts vs Doubtful Debts (Kid-Friendly Explanation!)

Whenever a business sells on credit, not every customer pays on time — and a few never pay at all! Businesses track overdue invoices using an **Aging of Debtors** schedule (30 days, 60 days, 90+ days overdue) and separate them into two clear categories:

| Term | Plain-English Meaning | What the Accountant Does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bad Debts** | Money owed by a debtor who **definitely CANNOT or WILL NOT pay** (e.g. went bankrupt, closed down, died insolvent, disappeared). | **Write it off completely!**<br/>1. `Dr Bad Debts` / `Cr Customer (Debtor)` *(closes their account to zero)*<br/>2. At year-end: `Dr Profit & Loss` / `Cr Bad Debts` *(Expense in P&L)* |
| **Provision for Doubtful Debts** | A cautious **estimate** (usually a percentage of remaining Debtors, e.g. **5%**) for customers who **MIGHT fail to pay** in the future *(applying the **Prudence** & **Matching** concepts!)*. | **Do NOT erase individual customer accounts!** Instead, maintain a **Provision for Doubtful Debts Account** (Credit balance) and subtract the full closing provision from Debtors on the Balance Sheet! |

:::warning Crucial Exam Order Rule!
If a question has **BOTH** a new Bad Debt to write off **AND** a Provision for Doubtful Debts percentage, **ALWAYS subtract the Bad Debt from Debtors FIRST**, then calculate the Provision percentage on the **remaining good Debtors**!
:::

## 2. How Changes in Provision for Doubtful Debts Hit the Profit & Loss Account

This is one of the **#1 Paper 1 & Paper 2 traps** — learn these three crystal-clear rules:

1. **Creating a Provision (Year 1):** Entire provision is an **Expense** in the Profit & Loss Account (`Dr P&L`, `Cr Provision for Doubtful Debts`).
2. **INCREASING an Existing Provision** *(New Provision > Old Provision)*: ONLY the **INCREASE** is charged as an **Expense** in the Profit & Loss Account (`Dr P&L`, `Cr Provision`).
3. **DECREASING an Existing Provision** *(New Provision < Old Provision)*: ONLY the **DECREASE** is treated as **Other Revenue / Add to Gross Profit** in the Profit & Loss Account (`Dr Provision`, `Cr P&L`)!
4. **On the Balance Sheet (EVERY year):** ALWAYS subtract the **FULL Closing Provision** from closing Debtors under Current Assets!
