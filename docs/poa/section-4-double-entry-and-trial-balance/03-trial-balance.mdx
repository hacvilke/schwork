## 1. What Is a Trial Balance?

A **Trial Balance** is a **two-column list of every ledger account's `Balance b/d`** (Debit column and Credit column) prepared on a specific date — normally at month-end or year-end.

**Two Key Uses:**
1. **Checks Arithmetical Accuracy:** Because every transaction had an equal Debit and Credit, the **Total Debits MUST equal Total Credits**. If they disagree, you know immediately that an arithmetic or posting mistake happened!
2. **Blueprint for Final Accounts:** It gathers every account balance onto one tidy sheet so you can quickly build the **Trading and Profit & Loss Account** and **Balance Sheet**!

## 3. Quick Cheat-Sheet: Which Column of the Trial Balance?

| Always in the **DEBIT** Column | Always in the **CREDIT** Column |
| :--- | :--- |
| All **Assets** (Premises, Machinery, Vans, Opening Stock, Debtors, Cash, Positive Bank) | **Capital** |
| **Purchases** & **Carriage Inwards** | **Sales** |
| **Returns Inwards** *(Sales Returns)* | **Returns Outwards** *(Purchases Returns)* |
| All **Expenses** (Rent, Wages, Electricity, Insurance, Carriage Outwards, Bad Debts, Depreciation) | All **Liabilities** (Creditors, **Bank Overdraft**, Bank Loan, Mortgage) |
| **Discount Allowed** | **Discount Received** & Other Incomes (Rent Received, Commission Received) |
| **Drawings** | **Provision for Depreciation** & **Provision for Doubtful Debts** |

:::warning Watch Out for Closing Stock!
**Opening Stock** appears inside the Trial Balance (Debit column), while **Closing Stock** is counted at year-end and normally appears **outside / underneath** the Trial Balance as a note!
:::

## 4. Limitations of the Trial Balance (Errors It CANNOT Catch!)

A balancing Trial Balance proves that **Total Debits = Total Credits** — it does **NOT** prove the books are 100% error-free! Six classic errors still allow the Trial Balance to balance:

| Error Type | Kid-Friendly Explanation & Example |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Error of Omission** | A transaction was completely left out of **both** accounts (forgot to record a J\$5,000 cash sale at all). |
| **2. Error of Commission** | Posted to the **wrong person's account** in the right class (credited *N. Lewis* instead of *M. Lewis*). |
| **3. Error of Principle** | Posted to the **wrong class of account** (debited a new photocopier to *Purchases* instead of *Office Equipment*). |
| **4. Error of Original Entry** | Wrote the **wrong number** on both sides from the start (recorded J\$615 as J\$651 in both Dr and Cr). |
| **5. Complete Reversal of Entries** | Put the Debit on the Credit side and the Credit on the Debit side (Dr Sales, Cr Cash for a cash sale). |
| **6. Compensating Errors** | Two unrelated mistakes of the exact same size cancel each other out (+J\$200 on Dr and +J\$200 on Cr). |
