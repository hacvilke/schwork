## 1. How a T-Account Works (Golden Posting Rule!)

A **T-Account** looks like a capital letter **`T`**:
- **Left side = Debit (Dr)**
- **Right side = Credit (Cr)**
- **Golden Labelling Rule:** Inside any account, write the **name of the OTHER (corresponding) account** in the `Details` column!
  - Example: When you buy goods on credit from **K. Ellis** for **J\$54,000**:
    - Inside **Purchases Account (Dr side):** write `May 2 | K. Ellis | 54,000`
    - Inside **K. Ellis Account (Cr side):** write `May 2 | Purchases | 54,000`

---

## 2. How to Balance Off an Account in 4 Easy Steps (`c/d` → `b/d`)

Examiners constantly report that students mix up **`Balance c/d`** (*carried down*) and **`Balance b/d`** (*brought down*). Follow these 4 steps and you will **never** miss a balancing mark again:

1. **Add up both sides** separately on rough paper and find which side is **bigger**.
2. Write the **bigger total** in the total box on **BOTH sides** (double-underlined!).
3. On the **SMALLER side** (right *above* the total line), write the missing difference as **`Balance c/d`** (*carried down* — closing this month).
4. On the **OPPOSITE (BIGGER) side** (*below* the double-underlined total, dated the **1st of the next month**), bring that exact same number down as **`Balance b/d`** (*brought down* — opening next month!).

:::tip Quick Check!
An account is named after the side where **`Balance b/d`** lands!
- **`Balance b/d` on the Debit side** = a **Debit Balance** *(Assets, Debtors, Expenses, Drawings)*.
- **`Balance b/d` on the Credit side** = a **Credit Balance** *(Liabilities, Creditors, Capital, Revenues)*.
:::

## 3. Worked Example: Complete Set of Balanced T-Accounts (*D. Fraser Books*)

Watch all eight transactions for **D. Fraser Books** in **May 2026** posted into neat T-accounts and balanced off at **31 May** — we will carry every single `Balance b/d` straight into the **Trial Balance** in Lesson 4.3!

| Date | Transaction | Account Debited (Dr) | Account Credited (Cr) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| May 1 | Owner D. Fraser put **J\$150,000** into the business bank | Bank *(Asset ↑)* | Capital *(Capital ↑)* |
| May 2 | Bought goods on credit from **K. Ellis** for **J\$54,000** | Purchases *(Expense ↑)* | K. Ellis *(Creditor ↑)* |
| May 5 | Bought Shop Fittings by cheque **J\$35,000** | Shop Fittings *(Asset ↑)* | Bank *(Asset ↓)* |
| May 9 | Sold goods on credit to **M. Gayle** for **J\$68,000** | M. Gayle *(Debtor ↑)* | Sales *(Revenue ↑)* |
| May 14 | Paid **K. Ellis** **J\$30,000** by cheque | K. Ellis *(Creditor ↓)* | Bank *(Asset ↓)* |
| May 18 | Received cheque **J\$42,000** from **M. Gayle** | Bank *(Asset ↑)* | M. Gayle *(Debtor ↓)* |
| May 24 | Paid shop rent by cheque **J\$18,000** | Rent *(Expense ↑)* | Bank *(Asset ↓)* |
| May 28 | Owner withdrew **J\$12,000** by cheque for private use | Drawings *(Drawings ↑)* | Bank *(Asset ↓)* |
