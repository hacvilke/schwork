## 1. Payroll Source Documents, Payment Methods & Software

Every business with staff must track hours worked accurately, deduct taxes legally, and pay workers on time!

- **Payroll Source Documents:**
  - **Time Cards / Clock-In Cards / Electronic Time Logs** — record exact hours worked (regular hours + overtime hours).
  - **Employee Earnings Record & Payslip (Pay Advice)** — tracks each worker's basic pay, overtime, bonuses, deductions, and net pay.
  - **Payroll Register / Wages Sheet** — summary table of all staff for a pay period.
- **Methods of Payment:**
  - *Employees:* Direct deposit (bank transfer — safest and fastest!), cheques, or cash envelopes.
  - *Suppliers & Goods/Services:* Cheques, Standing Orders, Direct Debits, Electronic Funds Transfer / Point of Sale (**EFTPOS**), online banking transfers, debit/credit cards, cash.
- **Common Payroll Accounting Software:** **QuickBooks**, **Sage / Peachtree Accounting**, **Microsoft Excel**, **AccountEdge** *(automatically computes tax tables, payslips, and statutory reports!)*.

## 2. Statutory Deductions vs Voluntary Deductions (Jamaican Context!)

| Type of Deduction | Plain-English Rule | Common Jamaican / Caribbean Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Statutory Deductions** | **Compulsory by law** — employers MUST deduct these and pay them over to the Government! | • **NIS** *(National Insurance Scheme / Social Security)*<br/>• **NHT** *(National Housing Trust)*<br/>• **Education Tax**<br/>• **Income Tax (PAYE — Pay As You Earn)** *(charged on taxable income above the tax-free threshold)* |
| **Voluntary (Non-Statutory) Deductions** | Deducted **ONLY if the employee requests / authorises** it in writing! | • **Credit Union savings / loan payments**<br/>• **Trade Union dues**<br/>• **Group Health / Life Insurance**<br/>• **Private Pension contributions** & staff social club dues |

## 3. Core Payroll Formulae (Hover or Tap to Simplify!)

<FormulaCard
  title="Gross Pay, Overtime & Net Pay Formulae"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Add Basic Pay plus Overtime (and any bonuses) to get Gross Pay, then subtract ALL Statutory and Voluntary Deductions to get take-home Net Pay!"
>

<FormulaHint
  block
  label="Gross Pay (Total Earnings Before Deductions)"
  short="Regular hours × Basic Rate plus Overtime hours × Overtime Rate (e.g. Time-and-a-half = 1.5× basic rate; Double-time = 2× basic rate)."
>

$$
\text{Gross Pay} = \text{Basic Pay} + \text{Overtime Pay} + \text{Bonuses / Allowances}
$$

</FormulaHint>

<FormulaHint
  block
  label="Net Pay (Take-Home Pay)"
  short="What the employee actually receives in their bank account after subtracting both compulsory (statutory) and chosen (voluntary) deductions."
>

$$
\text{Net Pay} = \text{Gross Pay} - (\text{Statutory Deductions} + \text{Voluntary Deductions})
$$

</FormulaHint>

</FormulaCard>

## 4. Worked Example: Complete Weekly Payroll Register

<WorkedExample
  title="Kingston Bakery Ltd — Weekly Payroll Sheet (Basic, Overtime, Deductions & Net Pay)"
  marks={12}
  problem={
    <>
      Prepare the Weekly Payroll Sheet for two employees of <strong>Kingston Bakery Ltd</strong> (normal working week = <strong>40 hours</strong>):
      <br />
      • <strong>K. Brown:</strong> 40 basic hrs @ <strong>J$1,000/hr</strong> + <strong>5 hrs double-time</strong> (2× basic rate). Voluntary deductions: Credit Union <strong>J$2,000</strong>; Trade Union dues <strong>J$500</strong>.
      <br />
      • <strong>D. Grant:</strong> 40 basic hrs @ <strong>J$800/hr</strong> + <strong>5 hrs time-and-a-half</strong> (1.5× basic rate). Voluntary deductions: Credit Union <strong>J$1,500</strong>; Group Health <strong>J$600</strong>.
      <br />
      <strong>Statutory Deduction Rates (given for this exercise):</strong> NIS = <strong>3% of Gross Pay</strong>; NHT = <strong>2% of Gross Pay</strong>; Income Tax (PAYE) = <strong>25% on Gross Pay above J$30,000</strong> weekly threshold.
    </>
  }
  examTip="Always check the PAYE Income Tax rule carefully! Do NOT multiply 25% by total Gross Pay if a tax-free threshold is given — subtract the tax-free threshold (J$30,000) FIRST, then take 25% of the remaining taxable amount!"
>

### Step-by-Step Workings
- **K. Brown:**
  - Basic = $40 \times 1{,}000 =$ **J\$40,000** | Overtime = $5 \times (2 \times 1{,}000) = 5 \times 2{,}000 =$ **J\$10,000** → **Gross Pay = J\$50,000**
  - Statutory: NIS ($3\% \times 50{,}000 = 1{,}500$) + NHT ($2\% \times 50{,}000 = 1{,}000$) + PAYE ($25\% \times (50{,}000 - 30{,}000) = 25\% \times 20{,}000 = 5{,}000$) = **J\$7,500**
  - Voluntary: $2{,}000 + 500 =$ **J\$2,500** → Total Deductions = **J\$10,000** → **Net Pay = J\$40,000**
- **D. Grant:**
  - Basic = $40 \times 800 =$ **J\$32,000** | Overtime = $5 \times (1.5 \times 800) = 5 \times 1{,}200 =$ **J\$6,000** → **Gross Pay = J\$38,000**
  - Statutory: NIS ($3\% \times 38{,}000 = 1{,}140$) + NHT ($2\% \times 38{,}000 = 760$) + PAYE ($25\% \times (38{,}000 - 30{,}000) = 25\% \times 8{,}000 = 2{,}000$) = **J\$3,900**
  - Voluntary: $1{,}500 + 600 =$ **J\$2,100** → Total Deductions = **J\$6,000** → **Net Pay = J\$32,000**

### Kingston Bakery Ltd — Weekly Payroll Register

| Employee | Basic (J\$) | Overtime (J\$) | **Gross Pay (J\$)** | NIS 3% | NHT 2% | PAYE 25% | Voluntary (J\$) | Total Ded. (J\$) | **Net Pay (J\$)** |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| K. Brown | 40,000 | 10,000 | **50,000** | 1,500 | 1,000 | 5,000 | 2,500 | 10,000 | **40,000** |
| D. Grant | 32,000 | 6,000 | **38,000** | 1,140 | 760 | 2,000 | 2,100 | 6,000 | **32,000** |
| **TOTALS** | **72,000** | **16,000** | **88,000** | **2,640** | **1,760** | **7,000** | **4,600** | **16,000** | **72,000** |

*(Cross-check: Total Gross **J\$88,000** − Total Deductions **J\$16,000** = Total Net Pay **J\$72,000** ✓!)*
