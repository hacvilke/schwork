## 1. Methods of Remuneration & Methods of Payment

*(Directly tested on May/June 2025 Paper 032 Q4!)*
Do not confuse **how wages are calculated** (*remuneration method*) with **how the money is delivered** (*payment method*):

### A. Four Common Methods of Calculating Employee Pay (Remuneration)
1. **Time Rate (Hourly / Daily Wage):** Paid per hour worked (e.g. J\$1,000/hr) — ideal for factory, construction, and retail shift staff. Extra hours earn **Overtime** (`1.5×` time-and-a-half or `2×` double-time!).
2. **Fixed Salary:** Fixed monthly or annual sum (e.g. J\$180,000/month) regardless of exact daily hours — common for managers, teachers, and office accountants.
3. **Piece Rate:** Paid strictly per **unit produced** (e.g. J\$250 per shirt sewn) — rewards fast workers, though quality must be inspected closely!
4. **Commission (or Basic Salary + Commission):** Paid a percentage (`%`) of **sales made** (e.g. 5% of cars or insurance policies sold) — motivates sales reps strongly!

### B. Methods of Paying Employees, Suppliers & Goods/Services (Syllabus Obj 11.1!)
| Who Is Being Paid? | Modern Methods of Payment |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Employees (Payroll)** | **Direct Deposit (Bank Credit Transfer)** *(fastest, safest, eliminates cash theft!)*, business **Cheques**, or sealed **Cash** pay packets *(for casual/unbanked staff)*. |
| **Suppliers & Bills** | **Cheques**, **Online Bank Transfer (ACH/RTGS)**, **Standing Orders** *(fixed monthly rent/loans)*, **Direct Debits** *(variable JPS/Flow utility bills)*, **Debit/Credit Cards**, **EFTPOS** *(Electronic Funds Transfer at Point of Sale)*, mobile wallets (e.g. Lynk), and cash. |

## 2. Payroll Source Documents & Computerised Payroll Software

Accurate payroll protects both the worker and the business from costly TAJ fines:
- **Time Cards / Time Books / Electronic Clock-In Cards (Biometric Scanners):** Track exact clock-in/clock-out times to separate regular hours from overtime hours.
- **Employee Earnings Record:** Permanent individual file recording each worker's tax registration number (TRN), pay rate, authorised voluntary deductions, and year-to-date earnings.
- **Payroll Register (Wages Sheet):** Master spreadsheet/table computing Basic Pay, Overtime, Gross Pay, every deduction, and Net Pay for all staff in a pay period.
- **Payslip (Pay Advice):** Private slip given to each employee showing exactly how their take-home Net Pay was calculated.
- **Payroll Accounting Software:** **QuickBooks**, **Sage 50 / Peachtree Accounting**, **Microsoft Excel** *(using formulas like `=SUM()`, `=IF()` for overtime/PAYE)* — speeds up calculations, prevents arithmetic mistakes, generates instant payslips, and prints TAJ statutory returns!

## 3. Statutory Deductions vs Voluntary Deductions (Jamaican Context!)

Every dollar deducted from an employee's Gross Pay falls into **one of two legal groups**:

| Category | Legal Rule | Jamaican / Caribbean Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Statutory Deductions** | **Compulsory by law** — employers MUST deduct these automatically and remit them to **Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ)** by the monthly deadline! | • **NIS** *(National Insurance Scheme / Social Security)*<br/>• **NHT** *(National Housing Trust)*<br/>• **Education Tax**<br/>• **Income Tax (PAYE — Pay As You Earn)** *(charged ONLY on taxable earnings ABOVE the tax-free threshold!)* |
| **Voluntary (Non-Statutory) Deductions** | Deducted **ONLY with the employee's written consent / instruction!** | • **Credit Union** savings or loan repayments<br/>• **Mortgage** salary deductions<br/>• **Trade Union** membership dues<br/>• **Group Health / Life Insurance** & private pension contributions |

*(Accounting Note — **How Payroll Enters the Ledger!** When payroll is recorded: **Dr Wages & Salaries Expense** with **TOTAL GROSS PAY** [the true cost to the business!]; **Cr Bank** with **Total Net Pay** paid to staff; and **Cr Statutory & Voluntary Deductions Payable** [Current Liabilities] until remitted to TAJ, Credit Unions, and Insurers!)*

## 4. Core Payroll Formulae

<FormulaCard
  title="Gross Pay, Overtime & Net Pay Formulae"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Add Basic Pay + Overtime + Bonuses to get Gross Pay, then subtract ALL Statutory and Voluntary Deductions to find take-home Net Pay!"
>

<FormulaHint
  block
  label="Gross Pay (Total Earnings Before Deductions)"
  short="Basic Hours × Basic Rate plus Overtime Hours × Overtime Rate (Time-and-a-half = 1.5×; Double-time = 2×) plus any commissions or bonuses."
>

$$
\text{Gross Pay} = \text{Basic Pay} + \text{Overtime Pay} + \text{Bonuses / Allowances}
$$

</FormulaHint>

<FormulaHint
  block
  label="Net Pay (Take-Home Pay)"
  short="The exact take-home amount deposited into the employee's bank account after deducting both Statutory and Voluntary deductions."
>

$$
\text{Net Pay} = \text{Gross Pay} - (\text{Statutory Deductions} + \text{Voluntary Deductions})
$$

</FormulaHint>

</FormulaCard>

## 5. Worked Example: Complete Weekly Payroll Register

<WorkedExample
  title="Kingston Bakery Ltd — Weekly Payroll Register (Basic, Overtime, PAYE Threshold & Net Pay)"
  marks={12}
  problem={
    <>
      Prepare the Weekly Payroll Sheet for two employees of <strong>Kingston Bakery Ltd</strong> (normal working week = <strong>40 hours</strong>):
      <br />
      • <strong>K. Brown:</strong> 40 basic hrs @ <strong>J$1,000/hr</strong> + <strong>5 hrs double-time</strong> (2× basic rate). Voluntary deductions: Credit Union <strong>J$2,000</strong>; Trade Union dues <strong>J$500</strong>.
      <br />
      • <strong>D. Grant:</strong> 40 basic hrs @ <strong>J$800/hr</strong> + <strong>5 hrs time-and-a-half</strong> (1.5× basic rate). Voluntary deductions: Credit Union <strong>J$1,500</strong>; Group Health <strong>J$600</strong>.
      <br />
      <strong>Statutory Deduction Rates (given for this problem):</strong> NIS = <strong>3% of Gross Pay</strong>; NHT = <strong>2% of Gross Pay</strong>; Income Tax (PAYE) = <strong>25% on Gross Pay above the J$30,000 weekly tax-free threshold</strong>.
    </>
  }
  examTip="WATCH THE PAYE TAX-FREE THRESHOLD! Never multiply 25% by total Gross Pay if a threshold is given — ALWAYS subtract the J$30,000 tax-free allowance FIRST, then take 25% of ONLY the excess above J$30,000!"
>

### Step-by-Step Workings
- **K. Brown:**
  - Basic = $40 \times 1{,}000 =$ **J\$40,000** | Overtime = $5 \times (2 \times 1{,}000) =$ **J\$10,000** → **Gross Pay = J\$50,000**
  - Statutory: NIS ($3\% \times 50{,}000 = 1{,}500$) + NHT ($2\% \times 50{,}000 = 1{,}000$) + PAYE ($25\% \times (50{,}000 - 30{,}000) = 5{,}000$) = **J\$7,500**
  - Voluntary: $2{,}000 + 500 =$ **J\$2,500** → Total Deductions = **J\$10,000** → **Net Pay = J\$40,000**
- **D. Grant:**
  - Basic = $40 \times 800 =$ **J\$32,000** | Overtime = $5 \times (1.5 \times 800) =$ **J\$6,000** → **Gross Pay = J\$38,000**
  - Statutory: NIS ($3\% \times 38{,}000 = 1{,}140$) + NHT ($2\% \times 38{,}000 = 760$) + PAYE ($25\% \times (38{,}000 - 30{,}000) = 2{,}000$) = **J\$3,900**
  - Voluntary: $1{,}500 + 600 =$ **J\$2,100** → Total Deductions = **J\$6,000** → **Net Pay = J\$32,000**

### Kingston Bakery Ltd — Weekly Payroll Register

| Employee | Basic (J\$) | Overtime (J\$) | **Gross Pay (J\$)** | NIS 3% | NHT 2% | PAYE 25% | Voluntary (J\$) | Total Ded. (J\$) | **Net Pay (J\$)** |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| K. Brown | 40,000 | 10,000 | **50,000** | 1,500 | 1,000 | 5,000 | 2,500 | 10,000 | **40,000** |
| D. Grant | 32,000 | 6,000 | **38,000** | 1,140 | 760 | 2,000 | 2,100 | 6,000 | **32,000** |
| **TOTALS** | **72,000** | **16,000** | **88,000** | **2,640** | **1,760** | **7,000** | **4,600** | **16,000** | **72,000** |

*(Cross-Check: Total Gross **J\$88,000** − Total Deductions **J\$16,000** = Total Net Pay **J\$72,000** ✓! Summary Journal: `Dr Wages Expense 88,000` / `Cr Bank 72,000`, `Cr Statutory Deductions Payable 11,400`, `Cr Voluntary Deductions Payable 4,600`.)*

</WorkedExample>
