## 1. Co-operative Societies (Member-Owned & Democratic!)

A **Co-operative Society** is owned, controlled, and used by its own **members** for their mutual benefit rather than maximising outside shareholder profit (famous Jamaican examples: **Credit Unions** like COK / JTA / EduCom, **Coffee & Agricultural Societies**, and **Fishermen's Co-operatives**).

### Core Co-operative Principles (High-Yield Exam Theory!)
1. **Voluntary & Open Membership** — anyone sharing the common bond can join or leave freely.
2. **Democratic Control ("One Member, One Vote")** — even if one member holds 500 shares and another holds 10 shares, each gets **1 vote** to elect the Board of Management!
3. **Members Are Both Owners AND Customers** — members buy shares (raising capital) and use the society's loans, groceries, or farm supplies.
4. **Fair Distribution of Surplus** — any yearly **Surplus** (excess of income over expenditure) is split according to the Co-operative's rules:
   - **Statutory Reserve Fund** *(mandatory % set aside by law for financial safety)*
   - **Education Fund** *(training members and staff)*
   - **Honorarium** *(small thank-you payment to voluntary committee officers)*
   - **Interest / Dividend on Members' Shares**
   - **Patronage Refund (Bonus)** *(shared back to members based on how much business they did with the co-operative!)*

## 2. Non-Profit Organisations (Clubs, Charities & Societies)

Sports clubs, youth clubs, churches, and charities exist to **serve members or the community**, NOT to make commercial profit. Notice how familiar Sole Trader terms simply get **new club-friendly names**:

| Sole Trader Term | **Non-Profit (Club / Society) Equivalent** | Plain-English Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cash Book** | **Receipts and Payments Account** | Summary of ALL **cash & bank** received (Dr) and paid (Cr) during the year — includes BOTH capital and revenue cash items! |
| **Profit & Loss Account** | **Income and Expenditure Account** | Accrual-based summary of **revenue income** and **revenue expenses** for THIS year ONLY (excludes capital purchases like new equipment!). |
| **Net Profit / Net Loss** | **Surplus / Deficit** | **Surplus** = Income > Expenditure; **Deficit** = Expenditure > Income. |
| **Capital (Owner's Equity)** | **Accumulated Fund** | The club's net worth: $\text{Accumulated Fund} = \text{Club Assets} - \text{Club Liabilities}$! |

### (b) Capital vs Revenue Expenditure *(Also Bridges to Old-Syllabus Income & Expenditure!)*
- **New Sports Equipment (J\$28,000)** is **Capital Expenditure** — it appears in the **Receipts & Payments Account** (because cash was spent), but it does **NOT** appear in an **Income & Expenditure Account**! Instead, it goes to **Fixed Assets** on the Club Balance Sheet (only annual *depreciation* on equipment goes into Income & Expenditure!).
- *(Old-Syllabus Tip: In a Club **Income & Expenditure Account**, Bar Profit = `42,000 − 24,000 = 18,000`, plus Subscriptions `65,000` + Donations `15,000` = `98,000` Income; less Revenue Expenses `30,000 + 12,000 + 8,000 = 50,000` → **Surplus of J\$48,000**, added to the **Accumulated Fund**!)*
