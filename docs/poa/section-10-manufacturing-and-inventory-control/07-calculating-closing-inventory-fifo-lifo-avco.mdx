## 1. The Ironclad Stock Valuation Rules & Formulas

<FormulaCard
  title="Master Reconciliation Proofs (Verify EVERY Single Method!)"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="If COGS + Closing Stock does NOT equal Total Cost of Goods Available, stop and check your layers immediately!"
>

$$
\text{Units Available} = \text{Opening Units} + \text{Units Purchased} = \text{Units Sold} + \text{Closing Units}
$$

$$
\text{Total Cost of Goods Available} = \text{Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)} + \text{Closing Inventory Value}
$$

$$
\textbf{Periodic AVCO Unit Cost} = \frac{\text{Total Cost of Goods Available}}{\text{Total Units Available}}
$$

</FormulaCard>

### Three Rules That Eliminate 100% of Stock Card Errors
1. **NEVER Use Selling Price in the Stock Card!** If the question says *"Jan 12: Sold 180 units at `$20` each"*, that **`$20` is Sales Revenue** (`180 × $20 = $3,600`) for the Income Statement — inside the Stock Card’s **Issues** column, you ONLY cost those 180 units using their **historical purchase costs (`$10`, `$12`, `$14`)**!
2. **Track Layers Explicitly in the Balance Column!** After every purchase or issue, list each surviving cost layer on its own mini-line *(e.g. `20 @ $12 + 100 @ $14`)* so your next issue peels off the exact right layer!
3. **Know Periodic vs. Perpetual Behavior:**
   - **FIFO:** Perpetual Stock Card AND Periodic shortcut ALWAYS give the **EXACT SAME answer**!
   - **LIFO:** Perpetual LIFO *(peels newest batch on hand at EACH sale date)* can differ from Periodic LIFO *(waits until month-end and leaves the oldest batches in Closing Stock)* — **always follow whichever system the exam specifies!**

## 4. Worked Example

<WorkedExample
  title="Calculating Closing Inventory & COGS Across FIFO, LIFO & AVCO (HarborTools Wholesale)"
  marks={15}
  problem={
    <>
      <p>
        <strong>Scenario:</strong> <strong>HarborTools Wholesale</strong> recorded the following

        <li><strong>Jan 12:</strong> Sold — <strong>180 units</strong> *(at `$20` selling price each)*</li>
        <li><strong>Jan 20:</strong> Purchased — <strong>100 units @ $14 each</strong> (`$1,400`)</li>
        <li><strong>Jan 28:</strong> Sold — <strong>50 units</strong> *(at `$20` selling price each)*</li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        Calculate <strong>Closing Inventory (31 Jan)</strong> and <strong>Cost of Goods Sold</strong>
        using: (1) a <strong>Perpetual FIFO Stock Card</strong> <em>[6 marks]</em>; (2) a
        <strong>Perpetual LIFO Stock Card</strong> *(plus note the Periodic LIFO figure!)*
        <em>[6 marks]</em>; and (3) <strong>Periodic AVCO (Weighted Average)</strong> <em>[3 marks]</em>.
      </p>
    </>
  }
>

*Pre-Calculation Anchor:* Total Available = `350` units (`$4,200`); Total Sold = `230` units; Closing Units = **`120` units**. *(Selling price `$20` is IGNORED inside all Stock Cards!)*

### 1. Perpetual FIFO Stock Card *(6 marks)*
| Date | Receipts *(Qty × $ = Total)* | Issues *(COGS)* | Running Balance on Hand |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Jan 1** | `100 @ $10 = $1,000` | — | `100 @ $10` (`$1,000`) |
| **Jan 8** | `150 @ $12 = $1,800` | — | `100 @ $10` + `150 @ $12` (`$2,800`) |
| **Jan 12** | — | `100 @ $10` (`$1,000`)<br/>`80 @ $12` (`$960`)<br/>**= `$1,960`** | `70 @ $12` (`$840`) |
| **Jan 20** | `100 @ $14 = $1,400` | — | `70 @ $12` + `100 @ $14` (`$2,240`) |
| **Jan 28** | — | `50 @ $12` = **`$600`** | `20 @ $12` (`$240`) + `100 @ $14` (`$1,400`) |

- **FIFO Closing Inventory (`120` units):** **`$1,640`** | **FIFO COGS (`230` units):** `$1,960 + $600` = **`$2,560`** *(Proof: `$2,560 + $1,640 = $4,200` ✓!)*

---

### 2. Perpetual LIFO Stock Card *(6 marks)*
| Date | Receipts *(Qty × $ = Total)* | Issues *(COGS)* | Running Balance on Hand |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Jan 1** | `100 @ $10 = $1,000` | — | `100 @ $10` (`$1,000`) |
| **Jan 8** | `150 @ $12 = $1,800` | — | `100 @ $10` + `150 @ $12` (`$2,800`) |
| **Jan 12** | — | `150 @ $12` (`$1,800`)<br/>`30 @ $10` (`$300`)<br/>**= `$2,100`** | `70 @ $10` (`$700`) |
| **Jan 20** | `100 @ $14 = $1,400` | — | `70 @ $10` + `100 @ $14` (`$2,100`) |
| **Jan 28** | — | `50 @ $14` = **`$700`** | `70 @ $10` (`$700`) + `50 @ $14` (`$700`) |

- **Perpetual LIFO Closing Inventory (`120` units):** **`$1,400`** | **Perpetual LIFO COGS:** `$2,100 + $700` = **`$2,800`** *(Proof: `$2,800 + $1,400 = $4,200` ✓!)*
- *(Periodic LIFO Note: Waiting until 31 Jan leaves the 120 oldest units → `100 @ $10 + 20 @ $12` = **`$1,240`** Closing Stock and **`$2,960`** COGS!)*

---

### 3. Periodic AVCO (Weighted Average Cost) *(3 marks)*
- **Weighted Average Unit Cost:** `$4,200 ÷ 350 units` = **`$12.00` per unit**
- **AVCO Closing Inventory (`120` units × `$12.00`):** **`$1,440`**
- **AVCO COGS (`230` units × `$12.00`):** **`$2,760`** *(Proof: `$2,760 + $1,440 = $4,200` ✓!)*

</WorkedExample>
