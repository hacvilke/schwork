:::info Essential Understanding
When a store buys identical bags of flour or bottles of motor oil at **different purchase prices** throughout the year (`$10`, then `$12`, then `$14`), how do you decide the dollar cost of the units still sitting on shelves on 31 December? Accountants apply one of three **Cost-Flow Assumptions** — **FIFO *(First-In, First-Out)***, **LIFO *(Last-In, First-Out)***, or **AVCO *(Weighted Average Cost)*** — always subject to the supreme Prudence rule of **IAS 2: value Closing Inventory at the LOWER of Cost and Net Realizable Value (NRV)**!
:::

## 1. Comparing the Three Inventory Valuation Methods

| Feature | FIFO *(First-In, First-Out)* | LIFO *(Last-In, First-Out)* | AVCO *(Weighted Average)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cost-Flow Assumption** | **OLDEST** stock bought is sold/issued FIRST | **NEWEST** stock bought is sold/issued FIRST | All batches blended into ONE **weighted average unit cost** |
| **What Remains in Closing Stock?** | **NEWEST** purchases *(realistic, up-to-date Balance Sheet value!)* | **OLDEST** purchases *(outdated Balance Sheet value!)* | Smoothed **middle average** cost |
| **Best Physical Fit** | **Perishables &amp; dated goods** *(milk, groceries, medicine, fashion)* | **Bulk stacked piles** *(coal, gravel, sand — top layer scooped first)* | **Mixed / liquid commodities** *(petrol tanks, grain silos, paint)* |
| **Permitted by IAS 2 / IFRS?** | **YES ✓** | **NO ✗** *(Banned under IFRS!)* | **YES ✓** |

### Perpetual vs. Periodic Inventory Control
- **Perpetual Inventory System:** Continuously updates stock quantities and costs **after EVERY purchase and EVERY sale/issue** using a Stock Ledger Card or barcode/POS software — gives real-time theft/shrinkage visibility!
- **Periodic Inventory System:** Counts physical stock **only at the end of the period** (e.g., 31 December stocktake) and computes COGS once (`Opening Stock + Purchases − Closing Stock`).

### The Golden IAS 2 Rule: Lower of Cost and Net Realizable Value (NRV)
Applying the **Prudence Concept** *(never overstate assets or profits!)*, Closing Inventory must be valued item-by-item at the **LOWER of**:
1. **Historical Cost:** Purchase price + Carriage Inwards + conversion costs to bring stock to its current location and condition.
2. **Net Realizable Value (NRV):**
   $$\text{NRV} = \text{Estimated Selling Price} - \text{Estimated Costs to Complete, Repair, or Sell}$$
*(If goods become damaged, spoiled, or obsolete so NRV falls BELOW Cost, you MUST write inventory down immediately to NRV!)*

### Part A: Method Selection & IFRS Rule *(3 marks — 1 mark each)*
1. **Fresh Bakery &amp; Dairy Distributor → `FIFO`:** Mirrors perishable physical rotation (oldest items sold first before expiry) and leaves up-to-date Closing Stock values.
2. **Gasoline Station Underground Tank → `AVCO`:** Once new fuel pours into the tank, old and new fuel physically mingle indistinguishably — blending costs into a weighted average matches reality seamlessly.
3. **Why No `LIFO` under IFRS:** IAS 2 prohibits LIFO because it leaves outdated historical purchase prices on the Balance Sheet and allows manipulation of reported profits through purchase timing.

### Part B: IAS 2 Lower of Cost and NRV Schedule *(7 marks)*

| Product Line | Units | Unit Cost | Unit NRV *(Sell − Costs)* | Lower of Cost / NRV | Total Value ($) |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **A. Travel Backpacks** | 10 | `$500` | `$650 − $30 = $620` | **`$500`** *(Cost)* | **5,000** |
| **B. Water-Damaged Sneakers** | 20 | `$80` | `$65 − $10 = $55` | **`$55`** *(NRV ↓)* | **1,100** |
| **C. Outdated Phone Cases** | 50 | `$15` | `$12 − $2 = $10` | **`$10`** *(NRV ↓)* | **500** |
| **Total Closing Inventory *(IAS 2)*** | | | | | **$6,600** |

- **Total Historical Cost:** `(10 × $500) + (20 × $80) + (50 × $15)` = `$5,000 + $1,600 + $750` = **`$7,350`**
- **Total Inventory Write-Down *(Prudence Loss recognized immediately)*:** `$7,350 − $6,600` = **`$750`** (`$500` on Sneakers + `$250` on Phone Cases)! *(Notice: Backpacks are NEVER marked up to `$620` — Prudence forbids recognizing unrealized gains!)*

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **FIFO** = Oldest sold first → **Newest** in Closing Stock (**IFRS ✓**).
- **LIFO** = Newest sold first → **Oldest** in Closing Stock (**IFRS ✗**).
- **AVCO** = Blended **weighted average** cost (**IFRS ✓**).
- **IAS 2 Golden Rule:** **Lower of Cost and NRV** *(evaluated item-by-item!)*!
:::
