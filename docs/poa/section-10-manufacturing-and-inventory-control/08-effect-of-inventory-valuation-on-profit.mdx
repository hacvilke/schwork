:::info Essential Understanding
Why do CFOs, investors, and tax authorities care so deeply whether a company uses **FIFO**, **AVCO**, or **LIFO**? Because **every single `$1` increase in Closing Inventory lowers COGS by `$1` and boosts Gross Profit & Net Profit by `$1`** — WITHOUT selling one extra product! During **rising prices (inflation)**, **FIFO** reports the **highest Closing Stock, highest Profit, and highest Tax bill**, **LIFO** reports the **lowest**, and **AVCO** glides cleanly in the middle — which is precisely why the **Consistency Concept** forbids switching methods back and forth!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | **Inflation vs. Deflation Rankings** (`FIFO > AVCO > LIFO` in rising prices; reversed in falling prices!), **Balance Sheet &amp; Tax Impacts**, **Two-Year Ripple Effect of Inventory Errors**, and the **Consistency Concept** |
| **Exam Focus** | Tracing **`Higher Closing Stock → Lower COGS → Higher Gross Profit`**, explaining the **2-year counter-balancing error effect**, and justifying why businesses **cannot flip-flop** valuation methods |
| **Learning Outcome** | Evaluate how FIFO, LIFO, and AVCO affect Gross Profit, Net Profit, Current Assets, Working Capital, and Taxation across rising and falling price environments |

## 1. The Chain Reaction & Inflation vs. Deflation Cheat Sheet

<FormulaCard
  title="The Closing Stock → Profit Chain Reaction"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Memorize this 1-to-1 relationship — it unlocks every conceptual Paper 1 & Paper 2 inventory question!"
>

$$
\text{Higher Closing Stock (}+\$X\text{)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{Lower COGS (}-\$X\text{)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \textbf{Higher Gross \& Net Profit (}+\$X\textbf{)}
$$

$$
\text{Lower Closing Stock (}-\$X\text{)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \text{Higher COGS (}+\$X\text{)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \textbf{Lower Gross \& Net Profit (}-\$X\textbf{)}
$$

</FormulaCard>

### Complete Ranking Matrix: Rising Prices (Inflation) vs. Falling Prices (Deflation)

| Financial Metric | **Rising Prices *(Inflation)*** | **Falling Prices *(Deflation)*** |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Closing Inventory *(Current Asset)*** | **FIFO** Highest &gt; **AVCO** &gt; **LIFO** Lowest | **LIFO** Highest &gt; **AVCO** &gt; **FIFO** Lowest |
| **Working Capital &amp; Current Ratio** | **FIFO** Highest &gt; **AVCO** &gt; **LIFO** Lowest | **LIFO** Highest &gt; **AVCO** &gt; **FIFO** Lowest |
| **Cost of Goods Sold *(COGS)*** | **LIFO** Highest &gt; **AVCO** &gt; **FIFO** Lowest | **FIFO** Highest &gt; **AVCO** &gt; **LIFO** Lowest |
| **Gross Profit &amp; Net Profit** | **FIFO** Highest &gt; **AVCO** &gt; **LIFO** Lowest | **LIFO** Highest &gt; **AVCO** &gt; **FIFO** Lowest |
| **Income Tax Payable** | **FIFO** Highest &gt; **AVCO** &gt; **LIFO** Lowest | **LIFO** Highest &gt; **AVCO** &gt; **FIFO** Lowest |

### The Two-Year Counter-Balancing Effect of an Inventory Error!
Suppose an error **OVERSTATES Year 1 Closing Inventory by `$1,500`**:
- **In Year 1:** Closing Stock is `+$1,500` too high → Year 1 COGS is `−$1,500` too low → **Year 1 Net Profit is OVERSTATED by `+$1,500`** *(and Year 1 Assets &amp; Capital are overstated by `+$1,500`!)*.
- **In Year 2:** Year 1’s Closing Stock automatically becomes **Year 2’s Opening Stock (`+$1,500` too high!)** → Year 2 COGS is `+$1,500` too high → **Year 2 Net Profit is UNDERSTATED by `−$1,500`**!
- **Combined 2-Year Total:** `+$1,500 − $1,500 = $0` *(self-corrects over two years)* — **HOWEVER, BOTH Year 1 AND Year 2 individual financial statements are distorted and misleading!**

### 1. Side-by-Side Trading Account Comparison *(Rising Prices — 7 marks)*

| Trading Account Line | FIFO ($) | AVCO ($) | Periodic LIFO ($) |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Sales** *(`230 units × $20`)* | **4,600** | **4,600** | **4,600** |
| **Less: Cost of Goods Sold:** | | | |
| Cost of Goods Available *(Open + Purchases)* | 4,200 | 4,200 | 4,200 |
| Less: **Closing Inventory** | **(1,640)** | **(1,440)** | **(1,240)** |
| **Cost of Goods Sold *(COGS)*** | **2,560** | **2,760** | **2,960** |
| **Gross Profit** | **$2,040** | **$1,840** | **$1,640** |
| **Gross Profit Margin (`GP ÷ Sales`)** | **44.3%** | **40.0%** | **35.7%** |
| *Illustrative 25% Tax on Gross Profit* | *$510* | *$460* | *$410* |

*Key Observation:* Because purchase prices rose steadily, **FIFO** leaves the newest `$14` costs in Closing Stock (`$1,640`) → lowest COGS (`$2,560`) → **highest Gross Profit (`$2,040`)**, while **Periodic LIFO** produces the exact opposite (`$1,640` Gross Profit), and **AVCO** (`$1,840`) sits cleanly in the middle!

### 2. Two-Year Ripple Effect of `+$1,500` Closing Stock Overstatement *(4 marks)*
- **Year 1 Net Profit:** **OVERSTATED by `+$1,500`** *(higher Closing Stock artificially lowers Year 1 COGS by `$1,500`)* *(1.5 marks)*.
- **Year 1 Current Assets &amp; Capital:** **OVERSTATED by `+$1,500`** *(1 mark)*.
- **Year 2 Net Profit:** **UNDERSTATED by `−$1,500`** *(Year 1’s overstated Closing Stock becomes Year 2’s overstated Opening Stock, inflating Year 2 COGS by `+$1,500`!)* *(1.5 marks)*.

### 3. Why Management Cannot Flip-Flop Methods *(3 marks)*
- Switching methods purely to inflate or smooth profits violates the **Consistency Concept** *(1.5 marks)*.
- It destroys **year-to-year comparability**, making it impossible for investors, bankers, and tax authorities to tell whether profit changed because of real business performance or accounting trickery *(1.5 marks)*.

</WorkedExample>

:::tip Section 10 Complete — Quick Exam Checklist!
- **Manufacturing Accounts:** `Direct Costs → Prime Cost → + Factory Overheads → + Open WIP − Close WIP = Cost of Production` → plugs into **Trading COGS**; ALL **3 Closing Stocks** (`RM + WIP + FG`) sit in Balance Sheet Current Assets!
- **Costing &amp; Pricing:** `Unit Cost = Total Cost of Production ÷ Good Units Produced`; `OAR = Budgeted Overheads ÷ Budgeted Hours`; Mark-Up (`% × Cost`) vs. Margin (`Cost ÷ [1 − Margin %]`).
- **Inventory Control:** **IAS 2** = Lower of Cost &amp; NRV (`FIFO` &amp; `AVCO` ✓; `LIFO` ✗); **Inflation Ranking:** `FIFO > AVCO > LIFO` for Closing Stock, Profit &amp; Tax (reverses in Deflation!); **Consistency Concept** prohibits flip-flopping!
:::
