:::info Essential Understanding
You are ready for the **Full Manufacturing Final Accounts Capstone**! A manufacturer’s year-end package links **three clean financial statements** together like clockwork: **(1) Manufacturing Account** computes **Cost of Production** → **(2) Income Statement** plugs **Cost of Production** straight into COGS (right where *"Purchases"* used to live!) and subtracts Non-Factory Expenses to find **Net Profit** → **(3) Classified Balance Sheet** displays **ALL THREE Closing Inventories (`Raw Materials + Work-in-Progress + Finished Goods`)** under Current Assets and balances `Total Net Assets = Total Capital Employed` to the exact dollar!
:::

## 1. How the Three Statements Lock Together

1. **Manufacturing Account → Trading Account Link:**
   $$\text{Cost of Goods Sold (Manufacturer)} = \text{Opening Finished Goods} + \textbf{Cost of Production} \text{ (+ Any Bought-in Finished Goods)} - \text{Closing Finished Goods}$$
2. **Depreciation &amp; Shared Costs Split Cleanly:**
   - **Factory Machinery Depreciation** &amp; **Factory Share of Rent/Power** → **Manufacturing Account** *(Factory Overheads)*
   - **Delivery Van / Office Equipment Depreciation**, **Office Share of Rent/Power**, &amp; **Loan Interest** → **Income Statement** *(Operating &amp; Finance Expenses)*
3. **Balance Sheet Current Assets (`3 Closing Inventories!`):**
   Never put ONLY Closing Finished Goods on a manufacturer’s Balance Sheet — list **ALL THREE Closing Inventories** cleanly and sum them:
   - Closing **Raw Materials**
   - Closing **Work-in-Progress**
   - Closing **Finished Goods**

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Trading COGS:** `Opening Finished Goods + Cost of Production − Closing Finished Goods`.
- **Balance Sheet Current Assets:** ALWAYS include **ALL 3 Closing Stocks** (`Raw Materials + WIP + Finished Goods`)!
- **Split Depreciation:** Plant → Manufacturing A/c; Delivery Vans / Office Equipment → Income Statement!
:::
