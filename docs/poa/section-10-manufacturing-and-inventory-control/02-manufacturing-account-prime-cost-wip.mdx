:::info Essential Understanding
The **Manufacturing Account** answers ONE crystal-clear question for factory owners: **"Exactly how much did it cost us to manufacture the goods we completed this year?"** It follows a rock-solid **4-Stage Waterfall** — (1) **Raw Materials Consumed** → (2) **Prime Cost** → (3) **+ Factory Overheads** → (4) **+ Opening WIP − Closing WIP = Cost of Production**! Master this 4-stage vertical template and you unlock effortless marks on every single manufacturing question!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | The **4-Stage Manufacturing Account** (`Raw Materials Consumed → Prime Cost → Factory Overheads → WIP Adjustment = Cost of Production`) and the **3 Factory Inventories** |
| **Exam Focus** | **NEVER** putting **Finished Goods Inventory** inside the Manufacturing Account *(it goes ONLY to the Trading Account!)*, adjusting **Accruals/Prepayments** on factory expenses, and remembering **`+ Opening WIP − Closing WIP`** |
| **Learning Outcome** | Construct a complete vertical Manufacturing Account incorporating raw material adjustments, shared overhead splits, factory depreciation, and Work-in-Progress |

## 1. The 4-Stage Manufacturing Account Formula Sheet

<FormulaCard
  title="Four Exact Stages to Cost of Production"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Memorize the order — Stage 1 feeds Stage 2, Stage 2 + Stage 3 feed Stage 4!"
>

$$
\textbf{Stage 1:}\quad \text{Raw Materials Consumed} = \text{Open RM} + \text{RM Purchases} + \text{Carriage In} - \text{Returns Out} - \text{Close RM}
$$

$$
\textbf{Stage 2:}\quad \text{Prime Cost} = \text{Raw Materials Consumed} + \text{Direct Labor} + \text{Direct Expenses}
$$

$$
\textbf{Stage 3:}\quad \text{Total Factory Cost} = \text{Prime Cost} + \text{Total Factory Overheads}
$$

$$
\textbf{Stage 4:}\quad \text{Cost of Production} = \text{Total Factory Cost} + \text{Opening WIP} - \text{Closing WIP}
$$

</FormulaCard>

### Why Do We ADD Opening WIP and SUBTRACT Closing WIP?
- **Work-in-Progress (WIP)** = partially completed items still sitting halfway down the assembly line *(e.g., chairs built last December that only needed varnishing in January)*.
- **ADD Opening WIP (1 Jan):** Those half-built items from last year were **finished THIS year**, so their opening cost becomes part of THIS year’s completed goods!
- **SUBTRACT Closing WIP (31 Dec):** Half-built items still unfinished on 31 December are **NOT completed yet**, so we strip their cost out and carry it forward into next year *(as a Current Asset on the Balance Sheet!)*!

:::warning #1 Exam Trap — Keep Finished Goods OUT of the Manufacturing Account!
A factory holds **THREE** inventories: (1) **Raw Materials**, (2) **Work-in-Progress**, and (3) **Finished Goods**.
- **Raw Materials** → Stage 1 of Manufacturing Account!
- **Work-in-Progress** → Stage 4 of Manufacturing Account!
- **Finished Goods** → **NEVER inside Manufacturing Account!** Finished Goods belong strictly in the **Trading Section of the Income Statement**!
:::
