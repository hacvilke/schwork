## 3. Master Case Dataset — BlueHarbor Craft & Roastery

Read all four phases carefully and complete your portfolio cleanly on accounting paper or in an Excel/Google Sheets workbook with separate tabs for Phases 1–4.

### Phase 1 Dataset — Opening Position & January 2026 Transactions *(25 points)*

**Opening Balances on 1 January 2026:**
- Roasting &amp; Shop Equipment: **`$24,000`**
- Inventory (`300` roasted coffee gift bags @ `$20.00` each): **`$6,000`**
- Trade Receivables (*K. Reid*): **`$4,000`**
- Bank: **`$18,000`**
- Trade Payables (*Island Beans Ltd*): **`$5,000`**
- **Task 1A:** Calculate **Opening Capital on 1 January 2026** using the Accounting Equation.

**Transactions During January 2026:**
1. **Jan 3:** Purchased **`400` bags of specialty beans** on credit from *Island Beans Ltd* at a list price of **`$10,000` less `10%` Trade Discount** *(net cost = `$22.50` per bag)*.
2. **Jan 6:** Sold **`220` bags** on credit to *K. Reid* for **`$12,000`**.
3. **Jan 9:** *K. Reid* returned damaged bags worth **`$800`** *(Credit Note #CN-01 issued)*.
4. **Jan 12:** Paid *Island Beans Ltd* Cheque `#104` to settle the **`$5,000` opening balance** in full, receiving a **`4%` Cash Discount**.
5. **Jan 16:** Recorded **EFTPOS card sales (`240` bags)** totaling **`$14,000` gross**; bank deducted a **`2%` merchant processing fee** and credited net proceeds to Bank.
6. **Jan 20:** Received a cheque from *K. Reid* for **`$9,500`** after allowing him **`$500` Cash Discount** (settling `$10,000` of his account).
7. **Jan 24 &amp; Jan 31 *(Imprest Petty Cash)*:** Established a **`$400` Petty Cash float** by cheque on Jan 24. Paid **`$260`** in minor postage &amp; cleaning expenses from Petty Cash during the week, then reimbursed Petty Cash **`$260`** by cheque on Jan 31 to restore the `$400` float.
8. **Jan 28:** Paid monthly Shop Rent **`$2,000`** via Standing Order and withdrew **`$1,500`** cash for Owner’s personal Drawings.
9. **Jan 31 *(Monthly Payroll)*:** Processed January payroll for 2 roasting staff: **Total Gross Wages = `$4,000`**; Statutory Deductions (`PAYE $450` + `NIS $120` = `$570`); Voluntary Credit Union Savings (`$130`); **Net Pay paid by Direct Deposit** *(deductions remain unpaid liabilities on 31 Jan)*.

**Phase 1 Deliverables:**
- *(1A)* Opening Capital calculation *(2 pts)*
- *(1B)* Summary General Journal / Day Books &amp; 2-Column Bank/Petty Cash Book *(10 pts)*
- *(1C)* Key Ledger T-Accounts (*Bank, K. Reid, Island Beans Ltd, Sales, Purchases, Capital*) *(6 pts)*
- *(1D)* Complete **Trial Balance as at 31 January 2026** *(7 pts)*

---

### Phase 2 Dataset — Controls, Bank Reconciliation, FIFO & Month-End Adjustments *(20 points)*

1. **Bank Reconciliation (`31 January 2026`):** The Bank Statement shows a **Credit balance of `$29,820`**. Investigation reveals:
   - Monthly **Bank Charges of `$40`** and **Bank Interest Earned of `$100`** appear on the Bank Statement but have not yet been entered in the Cash Book.
   - Cheque `#104` (**`$4,800`**) paid to *Island Beans Ltd* has not yet cleared the bank (**Unpresented Cheque**).
   - A late **`$4,000`** deposit banked on Jan 31 afternoon does not yet appear on the Bank Statement (**Uncredited Deposit**).
2. **Closing Inventory Valuation (`FIFO`):** From Phase 1, BlueHarbor held `300` opening bags @ `$20.00` + `400` Jan 3 bags @ `$22.50` (`700` bags available) and sold `460` net bags during January (`240` bags left on 31 Jan!). Value Closing Inventory under **FIFO**.
3. **Accruals, Prepayments, Depreciation &amp; Doubtful Debts (`31 January 2026`):**
   - Depreciate Roasting &amp; Shop Equipment (`$24,000`) at **`10%` per annum Straight-Line** *(charge **1 month** for January!)*.
   - **Electricity of `$300`** for January remained unpaid/accrued on 31 January.
   - The **`$2,000` Shop Rent** paid on Jan 28 includes **`$400` prepaid** for February.
   - Create a **`5%` Provision for Doubtful Debts** on closing Trade Receivables (`$5,200`).

**Phase 2 Deliverables:**
- *(2A)* **Adjusted Cash Book** &amp; **Bank Reconciliation Statement** as at 31 January 2026 *(8 pts)*
- *(2B)* **FIFO Closing Inventory Schedule** (`240` bags) *(4 pts)*
- *(2C)* **Month-End Adjusting Journal Entries** for Depreciation, Accrued Electricity, Prepaid Rent, and Doubtful Debts *(8 pts)*

---

### Phase 3 Dataset — Final Financial Statements & Ratio Analysis *(30 points)*

Using your balanced Trial Balance (Phase 1) and all verified adjustments &amp; revised Bank balance (Phase 2):
- *(3A)* Prepare BlueHarbor Craft &amp; Roastery’s **Vertical Income Statement** for the month ended **31 January 2026** *(12 pts)*.
- *(3B)* Prepare the **Classified Vertical Balance Sheet** as at **31 January 2026** showing Working Capital and proving `Total Net Assets = Closing Capital` *(12 pts)*.
- *(3C)* Calculate and interpret **four key ratios**: **Gross Profit Margin `%`**, **Net Profit Margin `%`**, **Current Ratio**, and **Acid-Test (Quick) Ratio** *(6 pts)*.

---

### Phase 4 Dataset — Expansion Structure, 3-Month Budgets & Ethics Brief *(25 points)*

1. **Expansion Structure Advisory *(Sections 8 &amp; 9 — 8 pts)*:** BlueHarbor needs **`$45,000`** to expand regionally. Compare **Option A** *(forming a Partnership with friend M. Lewis contributing `$25,000` cash + `$20,000` bank loan)* against **Option B** *(incorporating as a Private Limited Company `BlueHarbor Craft & Roastery Ltd` issuing shares and `8%` Debentures)* across **Limited Liability, Control, Profit/Dividend Sharing, and Continuity**, and give a justified recommendation!
2. **3-Month Sales &amp; Production Budget (`Feb–Apr 2026` — Section 11 — 10 pts):**
   - Forecasted monthly bag sales at **`$55.00` per bag**: **Feb `500`**, **Mar `600`**, **Apr `700`** *(and **May `800`**)*.
   - Desired Closing Inventory at each month-end = **`20%` of NEXT month’s budgeted sales**.
   - Opening Inventory on 1 February = **`240` bags** *(carried directly from your Phase 2 FIFO Closing Stock!)*.
   - Prepare both the **3-Month Sales Budget (Feb–Apr + Total)** and **3-Month Production Budget (Feb–Apr + Total)** with horizontal/vertical verification!
3. **Professional Ethics Audit *(Section 1 — 7 pts)*:** Before presenting January’s accounts to investor *M. Lewis*, an acquaintance urges the owner to *"hide the `$300` unpaid electricity bill until February and mark Closing Stock up by `$1,000` to make January Net Profit look even stronger."* Draft a firm **Professional Ethics Memo** citing **Objectivity, Integrity, Accrual Concept, Prudence Concept**, and **two legal/reputational consequences of fraudulent financial reporting**!
