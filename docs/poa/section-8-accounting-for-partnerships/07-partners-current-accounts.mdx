:::info Essential Understanding
Under the **Fixed Capital System**, every partner’s yearly financial story lives inside their **Current Account**! Rather than drawing separate T-accounts across two pages, examiners love the **columnar (side-by-side) Partners’ Current Accounts** format — putting Partner A and Partner B side by side on the Debit and Credit halves so you can post directly from the Appropriation Account in half the time and spot an **overdrawn partner (Debit balance)** at a glance!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | **Columnar Partners’ Current Accounts**: **Credit = Rewards Earned (`+`)**, **Debit = Drawings &amp; Penalties (`−`)**, plus handling **Opening &amp; Closing DEBIT (Overdrawn) Balances** and **Paid vs. Unpaid Salaries/Loan Interest** |
| **Exam Focus** | Never flipping Debit vs. Credit sides, handling an overdrawn partner whose Debit side exceeds their Credit side, and bringing down `Jan 1 Bal b/d` on the exact right side! |
| **Learning Outcome** | Construct accurate columnar Partners’ Current Accounts with both Credit and Debit (overdrawn) balances brought down cleanly |

## 1. Credit vs. Debit Side Logic (Remember: Current Account = Equity!)

Because a Partner’s Current Account represents **undrawn profits owed by the firm to the partner** (Owners’ Equity → normal **Credit** balance):
- Anything that **INCREASES** what the partnership owes the partner → **CREDIT (`+`)**!
- Anything that **DECREASES** what the partnership owes the partner → **DEBIT (`−`)**!

| DEBIT Side (`−` Taken Out / Owed by Partner) | CREDIT Side (`+` Earned by Partner) |
| :--- | :--- |
| • Opening `Balance b/d` *(ONLY if partner started **OVERDRAWN**!)* | • Opening `Balance b/d` *(Normal positive undrawn profit)* |
| • **Drawings** *(Cash OR Goods taken for personal use)* | • **Interest on Capital** *(from Appropriation A/c)* |
| • **Interest on Drawings** *(from Appropriation A/c)* | • **Partner’s Salary** *(unpaid portion — see note below!)* |
| • **Share of Residual LOSS** *(if Appropriations &gt; Profit)* | • **Share of Residual PROFIT** *(from Appropriation A/c)* |
| • *Closing `Balance c/d`* *(when Cr &gt; Dr → normal positive Cr b/d!)* | • **Unpaid Interest on Partner’s Loan** *(if left in Current A/c)* |
| | • *Closing `Balance c/d`* *(when Dr &gt; Cr → **OVERDRAWN Dr b/d**!)* |

### Exam Pro Tip: Watch Out for "Partner’s Salary Already Paid in Cash"!
Check the wording carefully:
- **Case A — Salary NOT yet paid in cash *(most common)*:** Credit the **FULL** Partner Salary to the partner’s Current Account!
- **Case B — Salary ALREADY withdrawn in cash** *(e.g. Partner took `$4,000` of a `$12,000` salary by cheque)*:
  - Either Credit ONLY the **unpaid `$8,000`** to Current A/c *(if the `$4,000` was posted `Dr Partner Salary / Cr Bank`)*, **OR** Credit the full `$12,000` AND include the `$4,000` inside Drawings on the Debit side *(net `+$8,000` — identical closing balance!)*!

### Meaning of the 1 January 2027 Closing Balances *(2 marks)*
- **Maya (`$13,400` Credit Balance):** Maya left **`$13,400`** of undrawn earnings inside the business — the partnership owes Maya `$13,400` on top of her Fixed Capital *(ADDED on the Balance Sheet)* *(1 mark)*.
- **Liam (`$2,250` Debit Balance):** Liam is **overdrawn by `$2,250`** because his opening debit + drawings + interest on drawings (`$12,750`) exceeded his total credits (`$10,500`) — Liam owes `$2,250` back to the firm *(SUBTRACTED in brackets on the Balance Sheet!)* *(1 mark)*.

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Credit Current A/c (`+`):** Int. on Capital, Salary, Share of Profit, Unpaid Loan Interest.
- **Debit Current A/c (`−`):** Drawings, Int. on Drawings, Share of Loss.
- Check EACH partner’s column separately — **Credit `b/d` = positive equity**; **Debit `b/d` = OVERDRAWN**!
:::
