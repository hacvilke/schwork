:::info Essential Understanding
When partners start a business, they don’t always invest only cash — Partner A might bring in **cash + a delivery van + inventory**, while Partner B brings in **equipment + receivables minus a creditor bill**! Even more importantly, partnerships must choose between two ways to track owner equity over time: the **Fixed Capital System** *(2 separate accounts per partner — preferred!)* or the **Fluctuating Capital System** *(1 combined account per partner)*. Let’s master both cleanly!
:::

## 1. Opening Journal Entries When Forming a Partnership

When a partner invests **Cash + Non-Cash Assets** (and optionally transfers existing **Liabilities** into the new partnership):
- **Debit** every **Asset** contributed at its **agreed valuation** *(NOT necessarily its old historical cost!)*
- **Credit** any **Liability** the partnership agrees to take over
- **Credit** the **Partner’s Capital Account** with the **Net Value (`Total Assets − Liabilities`)**!

<FormulaCard
  title="Opening Capital Contribution Entry (General Journal)"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Always use the AGREED revaluation figure when a partner brings in existing vehicles, equipment, or stock!"
>

- **Dr** Bank / Cash *(cash invested)*
- **Dr** Motor Vehicles / Equipment / Inventory / Trade Receivables *(at agreed value)*
- **Cr** Trade Payables / Bank Loan *(any liability assumed by the firm)*
- **Cr** **Partner’s Capital Account** *(Net Capital = Assets − Liabilities)*

</FormulaCard>

## 2. Fixed Capital System vs. Fluctuating Capital System

| Feature | Fixed Capital System *(Preferred — 2 Accounts!)* | Fluctuating Capital System *(1 Combined Account)* |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Accounts per Partner** | **2:** `Capital Account` + `Current Account` | **1:** Single `Capital Account` only |
| **What goes into Capital A/c?** | **ONLY permanent Capital** *(opening capital + permanent additions/withdrawals)* | **EVERYTHING** *(capital, interest on capital, salary, profit share, drawings, interest on drawings)* |
| **Does Capital Balance change yearly?** | **NO — stays FIXED!** | **YES — fluctuates up or down every year!** |
| **Can it turn into a Debit balance?** | Capital stays **Credit**; **Current A/c** can turn **Debit** *(overdrawn!)* | Combined Capital could shrink sharply if heavy losses/drawings occur |
| **Why Accountants Prefer Fixed** | 1. Permanent original capital stays crystal clear<br/>2. Easy to compute **Interest on Capital** &amp; **Capital Ratios**<br/>3. Immediately flags if a partner **overdraws** beyond profit! | Simpler to keep only 1 account, but hides overdrawings inside permanent capital |

### 2(a). Fixed Capital System *(2 Accounts per Partner — 5 marks)*

**Partners’ Capital Accounts *(Fixed — Unchanged!)***

| Dr (31 Dec) | Singh ($) | Reid ($) | Cr (1 Jan) | Singh ($) | Reid ($) |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Balance c/d | **50,000** | **35,000** | Opening Capital | 50,000 | 35,000 |
| | | | **Jan 1, 2027 Bal b/d** | **50,000** | **35,000** |

**Partners’ Current Accounts *(Yearly Activity)***

| Dr (2026) | Singh ($) | Reid ($) | Cr (2026) | Singh ($) | Reid ($) |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Drawings | 7,000 | 8,250 | Interest on Capital | 2,500 | 1,750 |
| **Dec 31 Bal c/d** | **5,000** | **9,000** | Partner Salary | — | 6,000 |
| | | | Share of Profit | 9,500 | 9,500 |
| **Totals** | **12,000** | **17,250** | **Totals** | **12,000** | **17,250** |
| | | | **Jan 1, 2027 Bal b/d** | **5,000** | **9,000** |

### 2(b). Fluctuating Capital System *(1 Combined Account — 5 marks)*

**Partners’ Capital Accounts *(Fluctuating)***

| Dr (2026) | Singh ($) | Reid ($) | Cr (2026) | Singh ($) | Reid ($) |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Drawings | 7,000 | 8,250 | Jan 1 Opening Capital | 50,000 | 35,000 |
| **Dec 31 Bal c/d** | **55,000** | **44,000** | Interest on Capital | 2,500 | 1,750 |

---

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Opening Net Capital** = `Agreed Assets Contributed − Liabilities Assumed`.
- **Fixed System** = **Capital A/c** *(permanent)* + **Current A/c** *(yearly rewards &amp; drawings)*.
- **Fluctuating System** = **1 combined Capital A/c** — same Total Equity, less visibility!
:::
