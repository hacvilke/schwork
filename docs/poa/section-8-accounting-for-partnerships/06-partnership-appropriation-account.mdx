:::info Essential Understanding
In a Sole Trader business, the Income Statement ends right at **Net Profit** because 100% belongs to one person. In a Partnership, you add one crisp extension directly underneath Net Profit called the **Profit and Loss Appropriation Account** — showing step by step how Net Profit is adjusted for **Interest on Drawings**, **Interest on Capital**, and **Partner Salaries**, before dividing the final **Residual Profit** among the partners!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Vertical **Profit and Loss Appropriation Account** structure (`Net Profit + Interest on Drawings − Interest on Capital − Partner Salaries = Residual Profit Split`) |
| **Exam Focus** | **EXCLUDING Drawings itself** AND **EXCLUDING Interest on a Partner’s Loan** (which belongs in the Income Statement BEFORE Net Profit!), and calculating **pro-rata Interest on Drawings** accurately |
| **Learning Outcome** | Prepare a clean, properly titled vertical Profit and Loss Appropriation Account from any Partnership Agreement and trial data |

## 1. Exact Vertical Format of the Appropriation Account

<FormulaCard
  title="Profit & Loss Appropriation Account Formula (Exact Order!)"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Start with Net Profit (AFTER Partner Loan Interest!), ADD Interest on Drawings, DEDUCT Interest on Capital & Salaries, then SPLIT Residual Profit!"
>

| Step | Appropriation Line | Sign | Double-Entry Counterpart |
| :-: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | **Net Profit for the Year** *(after Partner’s Loan Interest!)* | Start | Transferred from Income Statement |
| **2** | **Add: Interest on Drawings** *(Partner A + Partner B)* | **`+`** | **DEBIT** Partners’ Current Accounts |
| **3** | **Less: Interest on Capital** *(Partner A + Partner B)* | **`−`** | **CREDIT** Partners’ Current Accounts |
| **4** | **Less: Partner Salaries / Commission** | **`−`** | **CREDIT** Partner’s Current Account |
| **5** | **= Residual Profit (or Loss) Shared in Ratio** *(A + B)* | **`= 0`** | **CREDIT** *(Profit)* / **DEBIT** *(Loss)* Current Accounts |

</FormulaCard>

### Two Things That NEVER Enter the Appropriation Account!
1. **Drawings Itself** *(e.g. `$12,000` cash taken by a partner)* → goes **ONLY** to the Debit side of the partner’s **Current Account**! Only **Interest on Drawings** enters the Appropriation Account!
2. **Interest on a Partner’s Loan** → deducted as an **Operating Expense in the Income Statement** BEFORE arriving at Net Profit! *(If a question gives "Profit BEFORE Loan Interest", subtract Loan Interest FIRST!)*

### Quick Workings *(Show Clearly!)*
- **Interest on Alisha’s Loan *(Income Stmt Expense)*:** `8% × $15,000` = **`$1,200`** → **True Net Profit** = `$55,200 − $1,200` = **`$54,000`** *(2 marks)*
- **Interest on Drawings *(6 months: 1 Jul – 31 Dec = `6/12`)*:**
  - Alisha: `$12,000 × 10% × 6/12` = **`$600`**
  - Brent: `$8,000 × 10% × 6/12` = **`$400`** *(2 marks)*
- **Interest on Capital (`10%`):** Alisha = **`$6,000`**; Brent = **`$4,000`** *(2 marks)*

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Vertical Order:** `Net Profit` **`+`** `Int. on Drawings` **`−`** `Int. on Capital` **`−`** `Partner Salaries` **`=`** `Residual Split`!
- **NEVER include** **Drawings principal** OR **Partner’s Loan Interest** inside the Appropriation Account!
:::
