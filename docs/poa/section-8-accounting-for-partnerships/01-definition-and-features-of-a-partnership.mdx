## 1. Definition & Core Features of a Partnership

A **Partnership** is legally defined as:
> **The relation which subsists between persons (typically 2 to 20) carrying on a business in common with a view to making a profit.** *(Note: Professional firms such as accounting, law, medical, and architectural partnerships are legally permitted to exceed 20 partners.)*

### Five Core Features of an Ordinary (General) Partnership

| Feature | What It Means in Practice | Accounting / Legal Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Two to Twenty Owners** | Formed by at least **2** and normally up to **20** individuals | Separate **Capital** &amp; **Current Accounts** are kept for **each partner** |
| **2. Unlimited Joint &amp; Several Liability** | General partners are **personally responsible** for ALL business debts — creditors can claim personal homes/savings if firm assets fall short! | "Jointly" = together; "Severally" = creditors can even pursue ONE wealthy partner for the full debt! |
| **3. Mutual Agency** | Every general partner acts as an **agent** of the firm — a contract signed by Partner A in the ordinary course of business legally binds Partner B &amp; Partner C too! | Requires strong trust and clear written partner authority limits |
| **4. No Separate Legal Entity (Limited Life)** | Unlike a Limited Liability Company, the partnership is legally inseparable from its owners | Death, bankruptcy, or retirement of a partner legally **dissolves** the old partnership (unless a deed provides for continuation) |
| **5. Profit &amp; Loss Sharing** | Profits (and losses!) are divided using a **Profit &amp; Loss Appropriation Account** | Guided by the **Partnership Agreement** — or **equally** if no agreement exists! |

## 2. Three Main Types of Partners (Do Not Confuse Sleeping vs. Limited!)

| Type of Partner | Invests Capital? | Manages Daily Business? | Shares Profits &amp; Losses? | Liability for Firm Debts |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **1. General (Active / Ordinary) Partner** | **Yes** | **Yes** *(active)* | **Yes** | **UNLIMITED** *(personal assets at risk!)* |
| **2. Sleeping (Dormant) Partner** | **Yes** | **No** *(silent investor)* | **Yes** | **UNLIMITED** *(still personally liable!)* |
| **3. Limited Partner** *(in a registered Limited Partnership)* | **Yes** *(fixed sum)* | **No** *(legally forbidden from daily management!)* | **Yes** | **LIMITED** *(only up to agreed capital invested!)* |

*(Note: Every **Limited Partnership** MUST still have at least **one General Partner** who carries unlimited liability and runs the business! You may also meet a **Nominal Partner** — someone who lends their prestigious name to the firm without investing capital, yet can still face liability to outsiders who relied on their name!)*

## 5. Worked Example

<WorkedExample
  title="Classifying Partners & Calculating Liability Exposure on Insolvency"
  marks={10}
  problem={
    <>
      <p>
        <strong>Scenario:</strong> <strong>Tropical Dental &amp; Supply Partners</strong> is a
        registered Limited Partnership with three partners:
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Dr. Simone Grant:</strong> Invested $30,000; runs the clinic and manages staff daily.</li>
        <li><strong>Mr. Kevin Blake:</strong> Invested $25,000; does not work in the clinic and is NOT registered as a limited partner (he is an ordinary silent partner).</li>
        <li><strong>Ms. Nadia Chin:</strong> Invested $20,000; legally registered as a Limited Partner and takes no part in daily operations.</li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        Due to a sudden market downturn, the firm closes down with <strong>Total Assets realizing $15,000</strong>
        and <strong>Total Trade Liabilities of $45,000</strong> (leaving an unpaid debt shortfall of
        <strong> $30,000</strong>).
      </p>
      <ol>
        <li>Classify each partner (Simone, Kevin, and Nadia) by exact partner type. <em>[3 marks]</em></li>
        <li>Explain the exact personal financial liability of <strong>each partner</strong> for the remaining <strong>$30,000</strong> debt shortfall. <em>[5 marks]</em></li>
        <li>State <strong>two reasons</strong> why a Limited Partnership must always have at least one General Partner. <em>[2 marks]</em></li>
      </ol>
    </>
  }
>

**1. Exact Partner Classifications (3 marks — 1 mark each):**
- **Dr. Simone Grant:** **General (Active) Partner** (invests capital + manages daily business).
- **Mr. Kevin Blake:** **Sleeping (Dormant) Partner** (invests capital, inactive in management, NOT registered as limited).
- **Ms. Nadia Chin:** **Limited Partner** (formally registered under the Limited Partnership Act; inactive in daily operations).

**2. Personal Liability for the `$30,000` Shortfall (`$45,000 Debts − $15,000 Assets`, 5 marks):**
- **Ms. Nadia Chin (Limited Partner):** Loses ONLY her original `$20,000` capital invested in the firm — she pays **`$0`** out of her personal savings/assets toward the `$30,000` shortfall *(2 marks)*.
- **Dr. Simone Grant (General) &amp; Mr. Kevin Blake (Sleeping):** BOTH carry **unlimited joint and several liability**! Even though Kevin never managed daily operations, he is NOT a registered Limited Partner — so creditors can claim personal assets from Simone and/or Kevin to recover the entire **`$30,000`** unpaid balance *(3 marks)*.

**3. Why At Least One General Partner Is Legally Required (2 marks):**
1. **Management authority:** Limited partners are legally barred from running daily operations — at least one General Partner is needed to manage the firm and sign binding contracts *(1 mark)*.
2. **Creditor protection:** At least one partner must remain fully personally liable (unlimited liability) so outside creditors and suppliers have legal security when lending to the firm *(1 mark)*.

</WorkedExample>

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Definition:** **2–20 persons** carrying on a business in common **with a view to profit** (higher for regulated professions).
- **Sleeping Partner** = **UNLIMITED** liability; **Registered Limited Partner** = **LIMITED** liability (cannot manage!).
- **Joint &amp; Several Liability** + **Mutual Agency** = core legal risks of general partnerships!
:::
