## 1. Essential Components of a Partnership Agreement (Deed)

While a partnership *can* legally exist on a handshake (oral agreement), smart partners always draft a written **Partnership Agreement (Deed)** covering:
1. **Business Identity &amp; Purpose:** Firm name, address, nature of business, and start date.
2. **Capital Contributions:** Exact cash and/or non-cash assets each partner invests (and whether Capital is **Fixed** or **Fluctuating**).
3. **Profit and Loss Sharing Ratio:** E.g., `3 : 2`, capital ratio, or percentage split.
4. **Interest on Capital:** Agreed `%` rate *(e.g. `5%` or `10%` p.a.)* to reward partners who invested larger capital sums.
5. **Partner Salaries / Commission:** Agreed annual salary for active partners who devote full-time management effort.
6. **Drawings Limits &amp; Interest on Drawings:** Maximum cash/goods partners may withdraw each year and penalty `%` charged on Drawings to discourage draining business cash!
7. **Interest on Partners’ Loans:** Interest rate paid when a partner lends extra money to the firm *over and above* their Capital.
8. **Admission, Retirement, Valuation of Goodwill &amp; Dissolution:** Clear rules for welcoming a new partner, buying out a retiring/deceased partner’s share, and settling disputes.

---

## 2. The 5 Automatic Partnership Act Default Rules (Memorize Cold!)

Whenever **NO Partnership Agreement exists** *(or a written agreement fails to mention one of these items)*, the **Partnership Act** steps in automatically with these **5 strict defaults**:

| Clause | Default Rule Under Partnership Act *(No Agreement)* | Exam Memory Cue |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Profit &amp; Loss Sharing** | **EQUAL (`50 / 50` or `1 : 1 : 1`)** | Even if Partner A put in 90% of the Capital! |
| **2. Interest on Capital** | **NONE (`0%`)** | Zero reward for larger capital! |
| **3. Partner Salaries** | **NONE (`$0`)** | Zero salary even if one partner works full-time! |
| **4. Interest on Drawings** | **NONE (`0%`)** | Zero penalty on withdrawals! |
| **5. Interest on Partner’s LOAN** | **`5%` per annum AUTOMATICALLY!** | **THE ONLY NON-ZERO DEFAULT!** Deducted in the **Income Statement**! |

## 5. Worked Example

<WorkedExample
  title="Applying Partnership Act Default Rules When No Written Agreement Exists"
  marks={10}
  problem={
    <>
      <p>
        <strong>Scenario:</strong> <strong>Devon</strong> and <strong>Ria</strong> launched a
        hardware partnership on <strong>1 January 2026</strong> on a verbal handshake —
        <strong> without signing a written Partnership Agreement</strong>:
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Capital Invested:</strong> Devon $80,000; Ria $20,000.</li>
        <li><strong>Partner’s Loan:</strong> On 1 January 2026, Devon also lent the business an extra <strong>$20,000</strong> as a long-term loan.</li>
        <li><strong>Time Worked:</strong> Devon worked 50 hours/week; Ria worked 10 hours/week.</li>
        <li><strong>Drawings during 2026:</strong> Devon $8,000; Ria $4,000.</li>
        <li><strong>Operating Profit (BEFORE Loan Interest) for 2026:</strong> <strong>$41,000</strong>.</li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        At year-end, Devon demands: (i) 10% Interest on Capital, (ii) $15,000 Salary, (iii) an 80:20
        profit split, and (iv) 10% Interest on his $20,000 loan. Ria refuses.
      </p>
      <ol>
        <li>Rule on EACH of Devon’s four demands under the <strong>Partnership Act</strong>. <em>[4 marks]</em></li>
        <li>Calculate the firm’s true <strong>Net Profit</strong> and exact <strong>profit distribution</strong> for Devon and Ria. <em>[4 marks]</em></li>
        <li>Calculate the closing <strong>Current Account balance</strong> of each partner on 31 December 2026 (assuming unpaid loan interest is credited to Devon’s Current Account). <em>[2 marks]</em></li>
      </ol>
    </>
  }
>

**1. Partnership Act Ruling on Devon’s Four Demands (4 marks — 1 mark each):**
- **(i) 10% Interest on Capital:** **DENIED (`$0`)** — Partnership Act allows **NO Interest on Capital** without an agreement.
- **(ii) `$15,000` Salary:** **DENIED (`$0`)** — Partnership Act allows **NO Partner Salaries** without an agreement.
- **(iii) 80 : 20 Profit Split:** **DENIED** — Partnership Act mandates **EQUAL sharing (`50 : 50`)** regardless of capital differences!
- **(iv) 10% Interest on `$20,000` Loan:** **REDUCED TO `5%`** — Without an agreed rate, the Partnership Act grants **`5%` per annum** automatically!

**2. True Net Profit & Profit Distribution (4 marks):**
- **Interest on Devon’s Loan (Income Statement Expense):** `5% × $20,000` = **`$1,000`** *(1 mark)*
- **True Net Profit for Appropriation:** `$41,000 − $1,000` = **`$40,000`** *(1 mark)*
- **Share of Profit (`50 : 50` Equal):**
  - **Devon:** `1/2 × $40,000` = **`$20,000`** *(1 mark)*
  - **Ria:** `1/2 × $40,000` = **`$20,000`** *(1 mark)*

**3. Closing Current Account Balances at 31 December 2026 (2 marks):**
- **Devon:** Loan Interest `$1,000` + Share of Profit `$20,000` − Drawings `$8,000` = **`$13,000` (Cr)** *(1 mark)*
- **Ria:** Share of Profit `$20,000` − Drawings `$4,000` = **`$16,000` (Cr)** *(1 mark)*
*(Moral of the story: Even though Devon supplied 80% of the capital and worked 5× the hours, failing to sign a Partnership Agreement cost him thousands!)*

</WorkedExample>

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **No Agreement = Partnership Act Defaults:** **Equal** profit/loss · **`0%`** Int. on Capital · **`$0`** Salary · **`0%`** Int. on Drawings · **`5%`** Int. on Partner’s **LOAN**!
- **Partner’s Loan Interest** → **Income Statement Expense**; **Interest on Capital / Partner’s Salary** → **Appropriation Account**!
:::
