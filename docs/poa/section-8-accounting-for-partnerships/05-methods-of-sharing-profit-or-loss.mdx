:::info Essential Understanding
Once a partnership calculates its **Residual Profit** *(after deducting Interest on Capital and Partner Salaries, and adding Interest on Drawings)*, how do the partners divide what’s left? Three core methods dominate accounting exams: **Equal Sharing**, **Fixed Agreed Ratio (or Percentages)**, and **Capital Ratio**. And watch out for the favorite Paper 2 twist — **what happens when Salaries + Interest on Capital EXCEED Net Profit, creating a Residual LOSS?!**
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Splitting Residual Profit (or Residual Loss!) via **Equal Split**, **Fixed Agreed Ratio / Percentages**, or **Capital Ratio** |
| **Exam Focus** | Simplifying Capital Ratios cleanly (e.g. `$60k : $40k : $20k` → `3 : 2 : 1`), verifying partner shares sum to `100%` of the pool, and handling a **Residual Loss** accurately! |
| **Learning Outcome** | Calculate each partner’s exact share of Residual Profit or Residual Loss under every syllabus profit-sharing method |

## 1. The Three Syllabus Profit-Sharing Methods

<FormulaCard
  title="Universal Partner Share Formula"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Always sum the ratio parts first — then check that all partners' dollar shares add up to the exact Residual Profit or Loss!"
>

$$
\text{Partner’s Share} = \frac{\text{Partner’s Ratio Parts}}{\text{Total Ratio Parts}} \times \text{Residual Profit (or Residual Loss)}
$$

</FormulaCard>

| Method | How It Works | Best Used When… | Quick Example (`$36,000` Pool) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Equal Sharing** | Divide evenly by number of partners (`÷ 2`, `÷ 3`, etc.) | **Default under Partnership Act** when no deed exists, OR when partners contribute equal effort &amp; capital | 3 partners: `$36,000 ÷ 3` = **`$12,000` each** |
| **2. Fixed Agreed Ratio / %** | Split by custom ratio *(e.g. `5 : 3 : 1` or `50% : 35% : 15%`)* stated in the Partnership Deed | One partner brings senior expertise, key clients, or longer working hours | `5 : 3 : 1` (9 parts): `$20,000` / `$12,000` / `$4,000` |
| **3. Capital Ratio** | Split strictly in proportion to each partner’s **Fixed Capital Account balance** | Capital investment is the main driver of business earnings *(e.g. property/equipment firms)* | `$60k : $40k : $20k` = `3 : 2 : 1` (6 parts): `$18,000` / `$12,000` / `$6,000` |

### The "Residual Loss" Rule (Top-Band Paper 2 Skill!)
Suppose a partnership earns **`$15,000` Net Profit**, yet the Partnership Agreement awards **`$12,000` Interest on Capital** plus **`$9,000` Partner Salary** = **`$21,000` total appropriations**:
- **Do you cancel the Salary or Interest on Capital?** **NO!** *(Unless the deed explicitly says otherwise!)*
- You STILL credit the full `$12,000` Interest on Capital and `$9,000` Salary, leaving a **Residual LOSS of `($6,000)`** (`$15,000 − $21,000`).
- That **`($6,000)` Residual Loss** is then **split in the EXACT SAME profit-sharing ratio** and **DEBITED** to each partner’s Current Account!

### Part A: Comparing 4 Profit-Sharing Methods on `$36,000` Residual Profit *(8 marks — 2 marks each)*

| Method | Ratio / Fractions | Maya ($) | Devon ($) | Priya ($) | Total Check |
| :--- | :---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **1. Equal Split** | `1/3 : 1/3 : 1/3` | 12,000 | 12,000 | 12,000 | **$36,000 ✓** |
| **2. Fixed Ratio (`5:3:1`)** | `5/9 : 3/9 : 1/9` | 20,000 | 12,000 | 4,000 | **$36,000 ✓** |
| **3. Capital Ratio** *(`$60k:$40k:$20k`)* | `3/6 : 2/6 : 1/6` | 18,000 | 12,000 | 6,000 | **$36,000 ✓** |
| **4. Percentages** | `50% : 35% : 15%` | 18,000 | 12,600 | 5,400 | **$36,000 ✓** |

---

### Part B: Handling a Residual Loss (`Net Profit = $15,000` — 4 marks)
1. **Total Priority Appropriations:**
   - Interest on Capital (`10%`): Maya `$6,000` + Devon `$4,000` + Priya `$2,000` = **`$12,000`**
   - Partner Salary (Priya): **`$9,000`**
   - *Total Appropriations* = `$12,000 + $9,000` = **`$21,000`** *(1 mark)*
2. **Residual Loss:** `$15,000 Net Profit − $21,000 Appropriations` = **`($6,000)` Residual Loss** *(1 mark)*
3. **Share of Residual Loss (`3 : 2 : 1` Capital Ratio — DEBITED to Current Accounts):**
   - Maya (`3/6 × −$6,000`) = **`($3,000)`**; Devon (`2/6`) = **`($2,000)`**; Priya (`1/6`) = **`($1,000)`** *(1 mark)*
4. **Final Net Total Credited to Each Partner *(Proof Check — 1 mark)*:**
   - **Maya:** `$6,000 − $3,000` = **`+$3,000`**
   - **Devon:** `$4,000 − $2,000` = **`+$2,000`**
   - **Priya:** `$2,000 + $9,000 − $1,000` = **`+$10,000`**
   - *Grand Total:* `$3,000 + $2,000 + $10,000` = **`$15,000`** *(matches Net Profit exactly!)*

</WorkedExample>
