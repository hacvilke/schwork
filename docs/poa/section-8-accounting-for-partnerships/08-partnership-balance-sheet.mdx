:::info Essential Understanding
You have reached the **Section 8 Capstone**! Here’s the best news in Partnership Accounting: the **entire top half** of a Partnership Balance Sheet (**Non-Current Assets**, **Current Assets**, **Current Liabilities**, and **Working Capital**) is **100% IDENTICAL** to a Sole Trader Balance Sheet! Only the **"Financed By"** bottom half upgrades — cleanly displaying each partner’s **Fixed Capital Account**, each partner’s **Current Account** *(with overdrawn Debit balances subtracted in brackets!)*, and any **Partner’s Loan** under **Non-Current Liabilities**!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Full vertical **Partnership Balance Sheet**, focusing on the **"Financed By"** block: **Fixed Capital Accounts**, **Closing Current Accounts (`+` Cr / `−` Dr)**, and **Non-Current Liabilities (including Partner’s Loan!)** |
| **Exam Focus** | Subtracting **Debit (overdrawn) Current Account balances in brackets**, placing **Partner’s Loans under Non-Current Liabilities (NOT Capital!)**, and confirming **`Total Net Assets = Total Capital Employed`** |
| **Learning Outcome** | Construct a complete, balanced vertical Partnership Balance Sheet seamlessly integrated with an Appropriation Account and Partners’ Current Accounts |

## 1. Sole Trader vs. Partnership Balance Sheet — What Changes?

| Balance Sheet Section | Sole Trader | Partnership |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Non-Current Assets** *(Cost, Accum. Depr., NBV)* | Same 3 columns | **100% Identical!** |
| **Current Assets − Current Liabilities = Working Capital** | Same layout | **100% Identical!** *(watch unpaid Loan Interest if put in CL instead of Current A/c)* |
| **Financed By — Capital** | Single Capital (`Open + NP − Dr`) | **Fixed Capital Account listed for EACH partner** *(A + B = Total Fixed Capital)* |
| **Financed By — Current Accounts** | *(Not used)* | **Closing Current Account for EACH partner** *(Credit = `+`; Overdrawn Debit = `($X)` subtracted!)* |
| **Financed By — Non-Current Liabilities** | Bank Loans / Mortgages | **Partner’s Loan(s)** + Bank Loans / Mortgages! |

### Two Guaranteed Balance Sheet Exam Checks
1. **Partner’s Loan Placement:** A Partner’s Loan is a **long-term debt owed by the firm**, NOT permanent Capital — ALWAYS place it under **Non-Current Liabilities** (so on dissolution it ranks ahead of partner Capital!).
2. **Unpaid Loan Interest Double-Check:** If unpaid Loan Interest (`$900`) was **already credited inside the Partner’s Current Account**, DO **NOT** list it again under Current Liabilities — otherwise your Balance Sheet will be out of balance by `$900`!
