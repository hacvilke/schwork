:::info Essential Understanding
Under the **Objectivity (Verifiability)** principle, no transaction can enter the accounting books on hearsay alone. **Source documents** — such as **Invoices, Credit Notes, Debit Notes, Official Receipts, Cheque Counterfoils, Bank Deposit Slips, and Petty Cash Vouchers** — provide dated, numbered, verifiable proof of every dollar earned, owed, returned, or paid.
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Purpose, essential fields, and exact calculation layout of **Invoices, Credit Notes, Debit Notes, Official Receipts, and Vouchers** |
| **Exam Focus** | Completing a **partially filled Invoice or Credit Note** accurately (multiplying Qty × Price, subtracting Trade Discount, computing Net Total) |
| **Learning Outcome** | Prepare accurate Invoices, Credit Notes, and Receipts and explain the legal/accounting role of every document |

## 1. Essential Source Documents & Their Roles

| Source Document | Who Issues It? | Purpose | Key Details Included |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Invoice** | **Seller** → Buyer | Requests payment for goods/services sold on **credit** | Seller letterhead, Buyer name, Invoice #, Date, Payment Terms (`2/10, n/30`), Qty × Unit Price, Less Trade Discount, **Net Total**, `E. & O.E.` |
| **Credit Note** | **Seller** → Buyer | **Reduces** the amount the buyer owes (due to returned goods, damaged items, or an invoice overcharge) | Printed often in red/distinct header, Credit Note #, Original Invoice #, Reason for return/allowance, Net reduction amount |
| **Debit Note** | **Buyer** → Seller *(or Seller → Buyer)* | Buyer requests a Credit Note for returned/damaged goods; OR Seller increases buyer’s bill for an **undercharge** | Debit Note #, Reference Invoice #, Item details, Reason for adjustment |
| **Official Receipt** | **Payee** (Receiver) → Payer | Written proof that **cash or cheque payment** was received | Receipt #, Date, Payer’s name, Amount in **both words and figures**, Payment method (Cash/Cheque #), Signature |
| **Cheque Counterfoil (Stub)** | Retained by **Payer** | Payer’s permanent stub record of a cheque written from the business bank account | Cheque #, Date, Payee name, Purpose, Amount |
| **Bank Pay-In (Deposit) Slip** | Stamped by **Bank** | Proves cash/cheques were deposited into the business bank account | Account #, Date, Cash & Cheque breakdown, Total Deposit, Teller stamp |
| **Petty Cash Voucher** | Prepared by **Petty Cashier** | Authorizes and proves a small cash payout from the petty cash box | Voucher #, Date, Description of expense, Amount, Recipient & Supervisor signatures |

:::note What Does "E. & O.E." Mean — And What About a Statement of Account?
- **`E. & O.E.`** stands for **"Errors and Omissions Excepted"** — it reserves the seller’s legal right to correct honest arithmetic mistakes found later on an invoice.
- A **Statement of Account** is a monthly summary sent by a seller listing opening balance, all invoices, credit notes, payments, and closing amount owed. It is a **reminder/reconciliation document** — NOT a source document for recording new sales!
:::

<WorkedExample
  title="Preparing a Complete Invoice & Matching Credit Note (With Trade Discount)"
  marks={10}
  problem={
    <>
      <p>
        <strong>Scenario:</strong> On <strong>8 March 2026</strong>, <strong>Caribbean Office
        Supply Ltd.</strong> sold the following items on credit (Invoice #<strong>INV-312</strong>,
        Terms: <code>2/10, Net 30</code>) to <strong>BrightStart Academy</strong> with a
        <strong> 15% Trade Discount</strong>:
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>10 Whiteboards @ $80 each</li>
        <li>20 Packs Dry-Erase Markers @ $10 each</li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        On <strong>11 March 2026</strong>, BrightStart Academy returned <strong>2 cracked
        Whiteboards</strong> and Caribbean Office Supply issued <strong>Credit Note #CN-44</strong>.
      </p>
      <ol>
        <li>Prepare <strong>Invoice #INV-312</strong> showing the Gross Total, Trade Discount, and Net Amount Due. <em>[6 marks]</em></li>
        <li>Calculate and present the net value of <strong>Credit Note #CN-44</strong>. <em>[4 marks]</em></li>
      </ol>
    </>
  }
>

**1. Invoice #INV-312 (6 marks):**

> **CARIBBEAN OFFICE SUPPLY LTD.** — *INVOICE*
> **To:** BrightStart Academy &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Invoice #:** INV-312 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Date:** 8 March 2026 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Terms:** 2/10, Net 30 *(1 mark)*

| Qty | Description | Unit Price ($) | Amount ($) |
| :---: | :--- | ---: | ---: |
| 10 | Whiteboards | 80.00 | 800.00 |
| 20 | Packs Dry-Erase Markers | 10.00 | 200.00 |
| | **Gross Total (List Price)** *(1 mark)* | | **1,000.00** |
| | Less: 15% Trade Discount *(15% × $1,000)* *(2 marks)* | | (150.00) |
| | **Net Amount Payable** *(1 mark)* | | **$850.00** |

*E. & O.E.* *(1 mark)*

---

**2. Credit Note #CN-44 dated 11 March 2026 (4 marks):**
- Gross value of 2 returned whiteboards: `2 × $80.00` = **$160.00** *(1 mark)*
- **Less: 15% Trade Discount** *(must match original invoice!)*: `15% × $160.00` = **($24.00)** *(2 marks)*
- **Net Credit Note Amount:** `$160.00 − $24.00` = **$136.00** *(1 mark)*

*(Check: BrightStart Academy originally owed $850.00; after Credit Note #CN-44 of $136.00, BrightStart now owes `$850 − $136 = $714.00` — which equals 8 remaining whiteboards × $68 net ($544) + 20 marker packs × $8.50 net ($170) = **$714.00** ✓!)*

</WorkedExample>

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Invoice** = Credit sale/purchase (subtract **Trade Discount** to get Net Total).
- **Credit Note** = Seller reduces buyer’s debt (ALWAYS subtract the **same Trade Discount %** if one was given on the invoice!).
- **Receipt** = Proof of cash/cheque payment (words + figures + pre-numbered).
- **Statement of Account** = Monthly checkup ONLY — **never** record as a new entry!
:::
