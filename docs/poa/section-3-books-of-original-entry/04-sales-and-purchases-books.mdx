:::info Essential Understanding
The **Sales Journal**, **Purchases Journal**, **Returns Inwards Journal**, and **Returns Outwards Journal** are the workhorses of credit trading. Instead of making clunky individual ledger entries for every single invoice, you log each net credit invoice neatly in one line, post daily to each customer or supplier’s personal account, and post **one single monthly total** to the General Ledger!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Standard column layout, filtering rules, net trade-discount math, and exact month-end ledger transfers for **Sales, Purchases, and Returns Journals** |
| **Exam Focus** | Building complete Sales, Purchases, Returns Inwards, and Returns Outwards Journals from a mixed list of transactions (while filtering out cash & asset traps!) |
| **Learning Outcome** | Record credit trading transactions accurately in all four day books and state the exact double-entry postings to subsidiary and general ledgers |

## 1. Exact Format & Posting Rules of All Four Day Books

Every credit trading journal shares the exact same clean 5-column layout:
`Date | Details (Customer or Supplier Name) | Invoice / Credit Note # | Folio | Amount ($)`

*(When an invoice has multiple items or trade discounts, a **Details / Subtotal column** can sit right beside the **Total / Net Amount column** to show your math cleanly.)*

### Master Posting Reference Table

| Day Book | Individual Row Posting (Daily) | Month-End Total Posting (General Ledger) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sales Journal** | **DEBIT** each Customer’s A/c in the **Sales Ledger** | **CREDIT** Sales Account *(Transfer to Sales A/c)* |
| **Purchases Journal** | **CREDIT** each Supplier’s A/c in the **Purchases Ledger** | **DEBIT** Purchases Account *(Transfer to Purchases A/c)* |
| **Returns Inwards Journal** | **CREDIT** each Customer’s A/c in the **Sales Ledger** | **DEBIT** Returns Inwards Account |
| **Returns Outwards Journal** | **DEBIT** each Supplier’s A/c in the **Purchases Ledger** | **CREDIT** Returns Outwards Account |

:::warning The Three Things You MUST Filter OUT of Sales & Purchases Journals
Before writing a single line, cross out these three common exam decoys:
1. **Cash Sales & Cash Purchases** → belong in the **Cash Book**, NEVER in the Sales or Purchases Journals.
2. **Credit Purchases/Sales of Non-Current Assets** (e.g. buying a van or selling old office shelving on credit) → belong in the **General Journal**.
3. **Trade Discounts** → subtract them FIRST so only the **Net Amount** enters the Total column!
:::

| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | ---: |
| June 5 | D. Clarke | 210 | SL | 900 |
| June 22 | L. Reid *($1,200 − 15% of $1,200 = $1,200 − $180)* | 211 | SL | 1,020 |
| **June 30** | **Transfer to Sales Account (Cr)** | | **GL** | **1,920** |

**2. Purchases Journal** *(3 marks)*

| Date | Details | Inv # | Folio | Amount ($) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | ---: |
| June 2 | Vogue Wholesale *($2,000 − 10% of $2,000 = $2,000 − $200)* | 401 | PL | 1,800 |
| June 18 | Metro Apparel | 519 | PL | 1,100 |
| **June 30** | **Transfer to Purchases Account (Dr)** | | **GL** | **2,900** |

**3. Returns Inwards Journal** *(3 marks)*

| Date | Details | CN # | Folio | Amount ($) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | ---: |
| June 26 | D. Clarke | 08 | SL | 120 |
| **June 30** | **Transfer to Returns Inwards Account (Dr)** | | **GL** | **120** |

**4. Returns Outwards Journal** *(3 marks)*

| Date | Details | CN # | Folio | Amount ($) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | ---: |
| June 14 | Vogue Wholesale *($300 − 10% of $300 = $300 − $30)* | 12 | PL | 270 |
| **June 30** | **Transfer to Returns Outwards Account (Cr)** | | **GL** | **270** |

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Sales Journal** → Dr Customers (SL) / **Cr Sales A/c (GL)**.
- **Purchases Journal** → Cr Suppliers (PL) / **Dr Purchases A/c (GL)**.
- **Returns Inwards** → Cr Customers (SL) / **Dr Returns Inwards A/c (GL)**.
- **Returns Outwards** → Dr Suppliers (PL) / **Cr Returns Outwards A/c (GL)**.
- **Always filter out Cash Sales, Cash Purchases, and Credit Fixed Assets!**
:::
