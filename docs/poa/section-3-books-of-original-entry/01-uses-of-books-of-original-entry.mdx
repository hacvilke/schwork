:::info Essential Understanding
Imagine stuffing 500 daily sales invoices, cash receipts, and bills straight into the main General Ledger one by one — it would turn into an unreadable mess! **Books of Original Entry** (also called *Day Books* or *Books of Prime Entry*) act as **specialized sorting trays** where similar transactions are recorded chronologically FIRST, summarized into neat totals, and then posted cleanly to the ledgers.
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |

| **Learning Outcome** | Identify all seven Books of Original Entry, explain why businesses use them, and route any transaction accurately |

## 1. The Seven Books of Original Entry Master Guide

| Book of Original Entry | Exact Transactions Recorded | Source Document Used |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Sales Journal** *(Sales Day Book)* | **Credit sales of goods** held for resale ONLY | Sales Invoice (copy retained by seller) |
| **2. Purchases Journal** *(Purchases Day Book)* | **Credit purchases of goods** for resale ONLY | Purchase Invoice (received from supplier) |
| **3. Returns Inwards Journal** *(Sales Returns Book)* | Credit customers **returning goods** to us | Credit Note **issued** by us to customer |
| **4. Returns Outwards Journal** *(Purchases Returns Book)* | Goods we **return to credit suppliers** | Credit Note **received** from supplier |
| **5. Cash Book** *(2-column or 3-column)* | **ALL cash and bank** receipts & payments (including cash sales, cash purchases, bank transfers, and cash discounts) | Official Receipts, Cheque Counterfoils, Bank Deposit Slips |
| **6. Petty Cash Book** | **Small everyday cash expenses** (postage, stationery, bus fare, cleaning) | Signed Petty Cash Vouchers |
| **7. General Journal** *(The Journal)* | **Non-routine / unusual entries**: credit purchases/sales of **Non-Current Assets** (vans, machinery), opening entries, error corrections, bad debt write-offs, depreciation | Journal Vouchers, Asset Invoices, Management Memos |

### Why Businesses Use Books of Original Entry
1. **Reduces General Ledger Clutter:** Instead of posting 300 individual credit sales into the Sales Account, only **one single monthly total** gets posted.
2. **Division of Labour (Specialization):** One clerk can manage the Sales Journal while another updates the Purchases Journal and a third handles the Cash Book simultaneously.
3. **Chronological Audit Trail:** Every transaction is logged in date order with invoice/document numbers for quick checking.
4. **Easier Error Detection:** Grouping similar transactions lets accountants build Control Accounts (Section 7) and pinpoint discrepancies faster.

## 4. Worked Example

<WorkedExample
  title="Routing Eight Real Business Transactions to the Exact Book of Original Entry"
  marks={8}
  problem={
    <>
      <p>
        <strong>Question:</strong> State the exact <strong>Book of Original Entry</strong> in
        which each of the following eight transactions of <strong>Metro Sports Store</strong>
        should be recorded first. <em>[1 mark each = 8 marks]</em>
      </p>
      <ol>
        <li>Sold $1,800 of soccer jerseys on credit to Valley High School.</li>
        <li>Purchased $3,200 of basketballs for resale on credit from ProGear Wholesale.</li>
        <li>Purchased a $15,000 delivery van on credit from AutoCity Motors.</li>
        <li>Sold $450 of tennis rackets for immediate cash.</li>
        <li>Valley High School returned $200 of damaged soccer jerseys.</li>
        <li>Paid $14 from the office tin for courier postage stamps.</li>
        <li>Returned $350 of defective basketballs to ProGear Wholesale.</li>
        <li>Wrote off $180 owed by a bankrupt customer, T. Lewis, as a Bad Debt.</li>
      </ol>
    </>
  }
>

1. **Credit sale of soccer jerseys (resale goods):** **Sales Journal** *(1 mark)*
2. **Credit purchase of basketballs (resale goods):** **Purchases Journal** *(1 mark)*
3. **Credit purchase of a delivery van (Non-Current Asset!):** **General Journal** *(1 mark — NOT Purchases Journal!)*
4. **Immediate cash sale of tennis rackets:** **Cash Book** *(1 mark — NOT Sales Journal!)*
5. **Customer returns credit goods to us:** **Returns Inwards Journal** *(1 mark)*
6. **Minor $14 cash payment for stamps:** **Petty Cash Book** *(1 mark)*
7. **We return credit goods to our supplier:** **Returns Outwards Journal** *(1 mark)*
8. **Writing off a Bad Debt (non-routine adjustment):** **General Journal** *(1 mark)*

</WorkedExample>

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Goods for resale on credit** → Sales / Purchases / Returns Inwards / Returns Outwards Journals.
- **Any Cash or Bank movement** → **Cash Book** (small cash expenses → **Petty Cash Book**).
- **Credit purchase/sale of Fixed Assets, bad debts, error fixes, opening entries** → **General Journal**!
:::
