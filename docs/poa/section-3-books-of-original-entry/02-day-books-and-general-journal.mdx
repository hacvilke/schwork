## 1. The Four Credit Day Books (All Share the Same Simple Layout!)

Good news: the **Sales Journal**, **Purchases Journal**, **Returns Inwards Journal**, and **Returns Outwards Journal** all follow the **exact same clean column structure**:

> **Date** | **Details (Customer or Supplier Name)** | **Invoice / Note No.** | **Folio** | **Amount (J\$)**

*(Folio codes tell you which ledger page the account lives on: **`SL`** = Sales / Debtors Ledger, **`PL`** = Purchases / Creditors Ledger, **`GL`** = General Ledger!)*

## 3. How Day Book Totals Are Posted to the Ledgers (Don't Miss This!)

Every Day Book follows a super-logical two-step posting rule that completes double entry:

| Day Book | Individual Names Posted To... | Month-End Total Posted To... |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sales Journal** | **Dr** each customer's account in the **Sales Ledger** | **Cr** the **Sales Account** in the General Ledger |
| **Purchases Journal** | **Cr** each supplier's account in the **Purchases Ledger** | **Dr** the **Purchases Account** in the General Ledger |
| **Returns Inwards Journal** | **Cr** each customer's account in the **Sales Ledger** *(reduces what they owe)* | **Dr** the **Returns Inwards Account** in the General Ledger |
| **Returns Outwards Journal** | **Dr** each supplier's account in the **Purchases Ledger** *(reduces what we owe)* | **Cr** the **Returns Outwards Account** in the General Ledger |

## 4. The General Journal (Journal Proper)

Think of the **General Journal** as the "special instructions notebook" for transactions that do **not** fit into Cash, Petty Cash, or Credit Stock Day Books. Always write:
1. The **Debit** account on top (left-aligned),
2. The **Credit** account indented slightly below it, and
3. A short, clear **Narration** explaining *why* the entry was made!

### Common General Journal Entries
- Buying or selling a **Fixed Asset on credit** (e.g. machinery, computers, motor van)
- **Opening entries** when starting a business or a new set of books
- **Year-end adjustments** (Depreciation, Bad Debts written off, Provision for Doubtful Debts, Accruals & Prepayments)
- **Correction of errors** (Suspense Account entries)

**Example — Bought Office Equipment on credit from TechJa Ltd for J\$95,000 on May 25:**

| Date | Details | Folio | Dr (J\$) | Cr (J\$) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| May 25 | Office Equipment | GL 8 | 95,000 | |
| | &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TechJa Ltd | GL 15 | | 95,000 |
| | *(Being purchase of office equipment on credit from TechJa Ltd)* | | | |
