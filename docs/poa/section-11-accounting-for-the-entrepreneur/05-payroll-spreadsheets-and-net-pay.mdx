:::info Essential Understanding
Once you know every worker’s **Gross Pay**, you assemble the entire team into a **Payroll Spreadsheet Register** — subtracting **Statutory Deductions** (`PAYE`, `NIS`) and **Voluntary Deductions** (`Credit Union`, `Health Plan`, `Union Dues`) to compute exact **Net Pay (Take-Home Pay)**! Every payroll spreadsheet gives you an ironclad **Cross-Cast Check** (`Total Gross − Total Deductions = Total Net Pay`) AND feeds directly into ONE crisp **General Journal Payroll Entry**!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Building a **Payroll Spreadsheet Register** with exact **Excel Formulas** (`=B3+C3`, `=MAX(0,(D3-600)*30%)`, `=D3*3%`, `=SUM(E3:G3)`, `=D3-H3`), **Cross-Casting**, and the **Payroll General Journal Entry** |
| **Exam Focus** | Computing **Taxable Income (`Gross − Tax-Free Allowance`)** BEFORE multiplying by the PAYE `%`, cross-casting column totals, and ALWAYS debiting **GROSS Pay** (never Net Pay!) to `Wages & Salaries Expense` |
| **Learning Outcome** | Construct an accurate multi-employee Payroll Spreadsheet with spreadsheet formulas, verify cross-cast totals, and record the resulting General Journal entry |

## 1. Exact Payroll Formulas & General Journal Law

<FormulaCard
  title="Payroll Spreadsheet & Taxable Income Formulas"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Never charge PAYE on the tax-free allowance — subtract the threshold FIRST!"
>

$$
\text{Gross Pay} = \text{Basic Pay} + \text{Overtime / Bonus / Commission}
$$

$$
\text{Taxable Income} = \max\left(0,\; \text{Gross Pay} - \text{Tax-Free Threshold}\right) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{PAYE} = \text{Taxable Income} \times \text{Tax Rate \%}
$$

$$
\textbf{Net Pay (Take-Home Pay)} = \text{Gross Pay} - \text{Total Deductions (Statutory + Voluntary)}
$$

</FormulaCard>

### The #1 Payroll Accounting Rule (Journal Entry From Row Totals!)
When you post the Payroll Register Totals into the **General Journal**:
- **`Dr` Wages &amp; Salaries Expense** = **TOTAL GROSS PAY (`$3,000`)** *(The FULL cost of employing staff — NEVER Net Pay!)*
- **`Cr` PAYE Tax Payable** *(Current Liability until paid to Tax Office!)* = **`$360`**
- **`Cr` NIS Payable** *(Current Liability until paid to NIS!)* = **`$90`**
- **`Cr` Voluntary Deductions / Credit Union Payable** *(Current Liability!)* = **`$150`**
- **`Cr` Bank *(or Wages Payable)*** = **TOTAL NET PAY (`$2,400`)** *(Actual cash transferred to staff!)*
- **Check:** `$3,000 Dr` = `$360 + $90 + $150 + $2,400 = $3,000 Cr` ✓!

### 1. HarborCraft Services — Weekly Payroll Spreadsheet Register *(8 marks)*

| Row / Employee (`A`) | Basic (`B`) | OT (`C`) | **Gross (`D`)** | PAYE (`E`) | NIS (`F`) | Vol. (`G`) | **Tot. Ded (`H`)** | **Net Pay (`I`)** |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **3 · A. Brown** | 800 | 200 | **1,000** | 120 | 30 | 50 | **200** | **800** |
| **4 · M. Clarke** | 1,200 | 0 | **1,200** | 180 | 36 | 84 | **300** | **900** |
| **5 · T. Reid** | 640 | 160 | **800** | 60 | 24 | 16 | **100** | **700** |
| **6 · TOTALS** | **$2,640** | **$360** | **$3,000** | **$360** | **$90** | **$150** | **$600** | **$2,400** |

- *PAYE Workings (`30%` on excess above `$600`):* A. Brown `30% × $400 = $120`; M. Clarke `30% × $600 = $180`; T. Reid `30% × $200 = $60`.
- **Cross-Cast Verification:** Vertical Gross (`$3,000`) − Vertical Deductions (`$360 + $90 + $150 = $600`) = **`$2,400`** = Vertical Net Pay (`$800 + $900 + $700 = $2,400`) ✓!

### 2. Exact Spreadsheet Formulas *(3 marks — 0.5 mark each)*
- `D3` *(Gross Pay)*: `=B3+C3`
- `E3` *(PAYE with threshold protection)*: `=MAX(0,(D3-600)*0.30)` *(or `=IF(D3>600,(D3-600)*0.30,0)`)*
- `F3` *(NIS at 3%)*: `=D3*0.03`
- `H3` *(Total Deductions)*: `=SUM(E3:G3)`
- `I3` *(Net Pay)*: `=D3-H3`
- `I6` *(Total Net Pay)*: `=SUM(I3:I5)`

### 3. General Journal Entry for Week 12 Payroll *(3 marks)*

| Details &amp; Narration | Dr ($) | Cr ($) |

| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Bank *(Direct Deposit — NET Pay)* | | 2,400 |
| *Being Week 12 payroll expense, statutory &amp; voluntary liabilities, and net pay disbursed.* | | |

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Taxable Income** = `MAX(0, Gross Pay − Tax-Free Threshold)` → THEN multiply by PAYE `%`!
- **Cross-Cast Proof:** `Total Gross − Total Deductions = Total Net Pay`.
- **Journal Rule:** `Dr Wages Expense` = **GROSS PAY**; `Cr Bank` = **NET PAY**; all deductions = **Current Liabilities**!
:::
