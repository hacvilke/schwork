:::info Essential Understanding
Every single deduction shrinking an employee’s paycheck from **Gross Pay** down to **Net Pay** belongs to ONE of two legal worlds: **Statutory Deductions *(compulsory by law — PAYE Income Tax, NIS, Education Tax, NHT)*** OR **Voluntary Deductions *(optional by written employee consent — Mortgage, Credit Union, Health Plan, Trade Union Dues)***. Even more important for entrepreneurs: **Employer Matching Statutory Contributions** are an **EXTRA business expense** — NEVER subtracted from the worker’s Net Pay!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | **Statutory (Compulsory) Deductions** vs. **Voluntary (Optional) Deductions**, **Remittance Responsibilities**, and **Employer Matching Statutory Contributions** |
| **Exam Focus** | Classifying 100% of deductions accurately (`Tax/NIS/NHT = Statutory`; `Mortgage/Credit Union/Insurance/Union = Voluntary`), and calculating **Employee Net Pay** vs. **Total True Employer Cost** |
| **Learning Outcome** | Distinguish statutory from voluntary payroll deductions accurately and calculate both employee Net Pay and total employer payroll cost |

## 1. Statutory vs. Voluntary Deductions Compared

| Dimension | **Statutory Deductions *(Compulsory)*** | **Voluntary Deductions *(Optional)*** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Authority** | **Mandatory by National Law** *(Employee CANNOT opt out!)* | **Employee’s Personal Choice** *(Requires **signed written authorization**!)* |
| **Who Receives the Funds?** | **Government Revenue &amp; Social Security Agencies** | **Private Third Parties** *(Credit Unions, Banks, Insurers, Trade Unions, Charities)* |
| **Classic Examples** | • **PAYE Income Tax** *(Pay As You Earn)*<br/>• **NIS** *(National Insurance Scheme / Social Security)*<br/>• **Education Tax** &amp; **NHT** *(National Housing Trust)* | • **Mortgage / Building Society** payments<br/>• **Credit Union** savings or loan repayments<br/>• **Private Health / Life Insurance** &amp; Pension<br/>• **Trade Union Dues** &amp; Charity pledges |
| **Effect on Balance Sheet *(until paid)*** | **Current Liability** *(Statutory Payables)* | **Current Liability** *(Third-Party Payables)* |

### Crucial Distinction for Business Owners: Employer’s Matching Contributions!
- Many statutory schemes (like **NIS** and **NHT**) require **BOTH** the employee **AND** the employer to contribute!
  - **Employee’s Share** *(e.g. `3%` NIS + `2%` NHT)* → Deducted FROM the employee’s Gross Pay (lowers Net Pay!).
  - **Employer’s Matching Share** *(e.g. `3%` NIS + `3%` NHT)* → Paid **OUT OF THE BUSINESS’S OWN POCKET** as an **extra Operating Expense** (`Dr Employer Payroll Taxes Expense / Cr NIS & NHT Payables`) — **NEVER deducted from the employee’s paycheck!**

### 1. Monthly Payslip Summary — C. Morris *(6 marks)*

| Payslip Section | Calculation | $ | $ |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | ---: |
| **Monthly Gross Salary** | Given | | **4,000.00** |
| **Less: Statutory Deductions *(Compulsory)*** | | | |
| PAYE Income Tax | `25% × ($4,000 − $1,600)` | 600.00 | |
| Employee NIS | `3% × $4,000` | 120.00 | |
| Employee NHT | `2% × $4,000` | 80.00 | *(800.00)* |
| **Less: Voluntary Deductions *(Authorized)*** | | | |
| Building Society Mortgage | Signed Mandate | 450.00 | |
| Credit Union Savings | Signed Mandate | 150.00 | |
| Private Health Insurance | Signed Mandate | 80.00 | |
| Trade Union Dues | Signed Mandate | 20.00 | *(700.00)* |
| *Total Deductions (`$800 + $700`)* | | | **(1,500.00)** |
| **NET PAY *(Take-Home via Direct Deposit)*** | `$4,000 − $1,500` | | **$2,500.00** |

### 2. True Employer Cost & Government Remittance *(3 marks)*
- **Employer Matching Contributions:** Employer NIS (`3% × $4,000 = $120`) + Employer NHT (`3% × $4,000 = $120`) = **`$240.00`** *(1 mark)*
- **Total True Cost to Employer:** `$4,000.00 Gross Salary + $240.00 Employer Contributions` = **`$4,240.00`** *(1 mark)*
- **Total Cash Remitted to Government:** Employee Statutory (`$800`) + Employer Matching (`$240`) = **`$1,040.00`** *(1 mark)*

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Statutory *(By Law)*:** `PAYE Income Tax`, `NIS`, `Education Tax`, `NHT`.
- **Voluntary *(Written Consent)*:** `Mortgage`, `Credit Union`, `Health Plan`, `Union Dues`, `Charity`.
- **Employer Matching Contributions:** **Extra Business Expense** — **NEVER** subtracted from employee Net Pay!
:::
