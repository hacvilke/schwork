:::info Essential Understanding
Every operational budget in a business starts with ONE master domino: the **Sales Budget** *(forecasted customer demand × selling price)*! Once you know how many units customers will buy over the next **3 months (Quarter 1)**, the factory builds its **Production Budget** — adding a safety cushion of **Desired Closing Stock** (usually a `%` of *next month’s* sales!) and subtracting **Opening Stock** already sitting in the warehouse so you NEVER suffer embarrassing stockouts OR choke cash flow with dusty unsold inventory!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | **3-Month Sales Budget** (`Forecast Units × Selling Price`), **3-Month Production Budget** (`Sales Units + Desired Closing Stock − Opening Stock`), and **Budgeted Production Cost Extension** |
| **Exam Focus** | Computing **Desired Closing Stock** as `%` of **NEXT month’s sales** *(using Month 4 sales for Month 3’s closing stock!)*, rolling **Month 1 Closing Stock → Month 2 Opening Stock**, and **NEVER summing monthly stocks in the Q1 Total column** |
| **Learning Outcome** | Prepare linked 3-month Sales and Production Budgets accurately and explain how budgeting improves entrepreneurial control |

## 1. The Linked Sales & Production Budget Formulas

<FormulaCard
  title="Sales Budget → Production Budget Master Equations"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Sales Budget is in UNITS & DOLLARS; Production Budget is primarily in UNITS (then optionally multiplied by Unit Cost)!"
>

$$
\textbf{Sales Budget (\$)} = \text{Budgeted Sales Units} \times \text{Budgeted Selling Price per Unit}
$$

$$
\textbf{Production Budget (Units)} = \text{Budgeted Sales Units} + \text{Desired Closing Inventory} - \text{Opening Inventory}
$$

$$
\textbf{Rollover Rule:}\quad \text{Opening Inventory (Month } t\text{)} = \text{Closing Inventory (Month } t-1\text{)}
$$

</FormulaCard>

### Why Do Businesses Prepare Budgets? *(Classic Paper 1 &amp; Paper 2 Question!)*
1. **Forward Planning &amp; Coordination:** Aligns the Sales team, Factory floor, Raw Materials purchasing, and Cash Flow before the quarter even begins!
2. **Preventing Stockouts &amp; Over-Production:** Keeps an exact safety buffer (e.g., `20%` of next month’s sales) so sudden orders are fulfilled without tying up excess cash in unsold stock.
3. **Performance Control *(Variance Analysis)*:** Lets owners compare **Actual Sales / Production** against **Budgeted Targets** every month to spot waste or booming products early!

### 1. Caribbean Eco-Bottles Ltd — 3-Month Sales Budget (Q1: Jan–Mar 2026) *(4 marks)*

| Sales Budget | Jan | Feb | Mar | **Q1 Total** |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Budgeted Sales *(Units)* | 400 | 500 | 600 | **1,500** |
| Budgeted Selling Price *(per unit)* | `$25` | `$25` | `$25` | `$25` |
| **Budgeted Sales Revenue ($)** | **$10,000** | **$12,500** | **$15,000** | **$37,500** |

---

### 2. Caribbean Eco-Bottles Ltd — 3-Month Production Budget (Q1: Jan–Mar 2026) *(7 marks)*

| Production Budget *(Units)* | Jan | Feb | Mar | **Q1 Total** |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Budgeted Sales Units | 400 | 500 | 600 | **1,500** |
| **Add:** Desired Closing Inventory *(`20%` of next mo.)* | 100 | 120 | 140 | **140** *(Mar 31!)* |
| *Total Units Required* | *500* | *620* | *740* | *1,640* |
| **Less:** Opening Inventory *(prior mo. closing)* | (80) | (100) | (120) | **(80)** *(Jan 1!)* |
| **Budgeted Production *(Units to Make)*** | **420** | **520** | **620** | **1,560** |

- *Workings:* Jan Close = `20% × 500 = 100`; Feb Close = `20% × 600 = 120`; Mar Close = `20% × 700 (Apr!) = 140`.
- **Cross-Check Proof:** Horizontal Sum (`420 + 520 + 620 = 1,560`) = Vertical Q1 Total (`1,500 + 140 − 80 = 1,560`) ✓!

---

### 3. Budgeted Production Cost Schedule (`$14.00` per Bottle) *(3 marks)*

| Production Cost Extension | Jan | Feb | Mar | **Q1 Total** |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Budgeted Production *(Units)* | 420 | 520 | 620 | **1,560** |
| Unit Production Cost | `$14` | `$14` | `$14` | `$14` |
| **Budgeted Total Production Cost ($)** | **$5,880** | **$7,280** | **$8,680** | **$21,840** |

*(Check: `$5,880 + $7,280 + $8,680 = $21,840` = `1,560 × $14` exact ✓!)*

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Order:** **1. Sales Budget** (`Units × Price`) → **2. Production Budget** (`Sales + Desired Close − Open`).
- **Month 4 Sales** feeds **Month 3’s Desired Closing Stock**!
- **Q1 Total Column:** Uses **Jan 1 Opening Stock** &amp; **Mar 31 Closing Stock** *(NEVER add monthly stocks!)*!
:::
