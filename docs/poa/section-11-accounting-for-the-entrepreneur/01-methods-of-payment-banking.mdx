:::info Essential Understanding
Running a modern startup means moving money safely in and out of your business bank account every single day — paying rent, settling fluctuating utility bills, collecting customer card taps at the counter, and paying staff without keeping dangerous piles of physical cash on site! Choosing the right banking payment method — **Cheques, Standing Orders, Direct Debits, EFTPOS, Direct Deposit, or Wire Transfers** — protects cash flow, cuts bank fees, and strengthens internal control!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | **6 Business Payment Rails** (Cheques, Standing Orders, Direct Debits, EFTPOS, Direct Deposit / Credit Transfer, Online &amp; SWIFT Wire Transfers) |
| **Exam Focus** | Instantly contrasting **Standing Order** *(payer-initiated, FIXED amount!)* vs. **Direct Debit** *(payee-initiated, VARIABLE amount!)*, explaining **EFTPOS merchant fees**, and picking the best method for real business scenarios |
| **Learning Outcome** | Distinguish among paper and electronic payment methods and recommend the safest, most cost-effective banking method for any entrepreneurial payment |

## 1. Six Essential Business Payment Methods Compared

| Payment Method | Initiated By | Fixed or Variable? | Best Entrepreneurial Use | Key Caution / Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cheque** *(Paper)* | Payer *(Drawer)* | Any amount written | Formal supplier bills &amp; dual-signature internal control | Slow clearing (`3–5` days), dishonored/NSF bounce risk, cheque-printing cost |
| **Standing Order** | **Payer** instructs own bank | **FIXED** same sum every period | Monthly shop **rent**, fixed **loan repayments**, fixed **insurance** | Must manually update bank instruction if rent changes! |
| **Direct Debit** | **Payee** pulls via customer mandate | **VARIABLE** amounts each bill | Fluctuating **electricity**, **water**, **phone/internet** bills | Must monitor bank balance so variable spikes never trigger overdraft fees! |
| **EFTPOS** *(Debit/Credit Card Terminal)* | Customer taps/swipes at POS | Exact purchase total | **Retail checkout** (shops, cafés, salons, pharmacies) | Bank charges **merchant processing fee** (typically `1.5%–3.5%` on card sales)! |
| **Direct Deposit *(EFT / ACH)*** | Payer pushes batch electronically | Exact amount each run | **Employee payroll**, local vendor batches, government taxes | Verify bank account numbers carefully — wrong transfers are hard to reverse! |
| **Wire Transfer *(SWIFT)*** | Payer’s bank wires globally | Any international sum | Paying **overseas suppliers** for imported stock/machinery | Higher flat wire fee (`$25–$50`) + foreign exchange spread! |

### Part A: Optimal Payment Method Selection *(6 marks — 1 mark each)*
1. **Standing Order:** Fixed `$1,800` monthly sum initiated automatically by the bakery on the 1st.
2. **Direct Debit:** Electricity fluctuates monthly (`$600–$950`), so the utility pulls the exact billed amount via mandate.
3. **Direct Deposit *(EFT / Bank Credit Transfer)*:** Transfers net pay safely and simultaneously to all 14 staff bank accounts without cash-handling theft risk.
4. **EFTPOS *(Card Terminal)*:** Authorizes debit/credit cards instantly at the counter with zero dishonored-cheque risk.
5. **International Wire Transfer *(SWIFT)*:** Transfers foreign currency securely across borders to the overseas manufacturer.
6. **Crossed Cheque *(Dual-Signature)*:** Provides formal paper authorization requiring both co-owners’ signatures before funds clear.

### Part B: EFTPOS Merchant Fee Calculation & Journal Entry *(4 marks)*
- **Merchant Processing Fee:** `2.5% × $18,000` = **`$450`** *(1 mark)*
- **Net Cash Credited to Bank:** `$18,000 − $450` = **`$17,550`** *(1 mark)*

| General Journal | Dr ($) | Cr ($) |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Bank *(Net EFTPOS Settlement)* | 17,550 | |
| Merchant / Bank Processing Fees *(Expense)* | 450 | |
| &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sales *(FULL Gross Card Sales!)* | | 18,000 |
| *Being March EFTPOS card sales less 2.5% merchant fee.* | | |

*(Notice: ALWAYS credit Sales with the FULL `$18,000` and expense the `$450` bank fee separately — never bury the fee by recording Sales as `$17,550`!)*

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Standing Order** = **Payer-initiated**, **FIXED** amount (`Rent, Loan`).
- **Direct Debit** = **Payee-initiated**, **VARIABLE** amount (`Electricity, Water`).
