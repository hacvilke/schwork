:::info Essential Understanding
More young startups fail from **running out of cash** than from lacking profits! A profitable business with booming credit sales can still bounce rent and payroll cheques if customers pay 30 days late while suppliers and equipment vendors demand cash TODAY! A **6-Month Cash Flow Projection (Cash Budget)** forecasts exact **Cash Inflows** and **Cash Outflows** month by month — rolling `Closing Cash` straight into next month’s `Opening Cash` so you spot a **future cash deficit MONTHS early** and fix it before crisis hits!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | Constructing a **6-Month Cash Flow Projection (`Opening Cash + Cash Inflows − Cash Outflows = Closing Cash`)**, **Credit Sales Timing Lags**, excluding **Non-Cash Items (`Depreciation`!)**, and **Solving Cash Deficits** |
| **Exam Focus** | Rolling **Month 1 Closing Cash → Month 2 Opening Cash** accurately, shifting **Credit Sales** into the **actual collection month**, **EXCLUDING Depreciation**, and recommending smart fixes for negative Closing Cash months |
| **Learning Outcome** | Prepare a complete 6-month Cash Flow Projection incorporating credit lags and capital purchases, interpret deficits/surpluses, and propose practical cash-flow remedies |

## 1. The Cash Flow Rollover Formula & Four Unbreakable Rules

<FormulaCard
  title="Monthly Cash Flow Projection Equations"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Always double-check the 6-Month Total column: Jan 1 Opening + Total 6-Month Net Cash Flow MUST equal Jun 30 Closing Cash!"
>

$$
\text{Net Cash Flow (Month } t\text{)} = \text{Total Cash Inflows}_t - \text{Total Cash Outflows}_t
$$

$$
\text{Closing Cash}_t = \text{Opening Cash}_t + \text{Net Cash Flow}_t \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \text{Opening Cash}_{t+1} = \text{Closing Cash}_t
$$

</FormulaCard>

### Four Golden Cash Flow Rules (Memorize Before Building Any Table!)
1. **EXCLUDE ALL Non-Cash Expenses!** **Depreciation**, Provision for Doubtful Debts, and Bad Debts written off involve **ZERO cash leaving the bank** — NEVER put Depreciation inside a Cash Flow Projection!
2. **INCLUDE Cash Capital Purchases &amp; Loan Receipts!** Buying a `$4,500` commercial freezer for cash IS a real cash outflow (even though it isn’t an Income Statement expense!), and receiving a bank loan IS a real cash inflow!
3. **Follow the ACTUAL Collection / Payment Lag!** If customers pay **1 month after sale**, January’s `$3,000` credit sales appear in **FEBRUARY’s** Cash Inflows *(and June’s credit sales fall in July — OUTSIDE the Jan–Jun Cash Flow!)*!
4. **NEVER Sum Monthly Opening &amp; Closing Cash Balances in the Total Column!** The 6-Month Total Column starts with **Jan 1 Opening Cash (`$4,000`)** and ends with **Jun 30 Closing Cash (`$6,500`)**!

### 1. IslandFresh Smoothies & Snacks — 6-Month Cash Flow Projection (Jan–Jun 2026) *(12 marks)*
*(Notice: Credit Sales shift ONE month to the right — Dec’s `$2,000` lands in Jan; Jun’s `$5,000` lands in July outside this table! Depreciation `$400/mo` is **100% EXCLUDED**!)*

| Details ($) | Jan | Feb | Mar | Apr | May | Jun | **6-Mo Total** |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Opening Cash Balance** | **4,000** | 3,000 | 1,500 | *(2,000)* | 500 | 3,500 | **4,000** |
| **Cash Inflows (Receipts):** | | | | | | | |
| Cash Sales | 6,000 | 7,000 | 8,000 | 9,000 | 10,000 | 10,000 | 50,000 |
| Credit Sales *(1-mo lag)* | 2,000 | 3,000 | 3,000 | 4,000 | 4,000 | 5,000 | 21,000 |
| **Total Cash Inflows** | **8,000** | **10,000** | **11,000** | **13,000** | **14,000** | **15,000** | **71,000** |
| **Cash Outflows (Payments):** | | | | | | | |
| Ingredients / Stock Paid | 4,000 | 6,000 | 4,500 | 5,000 | 5,000 | 6,000 | 30,500 |
| Rent &amp; Utilities | 2,000 | 2,000 | 2,000 | 2,000 | 2,000 | 2,000 | 12,000 |
| Staff Wages | 3,000 | 3,500 | 3,500 | 3,500 | 4,000 | 4,000 | 21,500 |
| New Freezer *(Cash Buy)* | — | — | 4,500 | — | — | — | 4,500 |
| **Total Cash Outflows** | **9,000** | **11,500** | **14,500** | **10,500** | **11,000** | **12,000** | **68,500** |
| **Net Cash Flow (`In − Out`)** | *(1,000)* | *(1,500)* | *(3,500)* | 2,500 | 3,000 | 3,000 | **2,500** |
| **Closing Cash Balance** | **3,000** | **1,500** | **(2,000)** | **500** | **3,500** | **6,500** | **$6,500** |

*(Total Column Proof: Jan 1 Opening `$4,000` + 6-Month Net Cash Flow `+$2,500` = Jun 30 Closing **`$6,500`** exact ✓!)*

### 2. Analysis & Two Remedies *(4 marks)*
- **Why `$400/mo` Depreciation Is Excluded *(1 mark)*:** Depreciation is a **non-cash accounting book adjustment** — zero actual cash leaves the bank account!
- **Problem Month Identified *(1 mark)*:** **March** hits a projected **Cash Deficit of `($2,000)`**, triggered primarily by paying `$4,500` cash upfront for the new commercial freezer while receivables lag by 1 month.
- **Two Practical Remedies *(2 marks — 1 mark each)*:**
  1. **Lease or Defer/Finance the `$4,500` Freezer:** Spread payments over 12–24 months (or push cash purchase to May/June when Closing Cash reaches `$3,500–$6,500`), keeping March cleanly positive!
  2. **Arrange a Pre-Approved `$3,000` Temporary Bank Overdraft** *(or incentivize faster credit collection with a small prompt-payment discount)* to bridge the brief 1-month dip safely!

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Roll-Forward:** `Closing Cash (Month 1) = Opening Cash (Month 2)`.
- **EXCLUDE** Non-Cash items (**Depreciation**!); **INCLUDE** cash **Equipment Buys** &amp; **Loans**!
- **Total Column:** Uses **Period-Start Opening Cash** and **Period-End Closing Cash** *(NEVER sum monthly balances!)*!
:::
