:::info Essential Understanding
**Gross Pay** is an employee’s **total earnings BEFORE a single cent of tax, NIS, or voluntary deduction is removed**! Entrepreneurs compensate staff through **four core pay systems** — **Hourly Wages *(with `1.5×` & `2.0×` Overtime Tiers!)***, **Fixed Salaries**, **Piece-Rate Pay**, and **Basic Salary + Sales Commission**. Mastering how to split hours cleanly across tiers — and reading commission thresholds carefully — guarantees full marks on every payroll calculation!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | **4 Remuneration Methods** (Hourly + Overtime `1.0×` / `1.5×` / `2.0×`, Fixed Salary, Piece-Rate Pay, Base + Sales Commission) and **Gross Pay** |
| **Exam Focus** | **NEVER** multiplying ALL hours by `1.5×` *(split Basic `40` hrs first!)*, counting **GOOD units only** in Piece-Rate pay, and subtracting **sales thresholds** before applying commission `%` |
| **Learning Outcome** | Calculate accurate Gross Earnings for hourly, overtime, salaried, piece-rate, and commissioned employees |

## 1. The Four Employee Earnings Formulas

<FormulaCard
  title="Gross Pay Calculation Formulas (All 4 Pay Structures!)"
  onFormulaSheet={false}
  summary="Always separate Basic Hours, 1.5× Overtime Hours, and 2.0× Overtime Hours BEFORE multiplying!"
>

$$
\textbf{1. Hourly + Overtime:}\quad \text{Gross Pay} = (\text{Basic Hrs} \times R) + (\text{OT}_{1.5} \times 1.5R) + (\text{OT}_{2.0} \times 2.0R)
$$

$$
\textbf{2. Fixed Salary:}\quad \text{Monthly} = \frac{\text{Annual Salary}}{12} \qquad \text{or} \qquad \text{Weekly} = \frac{\text{Annual Salary}}{52}
$$

$$
\textbf{3. Piece-Rate:}\quad \text{Gross Pay} = \text{Good Units Completed} \times \text{Rate per Unit}
$$

$$
\textbf{4. Base + Commission:}\quad \text{Gross Pay} = \text{Basic Pay} + \left(\text{Commission \%} \times \text{Qualifying Sales}\right)
$$

</FormulaCard>

### Wages vs. Salaries *(Quick Paper 1 Contrast)*
- **Wages:** Typically paid **weekly or fortnightly** to factory, retail, or workshop staff based on **actual hours worked** (with overtime eligibility) or **units produced**.
- **Salaries:** Typically stated as a **fixed annual sum** paid **monthly** (or bi-weekly) to managers, accountants, and office staff (generally not paid extra for minor overtime).

### Weekly Gross Pay Schedule — Kingston Auto & Parts Workshop *(12 marks)*

1. **K. Grant *(Hourly + 2 Overtime Tiers — 4 marks)*:**
   - Basic Pay: `40 hrs × $20.00` = **`$800.00`**
   - Saturday OT (`1.5× = $30.00/hr`): `6 hrs × $30.00` = **`$180.00`**
   - Sunday OT (`2.0× = $40.00/hr`): `4 hrs × $40.00` = **`$160.00`**
   - **K. Grant Gross Pay:** `$800 + $180 + $160` = **`$1,140.00`** *(Check hours: `40 + 6 + 4 = 50` hrs ✓)*

2. **S. Campbell *(Fixed Weekly Salary — 2 marks)*:**
   - Annual Salary ÷ 52 weeks: `$41,600 ÷ 52` = **`$800.00`**

3. **D. Morgan *(Piece-Rate Pay — 2.5 marks)*:**
   - Good approved units only: `160 − 10 rejected` = **`150 good covers`**
   - **D. Morgan Gross Pay:** `150 × $6.00` = **`$900.00`**

4. **R. Blake *(Base + Threshold Commission — 2.5 marks)*:**
   - Qualifying sales above `$2,000`: `$10,000 − $2,000` = **`$8,000`**
   - Commission (`5% × $8,000`): **`$400.00`**
   - **R. Blake Gross Pay:** `$500 base + $400 commission` = **`$900.00`**

| Employee | Pay Method | Basic / Base ($) | Overtime / Commission ($) | **Gross Pay ($)** |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| K. Grant | Hourly + OT | 800.00 | 340.00 | **1,140.00** |
| S. Campbell | Fixed Salary | 800.00 | — | **800.00** |
| D. Morgan | Piece-Rate (`150 × $6`) | 900.00 | — | **900.00** |
| R. Blake | Base + 5% Comm. | 500.00 | 400.00 | **900.00** |
| **Total Weekly Gross Payroll** *(1 mark)* | | **$3,000.00** | **$740.00** | **$3,740.00** |

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Gross Pay** = Earnings **BEFORE** deductions (`Basic + OT + Commission + Bonus`).
- **Always Split Hours First:** `Basic (40)` · `1.5× OT` · `2.0× OT`!
- **Read Every Word:** Deduct **rejected units** in Piece-Rate &amp; check **"ALL sales" vs. "ABOVE threshold"** in Commission!
:::
