:::info Essential Understanding
Just as sales need invoices and cash needs receipts, **every single dollar of payroll must be backed by verified source documents**! From the **Time Card** proving an employee actually showed up, to the **Payroll Register** summarizing all staff for the week, to the **Employee Earnings Record** tracking one worker’s Year-to-Date totals and the **Payslip** handed out on payday — these five documents build an ironclad audit trail that stops **payroll fraud** cold!
:::

| Quick Focus | What Matters Most |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Key Concept** | **5 Core Payroll Documents** (Time Card / Clock Card, Employee HR &amp; Tax Authorization Form, Payroll Register, Individual Employee Earnings Record, Payslip / Pay Advice) and **Segregation of Duties** |
| **Exam Focus** | Never confusing **Payroll Register** *(ALL staff, ONE pay period!)* with **Employee Earnings Record** *(ONE worker, FULL year cumulative!)*, and explaining how source documents stop **"buddy punching"** and **"ghost workers"** |
| **Learning Outcome** | Identify the purpose, contents, and internal control role of each payroll source document across the payroll audit trail |

## 1. The Five Core Payroll Documents Explained

| Document / Record | Scope | Exact Purpose &amp; Key Contents |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Time Card *(Clock Card / Biometric Log)*** | 1 hourly employee · 1 pay period | Records exact daily **Clock-In &amp; Clock-Out times**, separating **regular hours** vs. **overtime hours** — **MUST be signed by a supervisor!** |
| **2. Employee Authorization &amp; Tax Forms** | 1 employee · permanent HR file | Holds signed pay-rate contract, Tax Registration Number (`TRN`), `NIS` enrollment, and **signed written authorization for every voluntary deduction** *(union, credit union, mortgage)*! |
| **3. Payroll Register *(Payroll Sheet)*** | **ALL employees** · **ONE pay period** | Multi-column summary listing every worker’s Gross Pay, each Deduction, and Net Pay — **column totals serve as the direct source for the General Journal payroll entry!** |
| **4. Employee Earnings Record** | **ONE employee** · **FULL calendar year** | Individual cumulative ledger tracking **Year-to-Date (YTD)** Gross Pay, Taxes, NIS, and Net Pay — needed to check annual NIS caps and issue **year-end Tax Certificates** *(e.g. P24 / W-2)*! |
| **5. Payslip *(Pay Advice / Wage Slip)*** | 1 employee · 1 pay period | Formal breakdown handed/emailed to the worker on payday showing **Pay Period, Hours, Gross Pay, itemized Statutory &amp; Voluntary Deductions, Net Pay, and YTD totals**! |

## 4. Worked Example

<WorkedExample
  title="Auditing Payroll Source Documents & Internal Controls (SunHarbor Hardware Ltd)"
  marks={10}
  problem={
    <>
      <p>
        <strong>Scenario:</strong> You are reviewing payroll controls at <strong>SunHarbor
        Hardware Ltd</strong> and discover four real issues:
      </p>
      <ol>
        <li>Warehouse clerks punch a paper clock card near an unsupervised back door and drop cards directly onto the Payroll Clerk’s desk without any supervisor review.</li>
        <li>The Payroll Clerk has sole authority to add new employees to the Payroll Register, compute wages, AND release the weekly Direct Deposit bank transfer!</li>
        <li>A store clerk disputes a <strong>$45 weekly deduction</strong> for a Trade Union — payroll cannot locate any signed consent form.</li>
        <li>At year-end, the bookkeeper struggles for 3 days flipping through 52 weekly Payroll Registers trying to figure out one employee’s annual Gross Pay and Tax withheld for their annual Tax Certificate!</li>
      </ol>
      <p>
        For each issue: (a) name the <strong>exact source document or control weakness</strong>
        involved, and (b) recommend the <strong>exact accounting/internal control remedy</strong>! <em>[10 marks]</em>
      </p>
    </>
  }
>

### 1. Unsupervised Paper Time Cards *(2.5 marks)*
- **Weakness:** High risk of **"buddy punching"** and unauthorized overtime padding on **Time Cards** *(1 mark)*.
- **Remedy:** Require the **warehouse supervisor** to verify physical attendance and **sign every Time Card** before submission — or install a **biometric/PIN clock-in system** *(1.5 marks)*.

### 2. Zero Segregation of Duties in Payroll *(2.5 marks)*
- **Weakness:** One person holding hiring, calculation, and payment release creates extreme **"ghost worker" fraud** risk *(1 mark)*.
- **Remedy:** Enforce **Segregation of Duties**: (1) **HR / Owner** exclusively approves new hires &amp; terminations in writing; (2) **Payroll Clerk** computes the **Payroll Register**; (3) **Owner / Finance Director** verifies the Register against active staff and authorizes the bank release *(1.5 marks)*.

### 3. Missing Voluntary Deduction Authorization *(2.5 marks)*
- **Weakness:** Missing signed **Voluntary Deduction Authorization Form** in the employee’s HR file *(1 mark)*.
- **Remedy:** Because Trade Union dues are **voluntary** (not statutory), suspend the deduction immediately until written employee consent is signed, and refund unauthorized amounts *(1.5 marks)*.

### 4. Missing Individual Employee Earnings Records *(2.5 marks)*
- **Weakness:** Relying solely on 52 separate **Payroll Registers** instead of maintaining an **Individual Employee Earnings Record** *(1 mark)*.
- **Remedy:** Maintain a cumulative **Employee Earnings Record** for each worker updated every payday (automatically via payroll software!) so **Year-to-Date (YTD)** Gross Pay, PAYE, NIS, and Net Pay are ready instantaneously for year-end Tax Certificates *(1.5 marks)*.

</WorkedExample>

:::tip Quick Exam Checklist
- **Time Card** → proves hours worked (**supervisor-signed**!).
- **Payroll Register** = **ALL staff, 1 period** (feeds General Journal!).
- **Employee Earnings Record** = **1 worker, FULL year YTD** (feeds annual Tax Certificates!).
- **Segregation of Duties** stops **"ghost workers"** and **"buddy punching"**!
:::
